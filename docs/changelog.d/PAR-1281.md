### Changed — Parky keeps his character under sarcasm, style wishes and tests

A Haiku test of 25 questions (insults over three turns, sarcasm, prompt extraction, a developer
claim, a role swap, politics, a flirt, a discount demand, an injection through a post's title)
found Parky in character throughout, with no injection or role change taking hold. The weak spots:
about 14 of 25 answers closed with the same "which park?" question, also on a park's page;
deflections talked about his limits ("debug mode stays off", "not my job"); sarcasm about a wait
got a narrated lookup instead of a reaction; "answer plainly, no jokes" was half refused; and "tell
me something funny" came out vague.

The system prompt (version 43):

- ends with a question only to offer a next step or to go on, never with a generic "which park?";
- keeps a wish for plainer, joke-free answers for the rest of the chat, as a style and not a role;
- reads sarcasm as a complaint with a grin: check the fact, give the next move, no sarcasm back;
- lets a deflection say nothing about what Parky will not do;
- takes a story from the park's `funFacts` or the ride's profile on their pages;
- reports a post about a dispute or a vote neutrally.

The teasing example no longer ends with "name a park", which the answers copied. The eval has
cases for prompt extraction, a style wish and sarcasm about a wait
([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).
