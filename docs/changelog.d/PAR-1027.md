### Changed — a served wait forecast is floored at 5 minutes, not 10

Every served wait forecast is rounded half-up to 5-minute steps and then, when
positive, raised to a floor. That floor was 10: on 2026-10-24, 22 % of live
anchors were 5–9 minutes, so a walk-on ride that showed 5 on the live board was
forecast as 10. The floor is now 5 — rounding to 5-minute steps stays, and a
positive operating forecast still never reads 0. The rule changes together in
its three copies, which must stay exact mirrors: ml-service `predict.py` (the
CatBoost post-processing of both the display and the crowd quantile), NestJS
`roundServedWait` (PCN and Chronos-2 serving, the deviation check, the forward
archive's paired scoring) and pcn-service `serve_round` (the shadow board).
Since `round_to_nearest_5` already maps anything ≥ 2.5 to at least 5, the floor
no longer moves any value; it stays in place as the stated rule. CatBoost picks
it up on its next prediction run — it is post-processing, not a feature, so no
retraining is needed.
