### Fixed — the Chronos-2 shadow no longer reads heartbeat rows as context

`nf-service` `fetch_readings` was the one truth reader without the heartbeat filter (the TFT
panel, PCN, the forward archive and ml-bench have it). A heartbeat re-stamps a frozen reading
every hour and so reset the context's 3-hour staleness clock: a ride that dropped out of every
feed at 14:00 with 45 minutes read 45 for up to 24 hours instead of going unknown at 17:00, and
stayed among the recent rides. The filter is in, and the per-park parquet cache carries a
`TRUTH_VERSION`, so the slots cached under the old rule are read as cold and refilled by the
background worker, one park at a time (a park answers `warming` until then). Shadow surfaces
`chronos_live` and `chronos_grid`; served values only where `SERVE_CHRONOS_INTRADAY` is on
([Chronos-2 shadow](ml/forward-archive/chronos-shadow.md)). Archive rows before and after the
deploy rest on different contexts.
