### Fixed — a queue's stall and lock settings apply to the queue that runs its jobs

Eight modules registered a queue a second time by name alone. Each registration builds its own Bull
queue and Nest hands the processor the one registered last, so `children-metadata`, `facilities`,
`downtime`, `weather-closure` and `park-day-operation` ran on Bull's defaults: one stall and a 30 s
lock. `sync-attraction-details` (2026-11-22) and the facilities import (2026-11-25, at 84 of 208
parks) failed with `stalledCounter` 2 where three stalls are allowed. Every module now registers
through `bullQueue()`, which hands out the one registration, and a spec refuses anything else.
`pcn-shadow` allows three stalls too: two deploys inside `train-pcn` failed it on 2026-11-27 and
left the PCN model a day old. See [a job a deploy stops](architecture/job-queues.md#a-job-a-deploy-stops).
