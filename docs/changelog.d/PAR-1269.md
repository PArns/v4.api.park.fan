### Fixed — Bridge days are only the days Nager.Date names, and a stale one goes

Disneyland Paris showed 10, 12 and 13 November 2026 as bridge days and 11 November, Armistice Day
on a Wednesday, as holiday and bridge day at once, and Parky told a visitor the 12th was a "jour de
pont". `saveLongWeekendsFromApi` stored every weekday of a Nager.Date long weekend as a "Bridge
Day", the holiday itself among them (1 January and 14 July in France), and nothing removed a day a
later answer no longer listed.

- Only the days in the long weekend's `bridgeDays` are stored, weekends and other years left out
  (`bridgeDaysOf`).
- Each sync replaces one country's set for one year through `replaceHolidaysBySource`, so a day
  Nager no longer names is deleted. A year with none listed keeps what it has.
- `getLongWeekends` answers `null` on a failed fetch instead of an empty list, and the sync skips
  that year rather than read it as "no bridge days".

Schedule rows carry `isBridgeDay` from their last sync and pick the correction up with the next
one. Whether every one of the November rows at Disneyland Paris came from this path still needs a
look at the stored holidays (PAR-1269).
