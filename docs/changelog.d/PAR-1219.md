### Added — a ride in maintenance reads like an outage: since when, and when it usually runs again

Rides the feed reports as `REFURBISHMENT` („Wartung") now carry the live `outage` line with
`signal: "maintenance"`: since when, plus a recovery estimate read off a curve built from
maintenance stops only. The nightly reconstruction builds these runs with the DOWN rules and
stores them as their own signal, so published fault counts and rates are unchanged. Over 30 days
Europa-Park had 289 such stops, at a median of 15 operating minutes.
See [the maintenance signal](analytics/ride-downtime/maintenance-signal.md).
