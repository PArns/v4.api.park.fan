### Added — Parky knows food, shows, last year, a country's coasters and draws four new cards

Parky, the chat assistant, closes the gaps visitors ran into
([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)):

- `find_restaurants` lists a park's restaurants and food stands, filtered by name or cuisine
  ("eis" finds "Eiscafé"); Phantasialand's 46 were invisible to it before. Status only where the
  restaurant has a reading of its own.
- `compare_rides` takes a country instead of a park (one query) and `type: "coaster"`, read off the
  ride profile's glossary types, so "the fastest coaster in Germany" comes from data, with the rides
  of unknown type counted apart. `show_card` kind `rides` takes the same filter and lists the rides
  that are down after the waits, each row with its status.
- `get_shows` lists a park's shows with their times on a day; `get_park_history` reads the calendar
  for the same date a year back and the nearest day of the same weekday.
- `get_ride` (and the admin MCP's `get_attraction`) carries the rope-drop recommendation, today's
  best visit times, the land, the paid fast pass and whether the ride is a headliner.
- `show_card` draws a crowd calendar (7 to 42 days), one day's tiles (live or forecast), a park's
  shows and its restaurants; the ride card gains its badges. Every time on a card carries the
  park's own offset, never `Z`.
- Park and ride links reach the model in the chat's language (`/nl/parks/…`), and the results of
  `show_card` and `open_page` tell the model its earlier text is already on screen, so an answer is
  no longer shown twice.
- `get_best_days` no longer ranks an `unknown` day as the quietest, and names its list for what it
  is: the ten quietest rated open days.
- The prompt (version 15) keeps the voice without copying or translating its examples, addresses
  one visitor in the singular, never calls a high-level day quiet, and adds a take of Parky's own
  after a list or a card.

Follow-up from a chat at Epic Universe, where Parky twice answered "the card above shows the
rides" without naming one, recommended from the eight longest waits and missed a 10-minute dark
ride, and ran `search_parks` five times:

- `show_card` kind `rides` hands the model its rows (name, wait, status, profile types) and kind
  `parks` its rows, so the sentence after the card names a pick; the prompt (version 24) asks for
  one or two picks with a reason after such a card.
- `type` on `show_card` kind `rides` and `compare_rides` also takes `dark_ride`, `water` and
  `family`, read off the glossary's attraction terms; rides without a profiled type are counted in
  `typeUnknown`, on the card too. A preference (immersion, wet, with kids, thrills) is answered
  from every open ride of that type, not the longest-wait list.
- The chat block after the cache breakpoint lists the parks and rides earlier answers looked up
  (name and id, newest first, at most eight), read off the stored tool inputs, so Parky reuses the
  id instead of searching again.
