### Added — level-driver analysis and the `driver_level` model in ml-bench

`python -m mlbench drivers` measures how much of a day's level is explainable in
advance: per ride-day and lead, `log(true daily P90 / naive level)` against
weekday, holidays (own and neighbour regions, OR semantics), bridge days,
published opening hours (only where the schedule row was written before the
origin), ticketed events, season start/end and — separately, as ORACLE —
weather, with walk-forward validation by origin month. **In this offline
analysis** the drivers explain, out of sample, 12–16 % of a ride-day's and
19–28 % of a park-day's squared log deviation (level MAE −4…−8 % per ride,
−7…−12 % per park, d1–d60) and lift the within-month day ranking from 0.27 to
0.34 at d1 over 385 park-months. The gradient-boosted version is a harness
plug-in (`driver_level`), scored like every other model — on a reduced feature
set, so the harness result below is a lower bound on the figures above and the
two sets of numbers must not be mixed.

**Scored through the harness** over all 231 origins of the 20261009 export, the
result splits by what is being measured. **The day *ranking* improves and it
holds over the whole backtest period**: `lvl_driver_level` has the highest value
of any daily level source on the day comparison (D7 +0.068 at d8–30, +0.094 at
d31–90) and the crowd bucket (D6 +0.032 at d30, +0.024 at d90), it is the only
level source that beats its reference on the within-park-month day ranking (UC4
+0.065 at d1 … +0.109 at d30, +0.086 at d60, on 349–607 park-months), and it
carries the planner's optimiser regret to d30 with the largest margin of any
model (−1.17…−1.52 against H5's −0.76…−1.14, on the same d30 horizon). **The
15-min slot MAE does not**: the win measured on the 8-week late-summer/autumn
window (usable horizon d10 all rides, d60 busy) **reverses over the full
period**, where `driver_level` has no usable horizon on either segment and is
worse than H5 at every lead. By season it beats H5 only in autumn
(−0.39…−0.52) and loses in spring, summer and winter (+0.29…+2.37); that
deficit is seasonal, not a cold start — the cold start is confined to winter
(1.9 % of slots) and part of spring, and excluding both still leaves +0.14…+0.24
against H5.

It is therefore **shadow-only, scoped to four ranking surfaces** (D6 crowd
bucket d14–d90, D7 day comparison d8–d90, D4 optimiser regret d1–d30, UC4
d1–d60) and serving nothing. Excluded for measured reasons: the 15-min slot
curve; `bestVisitTimes` (D3), which it **regresses** against both climatology
and H5 under both bootstrap units in both passes; the planner's `dayPeak`
ordering, where it wins at no lead and is beaten by both TFT×H5 and H5; and
everything beyond d90, where **both** ranking metrics turn at the same lead:
UC4 becomes a measured loss at d120 (−0.029 [−0.052, −0.007]) and D6 a measured
loss at d120 as well (−0.034 [−0.050, −0.017]), each significant under both
bootstrap units. See docs/ml/level-drivers.md.
