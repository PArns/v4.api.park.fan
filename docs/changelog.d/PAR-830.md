### Added — level-driver analysis and the `driver_level` model in ml-bench

`python -m mlbench drivers` measures how much of a day's level is explainable in
advance: per ride-day and lead, `log(true daily P90 / naive level)` against
weekday, holidays (own and neighbour regions, OR semantics), bridge days,
published opening hours (only where the schedule row was written before the
origin), ticketed events, season start/end and — separately, as ORACLE —
weather, with walk-forward validation by origin month. Out of sample the drivers
explain 12–16 % of a ride-day's and 19–28 % of a park-day's squared log
deviation (level MAE −4…−8 % per ride, −7…−12 % per park, d1–d60) and lift the
within-month day ranking from 0.27 to 0.34 at d1. The gradient-boosted version
is a harness plug-in (`driver_level`), scored like every other model.

Scored over all 231 origins of the 20261009 export, the result splits by what is
being measured. **The day *ranking* improves and it holds over the whole
backtest period**: `lvl_driver_level` beats every other daily level source on the
day comparison (D7 +0.068 at d8–30, +0.094 at d31–90) and the crowd bucket (D6
+0.032 at d30, +0.024 at d90), and it carries the planner's dayPeak ordering to
d90 and the optimiser to d30 — further than any other model. **The 15-min slot
MAE does not**: the win measured on the 8-week late-summer/autumn window (usable
horizon d10 all rides, d60 busy) **reverses over the full period**, where
`driver_level` has no usable horizon on either segment and is worse than H5 at
every lead. By season it beats H5 only in autumn (−0.39…−0.47) and loses in
spring, summer and winter (+0.32…+2.37).

It is therefore **shadow-only, scoped to the ranking surfaces**: it also
regresses `bestVisitTimes` (D3) against both climatology and H5, under both
bootstrap units and in both passes. UC4 is **not measurable** yet in either pass
(the unit is the park-month: 3 origin days windowed, 6–9 full period, against a
30-origin-day gate) — the empty usable horizon there means untested, not
unbeaten. See docs/ml/level-drivers.md.
