### Changed — The children sync runs once a day instead of twice

`fetch-all-children` was queued by two triggers: step 11 of `sync-all-parks` (03:00, finishing around 03:29) and its own cron at 04:00. The second run rewrote the same 6,682 active attraction rows in 204 parks about 31 minutes after the first. The 04:00 cron is gone and its repeatable entry is removed from Redis on the next boot. The chain from `sync-all-parks` stays, so a new park still gets its children on the day it appears. If the park sync does not start, or throws before step 11, no children sync is queued that day.
