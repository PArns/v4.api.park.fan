### Changed — the season level scales TFT by what a ride measured against it, per day off, eve and working day

The season level ([season level](ml/season-level.md), PAR-1343) served every working day of a
season one number and every day off another: the median of all the season's measured days of that
type. The level was flat within a type for weeks, and it lagged a season that builds, so
Wintertraum's November days would have outvoted its Advent days into late December. Fridays queue
above Monday to Thursday in an evening-driven season and were served the working days' median.
A school break in a neighbouring region counted for nothing, while TFT and CatBoost read it and
the level-driver analysis measures a neighbour's public holiday at +17 %
([level drivers](ml/level-drivers.md)).

`seasonDayTypes` now knows three types. A day off is the country's weekend, a public holiday,
school-break day or bridge day of the park's own region, or a public holiday or school-break day
in one of its `influencingRegions` (`influencingHolidayTest`: an entry without a region takes
every region of its country, an entry with one takes its region and its country's nationwide
rows, so Limburg has Koningsdag). An eve is a working day before a day off: Friday before a
Saturday–Sunday weekend, Thursday before a Friday–Saturday one, the day before a holiday or a
break. Everything else up to the park's own school-break coverage is a working day; a weekday past
it, and a working day before such a weekday, stays unknown. Each type needs three measured days of
its own and never borrows from another.

A reference reads the type's latest six measured days, not all of them. With
`SEASON_LEVEL_MODE=ratio`, the default, a TFT row is served TFT's own number × the median of
measured P90 ÷ TFT's freshest forecast made before the day, over the type's latest six days with a
forecast of at least 5 minutes (`tft_forecasts`, one primary-key lookup per measured day, in the
same query). TFT's day-to-day structure stays, and the season lifts it. CatBoost's rows (days 61+,
rides TFT has no row for) and a TFT row whose ratio has fewer than three days get the measured
level. `SEASON_LEVEL_MODE=level` serves the level on every row; the switch is read per call, and
`docker-compose.production.yml` passes it with `ratio` as the default. Rollback:
`SEASON_LEVEL_MODE=level`.

The holiday rows now come from the park's country and every influencing country, from three days
before the season to four past it, because an eve reads the day after. The medians moved out of
SQL into `seasonReferences`, where they are unit-tested. The Redis key moves to
`ml:season-level:v3:{parkId}:{date}`, since the cached shape changed (`dayType`, `eves`, the
ratio). Neither mode is measured against TFT yet; the season level page has the lead-snapshot
query for it (PAR-1345).
