### Fixed — trip regions: the best dates rank on what was measured, not on a forecast that read 40 % low

`/when`, the best-time pages, `/best-days`, the calendar's best park of a day
and the plan's day costs compared months from two bases: the forecast up to
mid-May, last year's measured days after it. Past day 60 the forecast is
CatBoost's long-range level, and on production's answers of 2026-11-14 it
read 42 % below the same dates of 2026 for Orlando's seven headliner parks
(38.6 against 66.3 minutes, February to April) and almost flat, its spread
12–23 % of the measured one. So the forecast months won on their basis:
Orlando's top windows were 16–25 February, 6–15 April and 2–11 March, where
2026 measured February and March as the busiest months after December, and
the ranking agreed with the measured one at a Spearman of 0.13
([long-range forecasting](ml/long-range-forecasting.md)).

Every trip day is now on the measured scale
([the days](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-days-get-v1trip-regionsorlandocalendarfromto)):
within 60 days the forecast times its lead factor (1.10 at a day to 1.29 at
31–60 days, the realised day P90 over the predicted one), past them the same
weekday a year back as measured; the long-range forecast stands in only where
nothing was measured a year back. Each day carries both values beside its
figure, `forecastWait` and `lastYearWait`. The tie-break that put forecast
windows first among equal ones is gone. Ranked past day 60 on a capture of
those answers, the served calendar and the measured days now agree at 1.0.

A window that touches the storm peak on any day now adds 10 minutes per
visit to its rank ([when to go](frontend/trip-regions-when.md#how-the-storm-peak-ranks)):
on measured days late August is Orlando's quietest stretch, 34 minutes a
park day against 42–43 outside the peak, and would have led. Ranked on 2026,
no window touching the peak stays in a best-time page's top three from 7
minutes on in Orlando and from 8 for Japan's typhoon peak.

Orlando, ten days in 2027, the seven headliners: 12–21 April (43 minutes),
26 October – 4 November (42), 8–17 May (45), in place of February, April and
March at 36–37. Japan: 31 May – 9 June, 18–27 June and 8–17 July; the two
September windows in the typhoon peak left the top five. The trip caches
moved to `trip-regions:calendar:v2`, `trip-regions:plan:v3` and
`trip-regions:when:v3`, and the warmup keeps last year's months −12 … −1.
The park calendars still show the long-range level (PAR-1320).
