### Fixed — an attraction that left the ThemeParks.wiki feed is retired instead of standing as CLOSED forever

The children sync had no path for an entity that disappears from its park's
`/children`: both retirement paths only handle an id that changed its
`entityType`. On 2026-10-02 that left 515 active rows in 83 parks, and in 43
name groups the dead row won the slug tie-break and was served as CLOSED in
place of its live twin (21 rides at Walibi Belgium, 8 mazes at Six Flags Fiesta
Texas).

`retireAbsentAttractions` now retires a row whose id has not been listed for 60
days while its park kept syncing, with the gates of the reclassification path
(no Queue-Times id, no foreign mapping) plus no real reading in 7 days. The
reason, `ABSENT_UPSTREAM_REASON`, joins `RECLASSIFIED_UPSTREAM_REASONS`, so
`retiredKind` reads `reclassified` rather than `closed` and the sync lifts the
retirement by itself if the id comes back.
