### Added — US school breaks for Pennsylvania, Ohio, Missouri, Virginia, Tennessee and North Carolina

Eighteen more US parks get school days off, from one school district per
state, read from the district's own calendar
([docs](architecture/holiday-sources-outside-europe.md#united-states-par-1071)):

- **PA — Derry Township School District** (Hersheypark): summer 2025 through
  2026-27, stopping on 2027-06-30.
- **OH — Sandusky City Schools** (Cedar Point): summer 2025 through 2026-27,
  stopping on 2027-06-30. Kings Island gets the same calendar.
- **MO — North Kansas City Schools** (Worlds of Fun, Oceans of Fun): summer
  2025 through 2027-28, stopping on 2028-06-30.
- **VA — Williamsburg-James City County** (Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Water
  Country USA): summer 2025 through 2027-28, stopping on 2028-06-30.
- **TN — Sevier County Schools** (Dollywood): 2025-26 and 2026-27, stopping on
  2027-06-30. Summer 2025 starts on 08-01 because Sevier County no longer
  shows its 2024-25 last day.
- **NC — Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools** (Carowinds): summer 2025 through
  2027-28, stopping on 2028-06-30.

No district has published the year after its last calendar, so later days
stay unknown. Days off for only some grades are not listed. The 21 US parks
in states without a district still read as unknown.
