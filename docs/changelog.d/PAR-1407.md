### Changed — `plan_add_rides` takes rides by name, and Parky says there is no store app

**Rides by name.** `plan_add_rides` took ride ids only, and the chat's history
keeps no tool results. So "Ja, alle Tipps einplanen" after a day plan cost one
`find_rides` per ride the plan had named: five in one answer in the chats of
16 to 22 November, and the same again in a test of prompt 62 with a Haiku agent.
Its `rides` now take each ride as an id or as its name at the park, resolved as
`get_ride` resolves one (`planRideIds`, `rideIdOf`). Names it cannot place come
back in `notAdded` and the rest still go in:
- a name that matches no ride comes back with reason `not_found`
- a name that matches several comes back with reason `ambiguous`, with the
  candidates and their ids

A ride given twice goes in once. `lookedUpIds` reads the ids among `rides`.
Prompt 64 sends rides an earlier answer named straight in by name
([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).

**No app.** Asked how he shows up in the Play Store, Parky answered "No tengo
datos sobre una ficha en Play Store". Prompt 64 says park.fan has no app in the
App Store or Google Play, and how to put it on the home screen from the browser.
Two eval cases cover the two changes.
