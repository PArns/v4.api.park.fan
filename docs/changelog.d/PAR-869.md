### Documented — TiDE and Chronos-2 measured against each other, not just against the incumbents

PAR-829 found TiDE serve-eligible on live and intraday leads and PAR-828 found
Chronos-2 ahead on the planner's 15-min curve, but in two separate runs, so the
two candidates had never been compared with each other. They cannot be: the
harness pairs models **inside** a run — `run --pair-with` writes the paired sums
per origin and `report --vs` turns those columns into a margin — so two runs
share no paired rows and a difference of two separately measured MAEs has no
confidence interval. `ml-bench/results/20261012-tide-vs-chronos` is one run with
both families on the same 47 origins across all seven weekdays (30-park subset,
`--shard 0/5`), Chronos-2 inferred on the GPU and TiDE replayed from PAR-829's
precomputed cache, so the TiDE forecasts scored are the ones its eligibility
rests on.

The duel is one-sided: of 224 paired cells (question × lead × segment × variant
pair × report pass) **212 go to Chronos-2** with the park-cluster CI excluding 0,
8 are ties and 4 are not measurable — **none** to TiDE. Margins run from 0.41 min
(`nf_tide_x_h5` vs `chronos2`, planner d7, all rides) to 2.99 min (`nf_tide` vs
`chronos2`, live m015, busy rides). That does not overturn PAR-829: TiDE does
beat the incumbents on intraday h2-4, Chronos-2 just beats them by more, and at
h8+ TiDE is worse than the H5 profile. Winning the duel is also not the same as
being worth serving — at live m015 `persistence` beats both candidates, and on
the planner's d3–d5 and d7 no Chronos variant separates from `h5` for all rides.

Three scripts under `ml-bench/` produce every published number, including
`verify_cells_from_parts.py`, which rebuilds the point estimate and the three
counts behind a published cell straight from the per-origin sums without the
report code — 19 cells re-derived, largest deviation 5.1e-15. The run also has
no model-vs-model margin for D3, D4 or UC4, because the ride-day sums are
written against the naive candidates alone; that is recorded as a follow-up
rather than papered over. See docs/ml/ml-bench/results-neuralforecast.md.
