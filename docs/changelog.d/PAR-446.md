### Added — the calendar serves climate normals past the 16-day forecast, marked as such

A day beyond the weather forecast used to come back without weather. `weather` on a calendar day now carries the park's climate normal for that calendar day: the mean of 2015 to 2024 from Open-Meteo's ERA5 archive, averaged over `±3` days in each year. Every weather block has a new `basis` field, `forecast` or `climate_normal`, and a normal also names its `normalPeriod`. It never replaces a forecast and does not enter the day's recommendation. `/plan/day` keeps its rule of no weather past the forecast: its context drops a normal.

The wait-time model cannot tell a normal from a forecast, so normals are kept out of its reach: they are fetched in the background (at most 20 parks a day, on their own rate-limit keys), not stored in `weather_data`, not returned by the forecast readers `MLService` uses, and cached under their own key. `weather.service.climate-normals.spec.ts` fails if one of those walls moves.
