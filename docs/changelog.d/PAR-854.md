### Fixed — accuracy figures measure the model the reader was shown

`/plan/day` `leadTimeMae` was the error of CatBoost's raw daily answer, while
the calendar and the planner serve TFT for headliners over days 1–60. The
nightly `generate-daily` run now snapshots the served daily level
(`getServingDailyPredictions`) into `prediction_lead_snapshots`, with a new
`source` column (`tft` | `catboost`), and the published mean counts only those
rows. If the served read fails, nothing is snapshotted rather than the raw
answer. The target day of a TFT row is now read as the park-local day it names
(`predictionLocalDate`); `new Date()` on the offset-less string put parks twelve
or more hours from UTC a day off.

The attraction accuracy badge scored `prediction_accuracy.predicted_wait_time`,
which is raw CatBoost, while the ride page serves PCN plus the persistence blend
(`SERVE_PCN_INTRADAY=true` in production). `prediction_accuracy` gains
`served_wait_time`, `served_source` (`pcn` | `catboost`) and
`served_absolute_error`: the hourly run records what `getParkPredictions` would
serve at that moment, and the comparison job scores it beside the raw value.
The served columns are written only while the slot is at least one hour away
(`SERVED_ACCURACY_MIN_LEAD_MS`), because the scored row used to be written at a
median lead of −0.6 minutes (measured over 261 419 production rows), where the
blend serves the reading of the moment and would be graded against itself. The
admin boards keep scoring raw CatBoost.

The ride day curve (`/stats/day`) plots raw CatBoost from
`wait_time_predictions`, so its `forecastError` band now reads the new
`attraction_accuracy_stats.raw_mae` (CatBoost, NULL below 10 compared slots)
rather than the served `mae`.

Transition: rows from before this change carry no source and count for nothing,
so the badge reads `insufficient_data` until a ride has 10 compared served slots
(a few hours into its first operating day), stats rows for rides with no
completed comparison in the window are deleted, and each `leadTimeMae` bucket
is absent until it has 100 scored served rows — about lead + 2 nights (d1 ≈ 3
days, d60 ≈ 62). Redis keys moved to `ml:lead-mae:v2:*` and
`accuracy:badge:v2:*`. See `docs/ml/reader-facing-accuracy.md`.
