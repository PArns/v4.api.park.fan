### Added — sights from OpenStreetMap: figure bins, statues, fountains, historic objects

`/facilities` serves a third type, `sight`
([facilities](frontend/facilities.md#the-contract-a-client-reads)): a named figure bin
(`amenity=waste_basket`, since an ordinary bin has no name), fountain, `tourism=artwork`,
`tourism=viewpoint` or `historic=*` inside the park's outline, and never an element with an
`attraction=*` tag, which is a ride. At Efteling that is the eleven Holle Bolle Gijs ("Opa Gijs",
"Kapitein Gijs" …), four statues, eight fountains and the Mausoleum; at Europa-Park the Brüder
Grimm, Froschkönig and Wilhelm Tell, two fountains, Schloss Balthasar and the Raumstation Mir. A
sight always has a name and none of the toilet fields; an element that is also a toilet or drinking
water stays that type (the Drinkebroer). Two sights within 15 m are one only when they carry the
same name, so a pair of statues stays two. `tourism=attraction` without a ride tag (the
Sprookjesbos fairy tales) is left out on purpose: in many parks that is also how rides are mapped.
A survey of the parks' outlines on 2026-11-28 chose the tags.

The Overpass query asks for the new tags with a `name` filter, so the unnamed bins and fountains
(112 of the 123 bins around Efteling) never leave Overpass. The admin's park list counts `sights`
beside toilets and drinking water, and the bulk approval's three visible points count every type.
Parky's `find_nearby` takes `sight` as a kind and the `navigate` card `place: "sight"`; the credit
names the layers asked for ("Sights: © OpenStreetMap contributors"), prompt version 70.

- A re-import no longer takes an approved park offline. New points, and points whose shown fields
  changed, come in as `awaitingReview` (new column on `park_facilities_osm`, default `false`, so
  every existing row stays served) and wait for the next approval; the rest of the park stays
  served. The park still goes back to "Zu prüfen", and `counts.awaitingReview` says how many
  points wait. `approvedAt` is now the last approval and survives that step; withdrawing clears
  it. Before, the 107 approved parks would have gone dark on the first re-import that found a
  sight.
- Web, app and admin show the new type in PAR-1460, PAR-1461 and PAR-1462; until then they ignore
  it, as the contract asks of an unknown `type`, and the admin lists a sight without its type word.
