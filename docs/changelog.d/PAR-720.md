### Fixed — the hourly heartbeat no longer takes the place of a feed that is still reporting

`writeHourlyHeartbeats` runs right after the wait-times sync in the same
five-minute tick and carries a ride's last row forward once that row is over 60
minutes old. The sync's own 60-minute rule in `isSignificantChange` does the
same thing as an observation. Whenever the 60-minute mark fell between the two,
the heartbeat won, the next sync saw a fresh row and wrote nothing, and a quiet
ride the feed kept reporting carried heartbeat rows instead of readings for
hours. Measured against production on 2026-10-01: the feed wrote 19 616
unchanged 60-minute rows and the heartbeat 12 564; of the heartbeats 3–6 h after
the ride's last observation, 90.6 % fell while the park's feed was running and
76.4 % were followed by an observation with the same wait.

A ride a feed delivered in the last 10 minutes (its `attraction:last-seen` key)
now gets no heartbeat; the next sync writes its row. Ten minutes rather than an
hour because wartezeiten-app is not fetched every tick: only 60 % of its own
60-minute rows land within 70 minutes, and a longer wait would push its rides
past `MAX_SEGMENT_MINUTES` in the downtime reconstruction. A heartbeat therefore
means "no feed has delivered this ride lately".

The hourly aggregator is unchanged: excluding heartbeats there (measured: −35.4 %
of the published hourly buckets) is re-measured a week after this deploy in
PAR-755.
