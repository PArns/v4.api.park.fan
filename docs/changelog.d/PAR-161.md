### Fixed — a Queue-Times ride that is already a show no longer becomes a second, starving catalogue entry

Queue-Times sells a ticket for anything you queue for, so it reports meet &
greets and theatre shows as rides. ThemeParks.wiki reports the same entity as a
`SHOW`, and we sync that into `shows`. When a park fell back to the Queue-Times
catalogue sync, the ride row was created beside the show — one entity in the
catalogue twice, and the ride half never received live data:
`ConflictResolverService.mergeEntities` keys on the name without the
`entityType`, folds the Queue-Times `ATTRACTION` into the wiki `SHOW`, and the
`qt-ride-…` id never reaches the wait-times lookup. What the visitor saw was
`reconcileMissingAttractions` writing `system-reconciliation` CLOSED forever,
on a ride the feed was reporting in every poll.

`ChildrenMetadataProcessor.syncQtAttraction` now checks this park's live shows
before creating a row, using the same `normalizeName` that decides whether two
sources mean the same ride. Existing rows keep being updated, mapping included
— dropping one that is already fed is the failure PAR-311 fixed.

21 such rows across 6 parks were retired in production on 2026-09-20; all 21
had a `shows` row that predated them, the newest by eight months.
