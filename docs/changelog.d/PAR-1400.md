### Added — A key can read the typical waits without the whole history

`typicalWaits` and `isHeadliner` in the park and ride detail came only with `stats.history`, which
also opens `/stats`, `/stats/hourly`, `/stats/day`, day curves, downtime and rope drop. A day
planner that needs the typical waits for a future day had to be given all of it. The new
permission `stats.typical-waits` opens just those two fields and no route. `stats.history` implies
it (`IMPLIED_PERMISSIONS`, added when a key is read), so a key with the history loses nothing
([API keys](frontend/api-keys.md)).
