### Fixed — the season level no longer serves a weekday what the opening weekend measured

The season level ([season level](ml/season-level.md), PAR-1343) fell back to the median of all of
a season's measured days while neither day type had three. On 2026-11-17 Phantasialand's
Wintertraum had three: Saturday, Sunday and Monday. Their median was a weekend's, and the calendar
served it on every day of the season to 24 January: "high", a headliner peak of 45 minutes
(`exactWait` 46.1) on every Tuesday, after the Monday had measured 25. Parky's day tip read the
same level and advised against every day.

`applySeasonLevel` now reads only the reference of the day's own type, and
`loadSeasonLevelInputs` no longer computes the all-days median. A day type without three measured
days keeps the model's number: for a season that starts on a Saturday, weekdays get the season
level from the first Thursday and weekends from the second Sunday. The Redis key moves to
`ml:season-level:v2:{parkId}`, so the six-hour cache of the old references is not read after the
deploy.
