### Fixed — the season level reads days off and working days apart, holidays included

The season level ([season level](ml/season-level.md), PAR-1343) fell back to the median of all of
a season's measured days while neither day type had three. On 2026-11-17 Phantasialand's
Wintertraum had three: Saturday, Sunday and Monday. Their median was a weekend's, and the calendar
served it on every day of the season to 24 January: "high", a headliner peak of 45 minutes
(`exactWait` 46.1) on every Tuesday, after the Monday had measured 25. Parky's day tip read the
same level and advised against every day.

`applySeasonLevel` now reads only the reference of the day's own type, and the two types are a
day off and a working day (`seasonDaysOff`): the country's weekend (`getWeekendDaysForCountry`)
and every public holiday, school-break day and bridge day the calendar marks (`dayHolidayFlags`)
are days off. The weekend-only split served a Tuesday of the Christmas break the level of a
Tuesday in November. A day type without three measured days keeps the model's number: for a
season that starts on a Saturday outside the school breaks, working days get the season level
from the first Thursday and days off from the second Sunday.

A weekday past the region's last stored school-break row (`schoolCoverageThrough`, PAR-1049) is
neither type: a break there is unknown, so the day is measured into no reference and served the
model's number. Counted as a working day, one unpublished break week would have lifted every
later working day of the season to break level.

`loadSeasonLevelInputs` takes the park and a holiday source (the country's holiday rows from the
first season day to the last, three days past each end for the bridge-day rule, and the school
coverage), returns `dayTypes` with the references, and no longer computes an all-days median. A
failed holiday read skips the season level rather than serve every holiday a working day's level.
The Redis key moves to `ml:season-level:v2:{parkId}:{date}`: the old inputs are not read after
the deploy, and a new park-local day reads its measurement on that day instead of up to six hours
later.

The UAE's weekend is Saturday and Sunday since 2022; `getWeekendDaysForCountry` and ml-service's
`add_weekend_feature` still read Friday and Saturday there.

`pnpm typecheck` (`tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.eslint.json`) now runs in CI. `pnpm build` compiles
`src/` only, so 23 type errors had collected in `test/` and `scripts/`: the Bull mock's argument-less
`mockResolvedValue()`, `null` waits in the queue-data fixtures, a `meta.parkId` the calendar no
longer sends, and the trip-region dev server's seasons without `pausedAttractionIds`.
