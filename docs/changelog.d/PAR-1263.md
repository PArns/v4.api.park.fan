### Fixed — Parky gives a closed park no crowd, and judges another day only from that day's figures

Asked about Six Flags Fiesta Texas on an evening it was closed, Parky said the crowd was "very low
for the moment" and called the next afternoon a good time to go, with no forecast for that day in
the chat. Asked whether Baron 1898 runs at the end of the year, he answered with the day's
breakdown alone.

- `parkNow` reads `get_park` for every Parky tool that shows a park's live state
  (`get_park_live`, `show_card` kinds `park`, `rides` and live `tiles`). While the park is not
  operating it drops the parts of the crowd reading that describe this moment (`level`,
  `avgWaitMinutes`, `occupancyPercent`, `comparedToTypicalPercent`) and keeps today's average and
  peak so far. A closed park's card shows no level.
- The system prompt (version 29) has Parky look up another day before calling it good, quiet or
  busy or saying whether a park or ride runs then, and say when a closed park opens next. The eval
