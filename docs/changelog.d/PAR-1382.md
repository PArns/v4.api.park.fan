### Added — Parky knows the day tip he was tapped on, and the day's facts

A tap on Parky's day tip in the calendar opened the chat with a question that quoted the tip, and
nothing else: Parky had to look the day up again and could contradict the sentence he had just
written. The chat request now takes `dayTip` (the park's path, the day, the sentence shown) with
its first question. The API resolves the park and reads the day's facts itself
(`ParkyTipService.dayFacts`, the lines `parkyTipInput` builds for the tip: the day and up to three
on each side, counted from the park's today), and "This chat" hands Parky the sentence as his own
words, quoted as data, and those facts. A park or day it cannot read drops the context, never the
question ([assistant API](frontend/assistant.md)).
