### Fixed — Parky's day tip gives rain in mm, his earlier answers stand, and a show is not carried across a season's edge

Five faults from the chats of 15 to 17 November.

**The day tip called millimetres a chance of rain.** `parkyTipInput` wrote
`weather.rainChance` with `% rain` behind it, and the field holds the day's
precipitation in mm despite its name. The tips read "47,5 % Regenchance" for
Phantasialand on 20 November (47.5 mm) and "2,7 % Regenrisiko" for Efteling on
the same day (2.7 mm). The line now reads `precipitationMm` as
`… mm precipitation over the day`, leaves rain out where nothing was measured,
and the tip's prompt says to put it in words, never as a number or a percentage.
`PARKY_TIP_PROMPT_VERSION` 5 is part of the cache key, so every tip is written
again on deploy. The chat reads the same lines for a tapped tip.

**Parky took back figures he had looked up.** The history carries the answers'
text only, so in a later turn he found no source for his own numbers. He
"corrected" the QUICK Pass Ultimate's 80 € for F.L.Y. once Taron's 12 € came in,
and told a visitor he had given a weekend's waits "without looking", after
`get_days`. "This chat" now lists the tools behind each earlier answer
(`earlierLookups`, off the stored tool calls, counted as the history counts its
answers). The prompt (version 61) says those figures stand, that he never says he
gave one without a lookup, and that only a new lookup of the very same fact is
worth a word, as news. Two eval cases cover it.

**`get_park_day` carried a Halloween parade into the winter season.** A projected
show is the show's last same weekday, and on 29 November at Europa-Park, the
second day of Winterzauber, that was a HALLOWinter Sunday: Parky named
"Ed's Halloween Parade". `PlanDayService` now drops a projection when the curated
programme seasons (Halloween, Christmas, winter, summer nights, a special event)
on the day it was seen differ from those on the day asked about. Maintenance,
closure and opening seasons change no programme. A failed season read projects
as before.

**An answer stopped mid-sentence.** `max_tokens` was 2,048 per round, thinking
included, and a Spanish answer about two Disney parks ended at "el espectáculo
del". `ASSISTANT_MAX_TOKENS` is 8,192, the eval uses the same constant, and a
round that still reaches it logs `Answer cut at …`.

**The park-day interview offered a chip that does nothing.** Its idea "a park by
name" became "Ich nenne dir den Park gleich". The step now asks for a real park
by its name, and the reply rule says a chip never only announces an answer to
come. See [the assistant](frontend/assistant.md).
