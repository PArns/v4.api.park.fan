### Added — Chronos-2 runs as a shadow forecast in the forward archive, scored against what users are served

The PAR-828 bench found zero-shot Chronos-2 (`amazon/chronos-2`, Apache-2.0)
ahead of persistence and H5 at 30–120 min and mostly ahead of H5 on d1–d7, but
on 33 origin days that were all Thursdays. The owner's rule needs a live,
leakage-free confirmation before anything changes, so Chronos-2 now runs in
production as a SHADOW only — never served to a user. nf-service gained
`POST /chronos/forecast`, a port of the bench's two winning variants with the
same inputs: `chronos2` (compressed operating-slot context, 8192 slots) at the
intraday origins 10–18, the next four hours; `chronos2_grid` (15-min grid
context, 8192 steps) once a day at the 06:00 origin for d0–d7. Both get the
bench's covariates — minutes since opening / until closing from the published
schedule, time of day, weekday, and the holiday flags from the shared
`holiday_features.py` (vendored byte-identical, a test compares the files) —
and the same post-processing (q50/q80/q95 clipped and ordered, closed slots
dropped). The forward archive stores them as surfaces 6 (`chronos_live`) and 7
(`chronos_grid`), each row recording whether that day's holiday covariates
rested on data (`model_version` `…;hol=full|short|none`). The daily scoring
scores them like the served curves under their own source (`chronos2`,
`chronos2_grid`, never into `all`) and writes paired per-park-day errors
against the served curve, persistence (live) and H5 (grid) to the new
`forecast_archive_paired` table; `GET /v1/admin/forecast-archive` returns a
`chronos` section with own MAE, paired difference and a 95 % park-cluster
bootstrap CI per use case × lead, marked `insufficient_data` until 14 days and
10 parks. Kill switches: `CHRONOS_SHADOW_ENABLED` (API, default on) and
`CHRONOS_ENABLED` (nf-service); a failing or slow nf-service costs a capture run
at most three 45-second timeouts and never the other surfaces. The model takes
~1.8 GB of VRAM, its weights (0.48 GB) and a per-park truth cache live on the
nf-models volume, and the cold start backfills the context in one background
thread. First Chronos scores: the 12:00 UTC scoring the day after deploy.
