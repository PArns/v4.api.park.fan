### Added — CatBoost hold-out scored at served leads; fixed training seeds

The nightly CatBoost run validated on weeks drawn from a wall-clock seed and
dropped features with a wall-clock seed too, so two runs' validation MAEs — the
number the promotion gate compares — were measured on different data. Both now
use `CATBOOST_RANDOM_SEED`; the week split is drawn per ISO week from
`(seed, week)`, so a week keeps its side when the window slides.

The 30-day hold-out scored every row with its real lags, i.e. as a 1-hour
nowcast. It is now also scored as the model would have been asked at 12 h,
24 h, 7 d and 61 d ahead (features rebuilt as known at the origin), and the
per-lead MAE is stored in the model metadata, on `GET /model/info/{version}`
and in the new nullable `ml_models.holdoutMetrics` column. The promotion gate
is unchanged.
At 61 d the weather comes from monthly climatology and the schedule columns
read as unpublished; the shorter leads keep observed weather and the final
schedule, so their figures are a lower bound on the served error.
