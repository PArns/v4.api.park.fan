### Fixed — a ride retired by hand as never a ride of its own no longer reads as closed

`POST /v1/admin/retire-attractions` takes an optional `kind: "closed" | "reclassified"`
per retirement, stored in the new nullable column `attractions.retired_kind`.
`retiredKindOf` prefers it over the reason text, so a show duplicate or a
single-rider line retired by hand now serves `retiredKind: "reclassified"`: off
the park payload's `closedAttractions`, out of the sitemap, and not "closed
permanently" on its own page. Before, only the children sync's own reason
strings read `reclassified`, and every human retirement read `closed` — 29 rows
(21 Queue-Times show duplicates from PAR-161, 8 single-rider lines from
PAR-965). The children sync never writes the column and never lifts a row that
carries one. Rows without a stated kind read exactly as before; the 29 existing
rows are switched after deploy with the same endpoint.
