### Fixed — the Halloween maze rule reads the rubric a park wrote into the name

A scare zone is an open-air area with scare actors and no house, and it carries
the same horror tokens and the same autumn season as the maze beside it. The
rule's three circumstantial marks cannot separate the two, which is how two of
Six Flags Fiesta Texas's scare zones came to be filed as `MAZE`.

Where a feed writes the park's own event rubric into the published name, that
rubric now decides on its own: `… | Scare Zone` and `Scare Zone: …` are refused
whatever the season says, and `… | Scare Maze` and `HAUNTED HOUSE: …` are filed
without the season gate, which is what the gate was guessing at anyway.

The rubric is matched as a delimited segment — the prefix before the first
colon, the suffix after the last pipe — and not anywhere in the name: a loose
search for `haunted house` also claims LEGOLAND Billund's `Ghost – The Haunted
House` and Lotte World's `Haunted House`, two year-round walk-throughs.

Measured against all 7,580 production rows on 2026-10-11, by running the rule
and its previous version over the same export: 2 rows are written that the
season gate had locked out, 8 scare zones are refused the moment
`detect-seasonal` reaches them (0 today, because none of the eight is flagged
yet), and no verdict already on a row is contradicted.

The two Fiesta Texas rows above carry no rubric, so the segment match does not
reach them — and clearing `attraction_kind` on them would hand them straight
back to the rule, whose curated autumn season selects them again. Their verdict
goes somewhere the rule cannot overwrite instead: `attraction_review_marks`
gains a third kind, `not_a_maze`, and the rule refuses every row carrying an
unexpired one. The column is `text`, so this is a value rather than a schema
change. Measured with the rule from this branch over both rows with the field
reset to `NULL`: 2 of 2 selected without the mark, 0 of 2 with it.

`detect-seasonal` and reverse-reconciliation still ignore review marks, and the
difference is what their output is: `is_seasonal` describes the feed, while
`attraction_kind` is a judgement about the thing, and a judgement is the only
one of the two a human can contradict.
