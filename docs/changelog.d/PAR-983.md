### Fixed — `/stats` at different `topN` depths could show different minutes for the same ride

The historical-stats cache was keyed by `topN`, so the default depth and `topN=30` were computed on their own 24-hour cycles. On Thorpe Park the two answers were a day apart and Hyperia read 60 minutes in `park-comparison-widget` and 55 in `ride-waits-widget` on the same blog post. `getParkHistoricalStats` now computes one payload at `MAX_TOP_ATTRACTIONS` (50, the controller's existing ceiling), caches it without `topN` in the key, and slices `topAttractions` to the requested depth on every read. The rank order is unchanged; the top-attractions query now asks for 50 rows instead of `topN`. Existing entries under the old key shape expire within 24 hours and are still matched by `parkHistoricalStatsPattern`.
