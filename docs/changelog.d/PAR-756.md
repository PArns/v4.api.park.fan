### Added — an admin endpoint queues the measured-operation backfill

`backfill-park-day-operation` (PAR-697) had no way to be started: no endpoint,
no script. `POST /v1/admin/backfill-park-day-operation` (owner) now queues one
job for `{ fromDate, toDate, parkId? }`, so the one-time fill of the past runs
in month slices as the runbook in
`docs/architecture/calendar-schedule-and-ml-rules.md` describes.

Also from the review of PAR-697: the gate's comment claimed a park's hours past
midnight count toward the day they started, which is wrong — they land on the
next calendar day, whose block then exceeds 14 hours, so a park operating past
midnight is never reopened (the safe direction; PAR-722). The counts in the
gate and calendar comments are brought up to the final measurement (6,174 /
1,238 / 210). `derived_open`/`derived_close` stay `timestamp`: they are
park-local wall-clock readings, and the comment now says why `timestamptz`
would be wrong.
