### Fixed — No „steht still" under „geöffnet": closure lines start inside their own window and never on free-flow rides

Phantasialand's Die 3 Mausketiere read OPERATING / 0 min / „Beste Zeit: Jetzt" (from its curated `open_with_park` flag) and, on the same card, „Steht seit Freitag, 18:05 Uhr still". Three fixes, all in the closure signal:

- The live closure statement (`CURRENT_CLOSURE_GAP_SQL`) measured `MIN_PARK_MINUTES_LEFT` against **today's** close, so a CLOSED run that began after yesterday's close — or in yesterday's last hour — passed and stood on the page all of the next day. It now belongs to the window it started in, as the nightly statement's `mid_day` always did. A start before the opening that is still standing inside that day's window is an outage of that day, and its line and elapsed minutes start at the opening. Phantasialand's Pferdekarussell read CLOSED from 08:55 in a park opening at 09:00, and the first version of this fix dropped it to a bare „geschlossen". A start after the day's last close is no closure, and a reading the feed leaves OPERATING after the close no longer counts as a run of the next day: outside every window, "today" begins halfway through the night, as it does for `notRunToday`. Measured against production at 13:25 Berlin: 30 live closure lines before, 24 after. Removed: Die 3 Mausketiere (Fri 18:05), Liseberg's Nattsvart på Gasten (01:50, after a 22:00 close) and four rides at Fantawild Adventure Zhengzhou that started in the last hour before a 21:30 close. Pferdekarussell is kept and now starts at 09:00.
- Both closure statements now leave `open_with_park` rides out, as the DOWN reconstruction always has: the flag says their CLOSED readings are not evidence. The recovery curve skips the 21 closures already stored for two of them.
- `AttractionOutageService` no longer asks the closure statement about a ride the page already resolved to OPERATING.
