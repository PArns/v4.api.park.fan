### Fixed — No „steht still" under „geöffnet": closure lines start inside their own window and never on free-flow rides

Phantasialand's Die 3 Mausketiere read OPERATING / 0 min / „Beste Zeit: Jetzt" (from its curated `open_with_park` flag) and, on the same card, „Steht seit Freitag, 18:05 Uhr still". Three fixes, all in the closure signal:

- The live closure statement (`CURRENT_CLOSURE_GAP_SQL`) measured `MIN_PARK_MINUTES_LEFT` against **today's** close, so a CLOSED run that began after yesterday's close — or in yesterday's last hour — passed and stood on the page all of the next day. It is measured against the window the closure started in now, as the nightly statement's `mid_day` always was; a start outside every window is no closure. Against production at 12:50 Berlin this removed 7 of 29 live closure lines in 3 parks, every one of them started after the close, before the opening or in the last hour (Mausketiere, Pferdekarussell, Liseberg's Nattsvart på Gasten, four rides at Fantawild Adventure Zhengzhou), and kept the other 22 unchanged.
- Both closure statements now leave `open_with_park` rides out, as the DOWN reconstruction always has: the flag says their CLOSED readings are not evidence. The recovery curve skips the 21 closures already stored for two of them.
- `AttractionOutageService` no longer asks the closure statement about a ride the page already resolved to OPERATING.
