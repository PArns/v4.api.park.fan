### Added — `nearby` names the rides where a wait is short right now

`GET /v1/discovery/nearby` answers `nearby_parks` with `shortWaits` beside
`parks`: rides in the open nearby parks whose standby wait is at most 60 % of
their usual one and at least 10 minutes below it, the usual one being at least 15. Biggest gap first, at most three per park and five in all. `pickShortWaits`
(`short-waits.util.ts`) reads only the cached park payloads that the endpoint
already fetches with one `MGET`, so the list costs no query; it uses the same
`baseline` and `comparison` the park page rates a ride with. The new homepage
of park.fan shows the list as „Jetzt kurz anstehen". Contract in
[discovery](frontend/discovery.md#shortwaits).
