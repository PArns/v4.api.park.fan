### Changed — Parky speaks like the app's store kit, and a cold planned day marks its wet rides

Parky's chat prompt (version 68) and day-tip prompt (version 7) now follow the website's writing
rules that the app's store copy was rewritten under. The new "How you sound" block asks for a fact
in every sentence and a joke only where it carries one. It bans exclamation marks, slogans,
contrast reframes and things that talk ("the curve tells you"). It fixes one word per thing in all
six languages: Warteschlange, never bare "Schlange"; Bahn, never "Fahrgeschäft"; Andrang, never
"Auslastung"; the site's own feature names. The tone examples lost their exclamation marks and
mirrored halves and gained a day plan. "The friend who knows every park", the park count and the
sign at the entrance are gone. In the German chats on version 67 every day plan had the same
skeleton and opened with a disclaimer.

Four rules the model broke in those chats got sharper: no ticket price from a post, walks in
minutes and never metres, no reply chip that only announces an answer ("Ein anderer Tag"), and no
answer that announces a card a failed tool never returned.

`tooColdToGetWet` now also sits on the rides of `/plan/day` when the planned day's warmest hour
stays below 15 °C, the climate normal included past the forecast's reach
([too cold to get wet](frontend/too-cold-to-get-wet.md)). Until now the key read only the weather
right now, so on 2026-11-26 Parky planned three water rides into a Europa-Park day at 0 °C.
`get_park_day` passes the key on (PAR-1447).

A projected show the park's other shows outlived on that weekday by a week or more is no longer
projected. Europa-Park has no curated Halloween season, so "Ed's Halloween Parade", last seen on
2026-11-06, was still listed for Fridays on `/plan/day` three weeks later, and Parky planned it
into late-November days (PAR-1448). A report the app sends as feedback
(`[app-report reason=… message=…]`) is now also stored at the answer, so the admin's reported
list finds it (PAR-1449).
