### Added — the paid return window on 36 Disney rides is recorded as a fast pass

`PAID_RETURN_TIME` is the only queue type the `has_virtual_line` seed leaves
out, on the grounds that the paid product has three columns of its own. Those
columns were empty for every ride that reported it, so the fact landed nowhere:
36 rides across ten Disney parks carried between 5,547 and 38,855 readings of a
paid return window and `has_fast_pass` NULL. They now read `true`, and the audit
that found them is in `docs/admin/curation.md` with its before and after — the
gap went 36 to 0 while `has_virtual_line` kept its 140 untouched.

The brand went on first, because `has_fast_pass` without
`parks.curated_fast_pass_name` publishes the neutral "Fast Pass": Tokyo
Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea now name **Disney Premier Access**, the six US
Disney parks **Lightning Lane**, each with the glossary term id that makes the
chip link out. Prices stay empty on all 36 — Disney prices this per day, and a
frozen number would be wrong most days.
