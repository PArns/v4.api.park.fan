### Fixed — name matching strips "(DE)" suffixes and ®/™

`normalizeForMatching` lowercased before its country-code rule ran, so `Taron Coaster (DE)` stayed `taron coaster (de)`, and `transliterate` turned `®` into `(r)` before the trademark rule saw it (`taron(r) coaster`). Both rules now run on the right side of those steps, so these names match their plain counterparts in `ConflictResolverService.mergeEntities` and the other callers. Also removes the unused private `calculateStringSimilarity` from the conflict resolver. The new `slug.util.spec.ts` has 5 cases.
