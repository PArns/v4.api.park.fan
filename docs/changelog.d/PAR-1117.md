### Documented — holiday feature importances after the holiday dropout

[CatBoost training](ml/catboost-training.md) now records the importance of
`is_holiday_primary`, `is_school_holiday_primary` and `is_bridge_day` before
PAR-1051, in the first model after it and on 2026-11-06. School holidays rose
from 0.06 % to 3.27 % (rank 10 of 75); public holidays and bridge days stay
under 0.25 %.
