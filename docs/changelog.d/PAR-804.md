### Fixed — weather warning sync skips parks without a coordinate

`WeatherWarningsService.syncWarnings` ran `Number(p.latitude)` on nullable columns. `Number(null)` is 0 and passes `Number.isFinite`, so a park with `latitude: null` stayed in the country group and was matched at latitude 0, and for DE it was queried at Bright Sky with `(0, lon)`. A null latitude or longitude now skips the park before the conversion. The spec case `skips parks without a country, coordinates or source coverage` covers both columns and fails without the guard.
