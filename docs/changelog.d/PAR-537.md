### Fixed — the attraction history no longer loses the day of a spring-forward change

`calculateAttractionHistory` walked its window with `setDate(getDate() + 1)` from a
start built by `subDays`. On a UTC runtime both are fixed 24-hour steps, so a window
that contains the spring clock change starts at 23:00 the day before and the walk
steps over the 23-hour day itself. With today at 2027-03-31 and `days=6` in
Europe/Berlin, the history listed 03-26, 03-27, 03-29 and 03-30 and had no entry for
2027-03-28, although every one of those five days had queue data.

The loop now takes one noon-anchored instant per park-local day
(`eachDayAtNoonInTimezone`), so a date can neither be skipped nor repeated. The
fall-back duplicate the ticket suspected does not occur through this caller:
`subDays` from the day after the change lands at 01:00 local, never at midnight, so
the 25-hour day is stepped through once. A spec covers both changes.
