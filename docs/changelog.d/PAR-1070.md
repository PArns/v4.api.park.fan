### Changed — a water area closing on one evening is a season again, not a feed event

The seasonal detector drops candidates whose last OPERATING reading falls in
one shared minute per park, because that is one upstream write rather than
forty seasons starting at once. Its share-of-park threshold was 15 %, measured
against four production feed events sitting at 19-49 %. A park that really does
close one water area on a single evening produces the same shape, so it was
suppressed too: 14 rides in Carowinds' Carolina Harbor and 13 in Six Flags
Fiesta Texas' White Water Bay have published CLOSED since 2026-09-07 with no
`season_out_since` to qualify it.

The threshold is now 20 %. Measured against production, the 15-20 % band holds
eight clusters, 74 rides in seven parks; 38 of those 74, in four parks, clear
the detector's candidate gates, and all 38 are one water area each closing
while the main park kept running — Carowinds and Six Flags Fiesta Texas from
the report above, plus Cinecittà World and Fårup Sommerland, where the source
names the rides every poll and says CLOSED on a `lastUpdated` minutes old. The
three parks in the band whose clusters the source does not confirm as closures
never reach the guard: they fail `ever_operating`, `current_status` or the
seven-open-day count first.

`seasonal-feed-event.spec.ts` now imports `FEED_EVENT_CLUSTER_THRESHOLDS`
instead of carrying its own copy of the numbers, so the thresholds production
runs on are the ones under test.
