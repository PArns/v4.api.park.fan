### Fixed — read paths: park refresh rebuilds, attraction cache carries `days`, park-local forecast filters

- The park payload's stale-while-revalidate refresh read the still-live
  `park:integrated` key and wrote it back unchanged, so it never refreshed
  anything; it now rebuilds. Concurrent cold misses for one park share one
  build (`SingleFlight`), and so do concurrent misses on the park-level ML read
  `ml:park:{id}:{type}:{date}`.
- `attraction:integrated` ignored `?days=`: whichever history length was asked
  for first was served to every caller for five minutes. The key is now
  `attraction:integrated:{id}:{days}`, and the park merge/repair eviction
  deletes every variant by pattern.
- The park payload and the calendar's `hourly[]` matched forecasts to the
  park-local day by the UTC date prefix, and the calendar took the UTC hour.
  A Los Angeles evening (already tomorrow in UTC) lost its forecasts, which
  also emptied the per-ride ML crowd fallback. Both now read the day and hour
  on the park's clock.
- A calendar served from the month caches derived `meta.hasOperatingSchedule`
  from the requested months, the uncached build from the park's whole
  operating range; both use the range now.
- `/plan/day?date=2026-02-31` returned March 3 instead of 400.
- The downtime-recovery coverage gate divides with `NULLIF`, like the rest of
  the query, instead of relying on `AND` evaluation order to avoid a
  division by zero.
