### Changed — the model-comparison gate weighs leads equally, resamples dates too and is judged on a fixed date

A cell pooled its question's leads by slot count, so `plan_d6_60` read as d6/d7
for two months — the only leads with rows. Each lead is now pooled on its own,
the cell is the mean of its leads, and the 10 parks × 14 days gate has to hold
at every lead. The gate's 95 % interval is a two-way park × date bootstrap, so a
day that moved every park at once counts once instead of once per park; the
park-only interval is shown beside it. A cell's earliest date no longer slides
with "today + 14" while a lead has no rows: it is never before the archive's
start (09.10.2026) + its longest lead + 14 days. The ML overview's decisions
carry a fixed `evaluateOn` date: before it a decision is never ready, from it on
it is judged once on the window ending the day before.
