### Added — Chronos-2 can serve the live 45–120 min forecast behind `SERVE_CHRONOS_INTRADAY`

Prepared, not switched on: with `SERVE_CHRONOS_INTRADAY=true` the served intraday wait for slots 45–120 minutes ahead comes from Chronos-2's `live` curve (q50 shown, q95 − q50 as `uncertaintyMinutes`, q80 against the P50 baseline for the crowd level), read from a per-ride Redis cache (`ml:chronos-serve:<id>`, 90-minute staleness guard) that the forward-archive capture writes at the intraday origins and — only while the flag is on — refreshes at the odd park-local hours 11–17. ≤ 30 min stays the persistence blend, > 120 min stays PCN, and both are the fallback. `prediction_accuracy.served_source` can read `chronos`; the archive codes served Chronos slots `s` on surface 1 and, with the flag on, captures the incumbent curve as surface 8 so the live A/B keeps comparing Chronos with the model it replaced. Default off changes nothing in production. Switch-on checklist and rollback: docs/ml/forward-archive.md §10.
