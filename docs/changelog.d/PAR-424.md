### Added — whether an attraction keeps you dry, as a curated field (`indoorOutdoor`)

A nullable `indoor_outdoor` on attractions, hand-decided through the existing
curated-field editor: `indoor` (ride and queue under a roof), `outdoor`
(neither), `covered_queue` (queue covered, ride outside). It reaches both the
attraction endpoint and every ride on a park payload. The rain nowcast can say
that it is about to rain; this is the field a rain plan needs to say where to
go.

No source we ingest or may ingest carries it: ThemeParks.wiki, Queue-Times and
Wartezeiten.app have nothing, and RCDB and park websites are ruled out by their
terms (frontend `docs/product/attraction-metadata-sources.md`). The column
starts empty. The source of each value goes into the `reason` and `sourceUrl`
of the audit row every curation already writes.

Null means nobody has checked the attraction. It is neither indoor nor outdoor,
and every reader behaves as it did before the column existed. Stored as text,
not a Postgres enum, like `attraction_kind`. It appears on the next deploy
through `synchronize`, so a first fill can only run after that.
