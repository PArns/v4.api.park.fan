### Documented — TFT's `predicted_peak` is the sample mean, not the median

[TFT](ml/tft.md) and [quantile serving](ml/quantile-serving-and-calibration.md) said in three
places that the served daily peak is the median of the forecast distribution. It is the mean of
1,000 draws: with a `DistributionLoss` NeuralForecast names the sample mean `TFT` and the median
`TFT-median`, and `nf-service` `_tft_column` returns the first of the two it meets. The backtests
read the same column, so the measured errors describe what is served; nothing served changes.
Whether `TFT-median`, which varies less between two runs of one model, is the better point on
busy days is a `backtest_headliners.py` question.
