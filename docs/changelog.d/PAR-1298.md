### Added — trip regions: the vacation plan knows Orlando's tickets

The region route serves a ticket catalog (`tickets`): 35 products of Walt
Disney World, Universal Orlando, SeaWorld Parks, Discovery Cove and LEGOLAND,
researched for late 2026 and 2027, each with its parks, the calendar days its
use may spread over per length, its hopping, its caps (one visit per park on
the 4-Park Magic Ticket, no Epic Universe on Universal's 1- and 2-day tickets),
its allowances (the UK Magic Tickets' water parks), its sale dates and, for
passes, the blockout calendar. No prices
([tickets](frontend/trip-regions.md#tickets)).

`/plan` takes the tickets a visitor holds (`tickets=wdw-uk-14-day-magic@2027-02-01,…`,
an unknown hopper as `.h?`): every day a ticket is used on lies in its window, it
is used on no more days than it has, a hop day needs a ticket that hops between
the two parks, and a ticket without a start is searched over every start on
sale. Owned tickets' resorts mix inside their windows only where that saves at
least 3 minutes per ride over the same plan in blocks (`meta.mixing`). When no
placement keeps a window, a day leaves it at a price and says so
(`ticket_window_relaxed`); a visit beyond a ticket's days is left out with
`no_ticket_day`. Each day names its ticket and its day on it. Without tickets
the plan suggests one per resort (`meta.ticketSuggestion`): its length, valid
dates and extra days, the UK 14-day tickets on a long trip, and whether a
hopper is worth adding, recommended only for an evening it opens, a park only it
fits, or 3 minutes a ride saved. `hopper=<resort>` gives plan first the hopper
per resort. A 21-day plan across three resorts answers in 0.2–0.6 s on the dev
shim. `/when` takes tickets without dates and `suggest=1`; Parky's
`plan_vacation` and `find_trip_dates` take both (prompt version 51).

The catalog is data edited in the admin, not code: `ticket_products` holds each
product with every rule the optimizer reads, `links` (seller, url, one official,
`sponsored`, markets and locales, in display order) and its sources, seeded once
from `tickets.ts` and `ticket-seed-links.ts` (plain links, never sponsored) for a
region whose table is empty. `GET /v1/trip-regions/:region/tickets` serves it with
its version, five minutes at the edge and ten stale; the region route keeps
`tickets` for older clients. Editors create, patch, archive and order products
under `/v1/admin/content/regions/:region/tickets`; a write is checked whole, every
problem by its field path, planned on a sample trip, audited before and after,
and published outside-in (`trip-tickets:<region>`, again after the edge window).
A new product goes last in its region, a PATCH clears an optional field with
`null`, and `validate?slug=<slug>` checks an edit without calling its own slug
taken.
Plans and `/when` are cached by the catalog version, and their tickets and
suggestions carry the product's links
([editing the catalog](frontend/trip-regions.md#editing-the-catalog)).
