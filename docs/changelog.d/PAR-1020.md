### Fixed — the schedule sync flags holidays by the calendar's rule

The schedule sync and the gap-fill wrote `isHoliday`, `holidayName` and
`isBridgeDay` from two private copies of the holiday map. Both now read
`buildLocalHolidayMap()` (`src/common/utils/holiday.utils.ts`), the map the
calendar and `/predictions/yearly` already use.

Two differences from the calendar are gone. A holiday row without a region that
is not nationwide counted as a neighbour's holiday in the sync and as the park's
own in the calendar; it is now the park's own in both. And a holiday was filed
under the day its row names read at noon UTC in the park's zone, which is the
next day east of UTC+12: Christmas Day at Rainbow's End (`Pacific/Auckland`)
was written on 26 December. Rows are now keyed by the date they carry, and each
schedule day is rated at park-local noon.

Rows already written keep their flags until the next sync rewrites them.
