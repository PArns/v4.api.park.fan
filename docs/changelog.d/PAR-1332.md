### Documented — the models forecast the wait given a queue, and a replay that sizes the cost

CatBoost, PCN, TFT and the forward archive's truth all read waits ≥ 5 min only, so each model
predicts the wait *given that there is a queue*, and a ride that is walk-on on most hours is
forecast at the height of its rare queues. On 2026-11-14 a ride at Fantawild Dreamland Xiamen
with a typical wait of 0 on 115 sample days and one evening at 50 minutes was forecast 45–50
minutes for every hour; the park's raw CatBoost bias over 28 days was +23 minutes, and the
all-park bias has read +6 since real zeros are scored (PAR-912) against +2 before. The forward
archive and the PCN board cannot see it, because their truth has the same floor.
[Which compared slot is a forecast error](ml/accuracy-scoring-rule.md#rides-that-rarely-have-a-queue-par-1332)
now carries a read-only replay that measures what a hurdle (serve 0 where a ride had a queue on
fewer than half of its scored slots in the two weeks before) would do per segment, checked
against the schema in the e2e container. Nothing served changes.
