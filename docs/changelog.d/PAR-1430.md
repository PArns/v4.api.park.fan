### Added — A visitor can delete their own Parky chat and report an answer; positions stay out of the logs

Three things park.fan's app needs before it goes to the App Store.

`DELETE /v1/assistant/conversations/:id` with `Authorization: Bearer <sessionToken>` deletes the server's copy of a chat for the session that started it, web or app: the chat and its messages go, the day statistics keep its numbers (counted in the same transaction as the nightly prune does, and skipped rather than blocking the delete when they cannot be written), feedback sent from it stays, unlinked. It answers 204 whether the chat was just deleted, deleted before, never there or another session's, which is left alone; 401 `challenge` without a valid token.

`POST /v1/assistant/report` `{ conversationId, messageId, reason, note?, sessionToken }` stores "Antwort melden" at the answer: `reason` is `wrong`, `inappropriate` or `other`, `note` at most 500 characters, 5 an hour per session apart from the feedback's share. Three nullable columns on `assistant_messages` (`reportReason`, `reportNote`, `reportedAt`) and `reportedAt` on `assistant_conversations` hold it; `GET /v1/admin/assistant/conversations?reported=true` filters the list, the chat's detail carries the report on the answer, and the MCP's chat tools show it. A report goes with its chat after 7 days, as the app's consent text promises. The feedback route still takes the older builds' `[app-report …]` messages. See [assistant](frontend/assistant.md#reporting-an-answer-and-deleting-a-chat).

A slow or failed request's log line no longer names where a visitor stands: `redactUrl` turns `lat`, `lng`, `lon`, `long`, `latitude` and `longitude` into `***`, in any casing and as `lat[]=`, for the request line, the error file and the error body's `path`. `/v1/discovery/nearby` no longer logs the park a caller stands in and the distance to it, and the popularity counter's debug line redacts its URL. What is still kept of a position: [no address in logs](rules/no-address-in-logs.md#what-is-kept-of-a-position).
