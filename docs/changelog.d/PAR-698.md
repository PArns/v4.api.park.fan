### Fixed — Days the schedule calls CLOSED no longer count in `topAttractions` or `/stats/hourly`

PAR-692 dropped shut days from the park's day values. The two queries that read
`queue_data_aggregates` directly kept them: 78,425 of Europa-Park's 252,893
aggregate rows over one year sit on days the calendar lists as CLOSED, where
the feed posts rides as OPERATING with a wait of 0 around the clock. Those
zeros pulled the top-ten averages down and bought rides sample days towards
the floor that decides whether they may be ranked at all.

The rule now lives once for the aggregate queries, in
`src/analytics/closed-park-days.sql.ts`, and all three readers use it —
including the day values, which carried it inline. The hourly profile applies it
in both halves of its statement: the ranking CTE and the hours it prints.
`PARK_DAY_IS_CLOSED_SQL` in the ML accuracy service states the same rule a
fourth time, in a shape that cannot read this CTE; it is left alone here and
tracked separately.

`topAttractions` also counts its sample days in the park's timezone now. It
read `DATE(qda.hour)`, a UTC day, while `schedule_entries.date` and both
sibling queries are park-local; dropping rows by one day notion and counting
days by another inside one query cannot be right. The counts move for 1,468 of
5,692 rides and the 20-day floor decides differently for 14 of them.

Both cache versions are bumped so the filter applies on deploy rather than up
to 24 hours later: `park:hourly-profile:v6` and `HISTORICAL_STATS_CACHE_VERSION = 4`.
