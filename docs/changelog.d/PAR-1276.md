### Changed — Parky reads a park's days with one tool and its hours with another

`get_park_forecast` (31 days from today, today's hours) and the new day comparison returned nearly
the same days, and the hours of a whole park existed for today only: "is the afternoon better on
Saturday?" had to be answered ride by ride.

- `get_days` is the one tool for days: the next seven by default, a stretch of up to 31 or 1 to 10
  particular dates, up to a year ahead, each with whether the park is open, closed (with the next
  open day) or has not published its hours, and now the rides a maintenance window shuts that day.
  `get_park_forecast` is gone.
- `get_hours` lays one day of one park out hour by hour, today or any day up to a year ahead: the
  headliners' average expected wait for each open hour, today's past hours marked, the three
  quietest hours still to come and the busiest, a closed day with the next open one, and what the
  numbers are (`tierWhy`: the hourly model, the day's forecast spread over the rides' usual day, or
  how comparable days went a year earlier).
- A single ride keeps `get_ride`'s `bestVisitTimesToday` for today and `get_ride_forecast` for
  another day.
- The system prompt (version 39) sends "when is it quietest in the day" to `get_hours`. The eval
  has a case for it.
