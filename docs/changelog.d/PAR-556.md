### Fixed — Queue-Times single-rider lines no longer become attractions

Queue-Times lists the single-rider line of a ride as a park entry of its own, and
`ChildrenMetadataProcessor.syncQtAttraction` created a row for it. Eight such rows
exist at Disney's Animal Kingdom, Islands of Adventure and Universal Studios
Florida; each one counts as a second ride and writes its wait times as `STANDBY`.

The creation branch now skips an entry named `<ride> Single Rider` when the park
already carries a ride of that name, compared with `normalizeName` so a trademark
sign does not matter. A ride that already has a row keeps being updated. The eight
existing rows are retired separately in PAR-965. No schema change.
