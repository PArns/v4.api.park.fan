### Fixed — event rows in the schedule are no longer deleted every night

The cross-type phase of the schedule cleanup (`crossTypeConflictSql`) now ranks
only `OPERATING`, `CLOSED` and `UNKNOWN`. `TICKETED_EVENT`, `PRIVATE_EVENT`,
`EXTRA_HOURS`, `MAINTENANCE` and `INFO` used to fall to `ELSE 4`, below
`UNKNOWN`, so a day that also had opening hours lost them on every pass and the
next sync wrote them back. A Halloween Horror Nights evening now stays next to
the park's opening hours. Production on 2026-10-02: 167 such rows (166 `INFO`,
1 `TICKETED_EVENT`), none of them currently sharing a day with a day-status row,
because the cleanup had already removed every one that did.
