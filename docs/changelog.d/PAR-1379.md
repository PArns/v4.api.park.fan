### Fixed — forecasts and rope-drop split on the park country's weekend in the Gulf and Israel

For parks in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman and Israel CatBoost's
`is_weekend` was Friday and Saturday, while everything else that split
weekdays from weekends counted Saturday and Sunday: CatBoost's
`rolling_avg_weekday/_weekend` and `volatility_weekday/_weekend` on both paths,
the weekend-or-weekday choice for far rows in serving, in the origin hold-out
and in the holiday dropout, TFT's and Chronos-2's `is_weekend`, and PCN's
`is_weekend` channel. A Saudi Friday was a weekend day to the model and a
weekday reading in the statistics it was given.

Each Python service now reads one table, `weekend_rules.py`: the API's
`CUSTOM_WEEKEND_RULES`, owned by ml-service and vendored byte-identical into
nf-service and pcn-service, with tests comparing the copies and the API's
TypeScript table. CatBoost, TFT, Chronos-2 and PCN split on it in training and
serving, and the served rolling statistics tag a weekend hour with the same
table in SQL ([CatBoost](ml/catboost.md)). A model trained before this change
reads the new inputs with the meaning it learned until the next nightly
training replaces it ([TFT](ml/tft.md), [PCN](ml/pcn.md)).

Rope-drop's weekend and weekday levels used Saturday and Sunday for every park;
they now take the country's weekend, as typical waits already did. Its
800-day history read started from the server's `CURRENT_DATE` and now starts
from the park's own today ([park timezone](rules/park-timezone.md)).

Removed `AnalyticsService.calculate95thPercentile`: nothing called it, and it
grouped hours and weekdays on UTC.
