### Added — the calendar can leave a headliner out of the days its season rules out

The models forecast every ride for every day and read no season, so a headliner outside its
season months or its bound park season counted towards a calendar day's average, crowd level and
headliner list, while `/plan/day` dropped the same ride for the same day. With
`CALENDAR_SEASON_FILTER=true` the calendar applies the planner's rule
(`withoutRidesOutOfSeason`) before it aggregates; a ride that is seasonal with no months and no
binding stays in. Default off, read per call: the switch changes a served crowd level, so it
waits for a look at the days it moves ([crowd levels](rules/crowd-levels.md)). No entity change.
