### Fixed — a ride its season rules out no longer counts in the calendar or gets a forecast on its page

The models forecast every ride for every day and read no season. Two readers took those rows as
they came, while `/plan/day` dropped the ride:

- **Calendar and yearly view.** A headliner outside its season months, outside its bound park
  season or paused by one counted towards a day's average, crowd level and headliner list. River
  Quest, a headliner paused for Phantasialand's Wintertraum, went into every Wintertraum day
  with a forecast of about 10 minutes. With `CALENDAR_SEASON_FILTER=true` (on in
  `docker-compose.production.yml`, off in the code) both apply the planner's rule
  (`withoutRidesOutOfSeason`) before they aggregate; a ride that is seasonal with no months and
  no binding stays in ([crowd levels](rules/crowd-levels.md)).
- **Ride page.** A ride its season rules out today was still served an hourly forecast, best
  visit times ("10:00, 10 min, optimal") and a rope-drop recommendation. All three are withheld
  now unless the feed says the ride is running after all, which refutes a detector's season
  note.

No entity change; one new read (`AttractionsService.getSeasonFacts`) while the switch is on.
