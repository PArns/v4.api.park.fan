### Fixed — `/plan/day` serves no ride opening on a weekday the ride does not run on

`opensAt` is the median first operating time over the days a ride ran, and
`/plan/day` served it on every day. Movie Park's Halloween mazes run Fri–Sun
evenings, so on a Monday four of nine read „opens at 17:00".

Each ride now carries `runsOnWeekdays` (ISO 1–7): a weekday counts when the
ride ran on at least half of the days the park was open on it in the last eight
weeks, read from observed readings rather than the calendar. A weekday the park
was open on only once counts only for a ride that runs whenever the park does,
so the mazes' one Wednesday stays out and Star Trek keeps the holiday Monday.
On any other weekday `opensAt` is left out. Rides with less than two weeks of
history, or with no operating day in the last seven, carry no pattern and keep
the old behaviour. Against production on 2026-10-19 the six mazes with readings
read `[5, 6, 7]`, so none of the nine has an `opensAt` on a Monday.
