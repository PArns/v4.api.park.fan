### Fixed — `/plan/day` serves no ride opening on a weekday the ride does not run on

`opensAt` is the median first operating time over the days a ride ran, and
`/plan/day` served it on every day. Movie Park's Halloween mazes run Fri–Sun
evenings, so on a Monday four of nine read „opens at 17:00".

Each ride now carries `runsOnWeekdays` (ISO 1–7), the weekdays it ran on in the
last eight weeks of observed readings, a weekday counting at half of its
occurrences since the ride's first day in the window. On any other weekday
`opensAt` is left out. Rides with less than two weeks of history, or with no
operating day in the last seven, carry no pattern and keep the old behaviour.
Against production on 2026-10-19 the six mazes with readings read `[5, 6, 7]`,
so none of the nine has an `opensAt` on a Monday.
