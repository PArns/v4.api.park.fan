### Fixed — quiet hours no longer vanish from `queue_data_aggregates`

`calculate-percentiles` and `backfill-percentiles` dropped every hour with fewer than
three `queue_data` rows. `queue_data` stores changes plus one heartbeat per quiet hour, so
a ride whose posted wait barely moves (Phantasialand) had one or two rows per hour and no
aggregate at all. `/stats/hourly` for Phantasialand returned hours `[10, 11, 12, 13, 17]`;
14 to 16 were missing.

Both jobs now share one SELECT (`src/queues/processors/hourly-aggregate.sql.ts`). Each hour
with a reading also takes the last STANDBY row before the hour (at most 3 hours back, and
only when it is OPERATING with a wait time) as an extra sample. The `HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3`
floor is now `>= 2`, which is one real row plus its anchor.

Effect on readers: every consumer of `queue_data_aggregates` (`typicalWaits`, calendar
rating, plan-day, ML training features, `forecast-accuracy`, park historical stats, ride day
curve) now sees the hours that were dropped, and means that were inflated by only the busy
hours come down. The anchor is added to every hour, so `sampleCount` is one higher than
before on hours that already had a row. `calculate-percentiles` upserts, so a re-run of a
day rewrites that day. `backfill-percentiles` uses `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`: it fills the
missing hours and leaves old rows as they were.

Known gap: an hour in which the ride wrote no row at all (last change at 13:59, heartbeat
only at 15:00) still has no aggregate.
