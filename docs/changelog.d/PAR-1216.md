### Added — Parky answers questions about a ride's facts

Parky could not say who built Black Mamba, when it opened, how fast Taron is or
how many launches it has, although the curated ride profile holds all of it:
the admin MCP's `get_attraction`, which Parky's `get_ride` reuses, dropped
`rideProfile`. It now carries the profile as `profile`, flattened by
`rideFacts` (type, track figures in order, launches, inversions, manufacturer,
model, opening year, top speed, height, length, duration; unknown fields left
out). Launches are counted off the track figures, `launch` and `swing-launch`
as on the ride page, and only when figures were curated. A new tool,
`compare_rides`, ranks one park's rides by one of those facts or by minimum
rider height, optionally only those a rider of a given height may ride, and
counts the rides it left out for want of data. Prompt version 12 sends ride
facts to these two tools. Details in
[the assistant page](frontend/assistant.md#the-answer-loop).
