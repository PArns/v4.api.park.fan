### Fixed — the release's review: unridden rides first, the park's own now, UTC parks, unstated days

A fresh review of this release and PArns/park.fan#1194 found:

- `compare_rides` without `by` sorted by name and cut at `limit`, so "which coasters haven't we
  done" could miss the ones not ridden yet. They now come first, counted as `notRidden`.
- Every time reached Parky in park time, but "now" only in the visitor's zone, so "the next show"
  could be one already running. `timesIn` names the park's own now, and the system prompt
  (version 46) has "next", "still" and "already over" go by it; its rule against narrating time
  zones names "park time" as the one exception.
- `get_park` and `park_forecast` wrote a UTC park's times with `Z`, which `localizeTimes` reads in
  the fallback zone when a call names several parks; they write `+00:00` now.
- `compare_parks` dropped a park whose published schedule gives the day no status without a word;
  it lands in `notKnown` with that reason, and `get_days` gives such a day a `why`.
- The rides card's `typeUnknownRides` and `shelterUnknownRides` carry ids like every other list.
- Parky's glossary lookup asks the frontend for its pass over the long definitions (`deep=1`),
  which the site's search palette leaves out.
- The PAR-1279 note had the frontend ship first; the API goes first.
