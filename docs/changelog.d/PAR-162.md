### Fixed — the source-absent check states why it ignores carried heartbeats

`isSourceAbsent` in `src/common/utils/source-absent-status.util.ts` reads only
`dataSource`, while `observedReadingsSql()` also checks `is_heartbeat`. The open
question about that difference is answered in a comment on the function: all
three callers read one row per queue type, and heartbeat and reconciliation both
write `STANDBY`, so the two never meet in one row set. A heartbeat is also
written for rides the feed still reports unchanged, so it cannot stand for
absence. No behaviour change. Measured 2026-10-02: 7 of 6,403 rides were in the
first 24 h after their last feed sighting.
