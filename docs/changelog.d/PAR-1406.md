### Changed — Parky leads with what he knows, stands by his tip, keeps a day plan short and offers only what a tool does

From the 36 chats of 16 to 22 November, 20 of them on prompt 62. The facts were
mostly right; the tone gave him away.

**A gap as the lead.** About 17 answers opened with what was missing: "No tengo
datos de un pase de pago…", "I don't have a price … that field is still
unknown", "Je n'ai pas d'info sur une file single rider", "kann ich aus den
Daten nicht ablesen". The rule against it sat in the middle of a long paragraph.
The prompt (version 63) now has a paragraph of its own. A gap is one clause after
the useful part, said as whose call it is or as not published yet, and the
phrases Parky used are named as never said, in any language. That a day is far
ahead or a figure an estimate is said once per chat at most.

**His own answers and tip "corrected".** "Dazu eine Korrektur meinerseits … kann
ich nicht mehr nachprüfen", "I need to correct my tip first". At Epic Universe
the tip's 81 % belonged to the day it compared with ("go today instead"), so the
tip was right. Words such as "correction" are now named as never said, and
`dayTipLine` says that a tip may weigh its day against a neighbour and that a
change is told as news.

**A day plan as a timetable.** "How do we make the most of that day?", the
question under a day tip, opened 12 chats and got 150 to 250 words in four or
five bullets full of figures. One plan began with "Vormittag nutzen" at 13:39.
A list now holds four one-line items at most. A day's game plan is three or
four bullets in the order of the day, the ride to start with, and the offer to
put it into the day planner. For today it plans only the hours still ahead.

**Offers no tool keeps.** "Want me to keep an eye on the calendar", "te dejo
marcados los juegos en el mapa", "Ich schaue kurz vorher nochmal drauf", and the
chip "Avísame si cambia la previsión". Parky now offers only what a tool does,
and a chip never asks what the answer just said.

**Smaller rules:**
- "Why will it be busy?" gets the reasons the lookup marks and the level in
  minutes, never "I can't tell why".
- Park words come in the answer's language, not "crowd level muy bajo" or
  "Peak-Waits".
- On a post, a question about its park reads the post first.
- Rides an earlier answer named are found with one `get_park_day`, not five
  `find_rides`.
- `riderHeightCm` is never a placeholder (one plan went in at 150 cm).
- A knowledge topic's prices may be given. The Quick Pass topic has them, and
  the old rule made Parky take back a correct 80 €.
- The interview asks one question per answer. It takes the park or region the
  page shows. It leaves a ticket's window to the plan, and "thanks, that's all"
  ends it.

The eval gains nineteen cases. Nine come from these chats. Ten are probes:
- an unknown minimum height
- sarcasm about a breakdown
- a price a post names
- a wheelchair and a pregnancy question, where nothing may be guessed
- an injected day tip
- a competitor
- "busy now?" at a closed park
- a disputed wait
- homework

It takes a `dayTip`, and it keeps the answer's text when the last round only
closes `suggest_replies`. `--render <dir>` writes every case as the model gets
it, for a stand-in such as a local agent, without a key.

**Reading a prompt version's chats.** The admin MCP gains `assistant_transcripts`.
It returns whole chats a page at a time, up to 20. It keeps every chat with at
least one answer on `promptVersion`, so a chat that spans a deploy shows both
versions, each answer marked with its own. `assistant_conversations` still
filters by the latest answer's version, and `assistant_conversation` still reads
one chat at a time. Rating, a word and the site's language narrow it further.
It names the version answering now. The visitor's words stay under `visitorData`;
no hash and no address goes out ([MCP](admin/mcp.md)).
