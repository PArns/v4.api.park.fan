### Fixed — the season level serves what was measured, floors a type until it has three days, and moves only with the warmup

The [season level](ml/season-level.md) (PAR-1343) served TFT's number × the median of measured P90 ÷
TFT's forecast by default. The early days' forecasts were made before TFT had seen the season,
the ratio was applied to its later runs, and nothing bounded it: a ride TFT forecast at 6 minutes
in the opening week that measured 30 carried a ratio of 5, so a Saturday TFT put at 32 minutes
would have been served 160. The band and the confidence of the TFT row stayed as they were.
`SEASON_LEVEL_MODE` now defaults to `level` in the code, in `docker-compose.production.yml` and in
`.env.example`. The ratio stays selectable and is held to 0.5–3, the product to the highest P90
the type measured, and its rows carry `tft+season-ratio`, so lead snapshots and `/plan/day` tell
them from a measured level. A row the season level replaces has no band and no confidence: both
described the model's number. The yearly view's `confidencePercentage` averages the rows that
carry one and is null where none does.

`/plan/day` scaled every ride at every lead by any season row, a ratio row included, on the ground
that a season level is measured; a ratio row is TFT's forecast × a ratio. It now takes the TFT
gates (busy rides, leads 0–2) like TFT; a measured level keeps scaling every ride at every lead
([slots and H5](frontend/plan-day/slots-and-h5.md#on-a-day-of-a-running-park-season)).

A neighbour region's holiday or school break made a day off. At Phantasialand 25 of the 30
weekdays from 28 September to 6 November 2026 carry an own or a neighbour's break, so a Halloween
season there would have had one type again, and 7, 8, 11 and 12 January 2027 got the Christmas
break's level through the neighbours alone; the measured neighbour effect is +17–21 %. A
**neighbour day** is now a type of its own, between the park's own days off and the eves, and an
eve is the day before an own day off. Past the earliest last stored school-break row among the
influencing regions that store any, a working day no stored row makes a neighbour day is unknown.

A type with fewer than three measured days read the raw models: after the deploy working days
until Thursday 19 November, days off until Sunday 22 November, and the eves until 11 December,
below the Thursdays before them. Such a row is now served the model's number or a quieter type's
level, whichever is higher: the working days' under an eve or a neighbour day, the highest of the
eves', neighbour days' and working days' under a day off. A type with its own three days is never
floored, and an unknown day never is.

The inputs were cached under the park-local date, so a type could reach its third day at the
park's midnight and move every day of that type: `/plan/day` and Parky followed while the
calendar grid kept the old level for up to nine hours, because only the calendar warmup rings the
frontend (PAR-1380). They now live under `ml:season-level:v4:{parkId}` without a date, for 14
hours, and the calendar warmup evicts them with the daily ML and month caches, so the forecast
ring after the run covers every change. A key that runs out after a failed warmup is recomputed
on the next read, and that change is not rung ([caching](architecture/caching-strategy.md)).
