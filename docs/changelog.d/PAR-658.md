### Added — the hourly forecast is recorded at fixed leads, so its error per lead can be measured

`prediction_accuracy` keeps the newest run per slot, so a row kept a long
lead only when no later run predicted the slot: over 30 days the 31,360
completed rows with a lead over 24 h were 345 rides at an actual wait of
0.03 min, parks that then stayed closed. The new hypertable
`hourly_lead_snapshots` (additive, created by `DB_SYNCHRONIZE` on deploy, made
a hypertable at boot) copies each full-hour slot once per lead bucket (`h0-2`, `h2-6`,
`h6-24`, `h24+`, sampled at 2, 6, 24 and 36 h) with the raw CatBoost answer,
its band, the model version and the served value and model. About
115,000 rows a day off-season, up to 240,000 on a peak day, dropped after 35 days by `predictions/cleanup-old`;
the actual comes from the slot's `prediction_accuracy` row. Nothing reads it
yet. See [hourly lead snapshots](ml/hourly-lead-snapshots.md).
