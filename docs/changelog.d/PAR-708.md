### Fixed — topAttractions drops single-sample hours like the other aggregate queries

`queryTopAttractions` was the only one of the three `queue_data_aggregates`
queries without `qda."sampleCount" >= MIN_SAMPLES_PER_HOUR` (2). A day made only
of one-sample hours was discarded by the day-values and hourly-profile queries,
but still counted as a sample day in the top-attractions table, towards the
`minAttractionDays` floor and in `avg_p50` / `avg_p90`. All three now leave out
the same hours, bound to the same constant. The effect on production rankings is
measured in PAR-964 after the deploy.
