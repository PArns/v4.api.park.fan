### Added — US school breaks for Orlando, Southern California and Texas

The US had 78 parks and no school-holiday rows: neither Nager.Date nor
OpenHolidays carries US school data, because US school calendars are set per
district. The holiday sync now writes a curated file,
`src/holidays/data/curated-holidays-us.ts`, with a source URL on every entry
taken from the district's own calendar
([docs](architecture/holiday-sources-outside-europe.md#united-states-par-1071)):

- **FL — Orange County Public Schools** (Orlando): summer 2025 through summer
  2028, which ends on the published 2028-29 first day.
- **TX — Northside ISD** (San Antonio): 2025-26 through 2027-28. Summer 2028
  stops on 2028-07-31, the last day its calendar shows.
- **CA — Los Angeles Unified**: 2025-26 through 2027-28, through 2028-06-30.

Every day students are off is listed: the breaks, summer, and single student
holidays. Parks match holidays by state, so each state carries one district's
calendar. Tampa, Winter Haven, Arlington, San Diego and the Bay Area get
that calendar too, which is a documented simplification.

**Changed:** in the US and AU, a state without school rows is now unknown
instead of borrowing another state's coverage (`REGION_ONLY_SCHOOL_COVERAGE`,
in TypeScript and both `holiday_features.py` copies). Without this, Ohio would
have read as "no school holiday" through 2028. The summer projection
(`synthesizeProjectedSummerHolidays`) now skips curated regions, so a curated
file that ends in June is not extended with an invented summer.

**Fixed:** Nager.Date's Truman Day (US-MO, types `School` + `Authorities`)
was stored as a school holiday. In the US, Nager's `School` tag is now
ignored, so the day is stored as `public`. Those rows had also given every US
park a "known, no break" school coverage through 2028-05-08; the ~38 states
without a district now read as unknown.
