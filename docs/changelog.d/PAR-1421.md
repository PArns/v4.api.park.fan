### Fixed — a park's Ø wait and crowd level are one reading in every answer

At Phantasialand on 24 November around 15:00 the favourites card said 20 min,
the nearby card Ø 25 min and the country list Ø 10 min. There were three
causes.

The geo tree computed its own statistic, the mean of every ride's last wait
with the walk-ons at 0, where every other answer reads the headliners' current
wait from `calculateParkOccupancy`. The park payload measured occupancy when it
was built but took `statistics.avgWaitTime` and `crowdLevel` from the
`park:statistics` snapshot of the previous warmup run, so one payload carried
two readings up to five minutes apart, and favourites and nearby paired the new
Ø wait with the old crowd level. The in-park park read the snapshot too.

Now the park payload states both from the reading it serves and stores that
reading as `park:occupancy`, and the tree, favourites, nearby and the in-park
park read the pair from there (`liveHeadline`). Each answer also says when the
reading was taken (`updatedAt`), so a client holding a park from several
answers can show the newest. See
[one reading per park](frontend/discovery.md#one-reading-per-park).
