### Added — the plan's ideas, short trips without a rest day in the middle, resort_left_out

- **`GET /v1/trip-regions/:region/plan/ideas` lists the plan's best single
  edits, each planned again.** The same query as `/plan`; at most three ideas,
  each a kind (`move`, `swap`, `add`, `travel_day`, `hopper`, `avoid_party`),
  the change as `/plan` parameters to apply as is (`arrival=park`,
  `lock=…,epcot@2026-12-09`, `hopper=walt-disney-world&hop=1`), and the park's
  `headlinerWait` on its day and the plan's `totals.headlinerWait` before and
  after. An idea places a chosen park the plan leaves out, adds a park on a
  spare day or saves at least 5 minutes over the visits both plans place; it
  never costs a visit or a pin. Every idea is `optimizePlan` on the changed
  query, on the calendar the plan reads, at most ten plans an answer, cached an
  hour apart from the plan (`trip-regions:plan-ideas:v1:*`), so `/plan` costs
  what it did. Orlando, 6–13 December with a Disney hotel: Hollywood Studios
  fits with a park on the arrival or departure day, or with the hopper, in
  45–60 ms; the heaviest trip measured, 21 days and eight parks, takes 2.2 s
  ([ideas](frontend/trip-regions.md#ideas-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplanideas)).
- **A trip of four days or fewer keeps its park days together.** Paris, 12–14
  March 2027, with a park on arrival and departure and two parks open, planned
  park, rest, park when the Saturday in the middle was the busier day; now two
  park days in a row and a free end day. The rest rule needs no rest day on a
  trip this short, so a day between two park days costs 400 minutes in the
  optimizer, more than days' waits differ and less than a repeat on the next
  day or a day without hours. No frozen Orlando plan or window changed
  ([rest days](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-plan-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplan)).
- **`resort_left_out` in `meta.warnings`** says that a resort the visitor chose
  has no park day at all for lack of days while another park has one. The
  Paris weekend of 14–16 May 2027 with a free arrival and departure day has one
  park day, and Parc Astérix's Saturday (40 minutes) beats Disneyland Park's (55)
  and Disney Adventure World's (65), so the plan is a day at Parc Astérix: the
  rule that drops the park whose best day is dearest, working as documented.
  The ideas price the way back: a park on the departure day places Disneyland
  Park.
- **Which park leaves no longer reads hours not yet published.** The drop
  order ranked a park's best day with the 1,000-minute penalty for a day
  without published hours, so where one resort publishes months ahead
  (Parc Astérix) and the other does not (Disneyland Paris), the other always
  dropped first. It now reads the day's waits; the penalty still decides which
  day of a park is used last.

### Fixed — the park calendar closes the days a curated closure states

The region calendar applied a `closure` season (`buildParkDay`), the park
calendar did not: Typhoon Lagoon's closure until 26 January 2027 made its days
`CLOSED` on the Orlando trip calendar, while its own page read the feed alone,
`UNKNOWN` with a crowd forecast wherever the feed was silent.
Both now read one rule, `curatedClosureOn` (`trip-calendar.util.ts`): a
`closure` that is `confirmed` or `announced` and runs that day closes it, never
an `OPERATING` one, and an `expected` closure closes nothing. A day it closes
carries `closedBy` (the season's id), which also marks it as a statement past
`scheduleCoverage.to`. The calendar's caches move to `calendar:month:v2`,
`calendar:resp:v2` and `best-days:v2`. `/predictions/yearly` does not read
closures yet
([calendar rules](architecture/calendar-schedule-and-ml-rules.md), [status for clients](frontend/calendar-schedule-status.md)).

### Fixed — the maze rule reads a ride's season bindings

`classifyHalloweenMazes` loaded the seasonality columns without the park
seasons that bind a ride, so a maze its park lists under Halloween counted as
not seasonal when the detector had never flagged it. The query now selects the
bindings (and pauses) as `Attraction.seasonBindings` does, `resolveCuratedFacts`
weighs them with the precedence of `attractionIsSeasonal` and
`attractionIsSeasonBound`, and a bound ride's season evidence is the months its
seasons' days fall in: a Halloween binding files a maze, a Christmas binding
does not ([maze rule](architecture/attraction-status/maze-rule.md)).

### Added — `/plan/ideas` leaves a metric row

`GET /v1/trip-regions/:region/plan/ideas` and Parky's ideas now write a
`trip_request_metrics` row with route `plan_ideas`: the trip's shape, the
answer's and the calendar's cache and the latency, no answer facts.
`trip_planner_ops` counts it like the other routes
([what the server records](frontend/trip-regions.md#what-the-server-records)).

### Fixed — `split=auto` and the stay tie read the parks the plan keeps

- **A park closed for the whole trip took a day of the room for second
  visits.** Paris, 12–14 March 2027 with a park on arrival and departure: Parc
  Astérix is closed on all three days, so Disneyland Park got no second visit
  and the arrival day stayed free. `extraVisitRoom` now counts only parks with
  an open day the trip can use; the plan is Disneyland Park, Disney Adventure
  World, Disneyland Park.
- **Two stay plans that tie went to the bases closer to every chosen park,
  dropped ones included.** The weekend of 14–16 May 2027 whose one park day is
  Parc Astérix slept at the Disney hotels, a long day trip, for the two Disney
  parks it left out. The tie now sums the reach of the parks a plan places
  (`keptReachMinutes`), and that weekend sleeps at Parc Astérix.

No Orlando plan or window of the identity fixture changed
([second visits](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-plan-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplan), [bases](frontend/trip-regions.md#bases-a-trip-across-several-cities)).

### Fixed — Parky calls a spare day a free day

The trip digest and the "what changed" lines Parky reads said "rest day" for a
`spare` day too. A day the rest rule set or the visitor pinned is still a rest
day; a day nobody asked for is a "free day", and Parky's plans carry
`spare: true` on it.

### Added — Parky offers an idea for the trip on screen

`get_trip_plan` and `change_trip` call `TripPlanService.ideas` with the query
they planned with and list up to three ideas for the trip on screen; `show_card`
kind `idea` sends one by its number as a card (`{ kind: "idea", region, idea }`),
which the planner applies with an undo. The prompt lets Parky offer one only when
it saves at least 5 minutes or places a park the plan leaves out, once an
answer, never one the visitor turned down. `SYSTEM_PROMPT_VERSION` is 57
([idea card](frontend/assistant.md#card-shapes)).

### Changed — one date helper per job in the trip planner and plan day

`minuteOfClock` reads `CLOCK_PATTERN`, `dayKindsOf` `isPlainWeekday`, the
ticket dry run walks its days with `eachIsoDate`, the park calendar takes
`round5` and `round1` from `trip-calendar.util`, `PlanDayService` uses
`addIsoDays`, `isoDaysBetween`, `isWeekendDate` and `isoDateWeekday`, and nine
`YYYY-MM-DD` checks read `ISO_DATE_PATTERN`. Behaviour-neutral.
