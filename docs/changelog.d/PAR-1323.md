### Added — the plan's ideas, short trips without a rest day in the middle, resort_left_out

- **`GET /v1/trip-regions/:region/plan/ideas` lists the plan's best single
  edits, each planned again.** The same query as `/plan`; at most three ideas,
  each a kind (`move`, `swap`, `add`, `travel_day`, `hopper`, `avoid_party`),
  the change as `/plan` parameters to apply as is (`arrival=park`,
  `lock=…,epcot@2026-12-09`, `hopper=walt-disney-world&hop=1`), and the park's
  `headlinerWait` on its day and the plan's `totals.headlinerWait` before and
  after. An idea places a chosen park the plan leaves out, adds a park on a
  spare day or saves at least 5 minutes over the visits both plans place; it
  never costs a visit or a pin. Every idea is `optimizePlan` on the changed
  query, on the calendar the plan reads, at most ten plans an answer, cached an
  hour apart from the plan (`trip-regions:plan-ideas:v1:*`), so `/plan` costs
  what it did. Orlando, 6–13 December with a Disney hotel: Hollywood Studios
  fits with a park on the arrival or departure day, or with the hopper, in
  45–60 ms; the heaviest trip measured, 21 days and eight parks, takes 2.2 s
  ([ideas](frontend/trip-regions.md#ideas-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplanideas)).
- **A trip of four days or fewer keeps its park days together.** Paris, 12–14
  March 2027, with a park on arrival and departure and two parks open, planned
  park, rest, park when the Saturday in the middle was the busier day; now two
  park days in a row and a free end day. The rest rule needs no rest day on a
  trip this short, so a day between two park days costs 400 minutes in the
  optimizer, more than days' waits differ and less than a repeat on the next
  day or a day without hours. No frozen Orlando plan or window changed
  ([rest days](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-plan-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplan)).
- **`resort_left_out` in `meta.warnings`** says that a resort the visitor chose
  has no park day at all for lack of days while another park has one. The
  Paris weekend of 14–16 May 2027 with a free arrival and departure day has one
  park day, and Parc Astérix's Saturday (40 minutes) beats Disneyland Park's (55)
  and Disney Adventure World's (65), so the plan is a day at Parc Astérix: the
  rule that drops the park whose best day is dearest, working as documented.
  The ideas price the way back: a park on the departure day places Disneyland
  Park.
- **Which park leaves no longer reads hours not yet published.** The drop
  order ranked a park's best day with the 1,000-minute penalty for a day
  without published hours, so where one resort publishes months ahead
  (Parc Astérix) and the other does not (Disneyland Paris), the other always
  dropped first. It now reads the day's waits; the penalty still decides which
  day of a park is used last.
