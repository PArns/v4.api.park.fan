### Fixed — the ML accuracy figures asked the calendar about the wrong day

The CLOSED-day gate on the system accuracy statistics compared
`DATE(pa.target_time)`, a UTC day, against the park-local
`schedule_entries.date`. Every park off UTC was therefore charged to its
neighbouring day in the hours at the edge of its own day, in both directions:
measured over 30 days of production, 334 rows landed on the wrong date — 270 at
Sesame Place Langhorne, where the 20:00–23:45 rows carry the next UTC date and
were counted against a shut day the park had been open on, and 64 at Six Flags
Qiddiya City, where the after-midnight rows carry the previous one and went the
other way. 190 of them crossed the gate, which is the subset whose actual wait
was under five minutes — exactly the rows the gate exists to judge. The day is
now read in the park's own timezone.

The rule itself no longer has two implementations. The correlated form moved
into `src/analytics/closed-park-days.sql.ts` beside the CTE pair the three
aggregate queries in `park-historical-stats.service.ts` already share, so both
shapes of the rule live in one file. The published MAE does not move: 10.1
before and after, over the same 30 days.
