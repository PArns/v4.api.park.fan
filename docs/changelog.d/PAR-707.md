### Fixed — the ML accuracy figures asked the calendar about the wrong day

The CLOSED-day gate on the system accuracy statistics compared
`DATE(pa.target_time)`, a UTC day, against the park-local
`schedule_entries.date`. Every park off UTC was therefore asked about its
neighbouring date in the hours at the edge of its own day, in both directions:
measured over 30 days of production, 334 rows landed on the wrong date — 270 at
Sesame Place Langhorne, whose 20:00–23:45 rows carry the next UTC date and were
charged to a shut day on a date its own calendar calls OPERATING, and 64 at Six
Flags Qiddiya City, whose after-midnight rows carry the previous one and went
the other way. 190 of them crossed the gate, which is the subset whose actual
wait was under five minutes — exactly the rows the gate exists to judge. The day
is now read in the park's own timezone.

The day it reads is the park-local *calendar* day, which is the same
approximation the three aggregate queries have always made rather than a new
claim about the operating day; the two differ at both ends of the day and
PAR-722 tracks that separately for all four callers at once.

The rule itself no longer has two implementations. The correlated form moved
into `src/analytics/closed-park-days.sql.ts` beside the CTE pair the three
aggregate queries in `park-historical-stats.service.ts` already share, so both
shapes of the rule live in one file. The published MAE does not move: 10.1
before and after over 30 days, 9.6 over the served 7-day window.
