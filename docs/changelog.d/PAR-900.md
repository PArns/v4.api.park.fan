### Fixed — nightly prediction cleanup no longer unpacks compressed chunks

`predictions/cleanup-old` failed every night from 2026-10-10 with a duplicate-key error: its daily step deleted on `predictedTime`, which is not the partition key, so TimescaleDB decompressed the compressed chunks to delete from them and hit duplicate keys two July chunks had been holding. The daily delete now runs only on chunks after the newest compressed one (the 90-day chunk drop covers the rest), with that bound inlined so the compressed chunks are not in the plan at all — on production 3 of 14 chunks instead of 14. The hourly purge and the daily delete also run independently now: one failing is logged, the other still runs, and the job fails afterwards naming both. New e2e `daily-prediction-cleanup.e2e-spec.ts` compresses a chunk and shows it stays unopened; without the bound the same spec deletes the compressed row.
