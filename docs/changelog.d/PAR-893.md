### Fixed — no Bull job runs in an E2E suite unless its spec asks for one

With `SKIP_QUEUE_BOOTSTRAP=false`, the jobs the boot chains queue (children
metadata, P50 baselines) ran in the test app while `setup-e2e.ts` truncated every
table between two tests. They lost with a deadlock (40P01) or wrote a park
against a destination the cleanup had just removed (23503). The E2E setup now
keeps every queue paused, the way Bull's own global pause does, before the app
boots and again after each Redis flush. The one spec that needs a worker, the
Search Console import, resumes its queue, and the cleanup waits for its job
before truncating. Turning off only the scheduler's repeat entries in tests would
have left both errors, since neither job comes from a cron, and waiting for the
jobs is no option: the children metadata sync runs for minutes.
