### Fixed — the children sync moves a row to the sister park that lists it

ThemeParks.wiki moves entities between the parks of a resort and keeps their
id. `attractions.externalId` is unique globally, so the park that now lists an
attraction could never insert its row (a nightly skip since PAR-714), while
shows and restaurants were found globally and kept updating the old park's
row. Measured on 2026-10-25 against all 198 parks' `/children`: 0 of 11,864
entities are listed under two parks, and **74 listed children have their row
in another park of the same resort** — 49 attractions, 17 shows, 8 restaurants
in 7 resorts (WildWater Adventure 16, Rulantica 15 shows, Oceans of Fun 14,
Cedar Point Shores 8 restaurants + 2, Hurricane Harbor 8, Superior Shores 7,
Knott's Soak City 2, Traumatica 2 shows).

After each run, the sync now moves such a row to the park that lists it: same
`destinationId` only, and only when the row's current park's `/children` from
that same run does not list the id (a park whose fetch failed moves nothing).
The row takes a free slug in its new park (`Rulantica Vibes` already exists in
Rulantica and becomes `rulantica-vibes-2`); an attraction is stamped listed,
its denormalised park columns (ride profile, baselines, typical waits,
headliner, schedule rows …) follow it, and its old path is kept as an
`attraction_slug_aliases` row that `resolveSlugAlias` now also answers under a
sister park's path. `queue_data_aggregates` keeps the park a reading was taken
under. Shows and restaurants have no alias table; their old path stops
answering. Each move is logged at INFO (`🚚 Moved …`) with row, entity and both
park ids.

**The first run after deploy moves these 74 rows**, and with them their
history: Michigan's Adventure hands 16 rides to WildWater Adventure, Worlds of
Fun 14 to Oceans of Fun, so crowd levels and percentiles of both parks change
retroactively. To revert one move: set the row's `parkId` (and the follower
tables' park column) back, restore the slug, and delete the
`attraction_slug_aliases` row the move wrote — and note the next run will move
it again unless the feed changed.
