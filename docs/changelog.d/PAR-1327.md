### Fixed — TFT trains every series with its own country and region again

Since PAR-1220 the fit leaves out series with fewer than two observed days, but the static frame
(country and region codes) was still built for the whole panel and handed to `nf.fit` as it was.
NeuralForecast pairs static rows with series by position after sorting by id and never compares
the ids, so every series sorted after a dropped one trained with another ride's codes, while
`nf.predict` (full panel) read the right ones. It hit the served run and both grid shadows in
every chunk holding such a series — the Magic Kingdom / Epic Universe / Hersheypark chunk had ten
on 2026-11-02. `forecast.fit_static` now cuts the static frame to the fit frame's series; the
codes stay the full panel's, so fit and predict agree on what a code means
([TFT](ml/tft.md)). The size on served numbers is not measured; static covariates were measured
inside training noise.
