### Changed — the day optimizer files starts on five minutes and counts the walk

`/plan/optimize` rounded every start up to the quarter hour, held every queue for at least fifteen
minutes and let a walk cost nothing. A packed headliner day lost about seven minutes per stop to the
grid, and the search crossed the park for a queue a minute shorter: Phantasialand on 2026-11-21 put
Winja's Fear at 11:45 and Winja's Force, the same station, at 16:30, with 1.8 km on foot.

Starts now sit on a five-minute step (`START_STEP_MIN`), a stop occupies its expected queue with no
minimum, and the cost is `wait + 0.5 × idle + 0.2 × walk` (`WALK_WEIGHT`). The weight stays under
park.fan's rule that waiting outweighs walking: a queue five minutes shorter still beats a kilometre
more on foot. The floors stay on the quarter hour. `before`, `after` and `plan` report `walkMinutes`,
and Parky's day check names the walking a press would save.

Over 23 park-days (six parks, four dates, every headliner) the time between the end of one queue and
the start of the next fell from 23.7 to 15.7 minutes, walking by 23 %, and the last queue is left
about an hour earlier, for 1 % less queueing. The Phantasialand day walks 1.0 km and both Winja's
follow each other. The vector fixtures that assumed the quarter-hour step were resized and
`browser-parity.spec.ts` re-records its answers with `RECORD_PARITY=1`; see
[`/plan/optimize`](frontend/plan-day/optimize.md#the-schedule).
