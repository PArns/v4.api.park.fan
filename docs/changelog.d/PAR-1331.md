### Added — the H5 composer can be served up to a chosen lead

`PLAN_DAY_H5_COMPOSER` switched H5 on for every lead to day 90 at once, so the whole decision
waits for the longest lead (2026-12-22) although the shorter ones are decided: on 2026-11-14 the
model comparison held H5 for days 2–5 at −1.46 min against the served planner (CI −2.04 to −0.91,
28 days, 137 parks), with the offline check and the live check both passed, and −1.79 min over
days 6–21. `PLAN_DAY_H5_MAX_LEAD_DAYS` caps the lead H5 is served at; past it the old composer
serves, and the forward-archive shadow keeps its 90 days so the longer leads go on being scored.
Unset, nothing changes ([slots and H5](frontend/plan-day/slots-and-h5.md)).
