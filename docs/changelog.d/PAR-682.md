### Fixed — a seasonal maze is no longer retired between seasons, and a re-issued wiki id no longer grows a `-2` twin

The absence step from PAR-621 retired 186 rows on its first run, 141 of them
seasonal — mostly Halloween mazes that are missing from ThemeParks.wiki's
`/children` until their next season. It now leaves a seasonal row alone
(`curated_is_seasonal` wins over the detector, `false` included), unless a row
of the same run already carries its name.

The wiki also hands seasonal attractions a new entity id every season, and the
rename guard in `findExistingAttraction` turned each into a second row with a
`-2` slug and no history — 16 such pairs on 2026-10-03. A row whose own wiki id
is no longer listed in the park's `/children` may now be matched by name, and
moves onto the new id, keeping its history and its slug.
