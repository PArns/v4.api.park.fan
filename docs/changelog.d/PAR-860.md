### Fixed — Admin model comparison: the day view opens on a real day, on that day's operating hours

The day comparison (PAR-855) opened on whichever park sorted first on the
newest archive date. On 2026-10-11 that was Adventureland Resort / Draken
Falls: a day not yet over in America/Chicago, with no OPERATING reading and no
published hours, laid out on the served curve's 06:00–03:00 span.

- `GET /v1/admin/model-comparison/day/options`
  - `dates` now lists only complete days, meaning the day is over in every
    park's timezone.
  - `parks` are ordered by data richness, the number of rides with truth in at
    least half of the operating window.
  - `rides` are listed headliners first, then by truth coverage. A ride with no
    OPERATING truth that day is left out.
  - The new `suggested: { date, parkId, attractionId } | null` names the ride
    to open first: a well-covered ride with curves from the most models. For
    2026-10-10 on production that is Alton Towers / Spinball Whizzer
    (headliner, 99 % coverage, 5 surfaces).
- `GET /v1/admin/model-comparison/day`
  - `slots` cover the operating window only. That is the published hours,
    else the truth span ± 1 h, else the span where the served curve has values.
  - The new `truthCoverage` gives the share of slots with a measurement.
- `offline-model-verdicts.json`
  - `live_h0_1` × Chronos-2 is `pending` instead of `worse`. Against
    persistence it is worse only at 15 min (+0.26) and better at 30 min (−0.42)
    and 60 min (−1.26), all significant.
  - `live_h0_1` × persistence is `unclear` instead of `better`. The served PCN
    is blended to that same value in the first hour, and no run compares the two.
  - `plan_d6_60` × TFT×H5 is `unclear` instead of `worse`. The +0.42 it cited
    was measured against the weekday average, not against what is served.
  - The `plan_d0_1` × TFT×H5 `diff` is removed. It was measured against the
    weekday average and in the headline window only.
  - Eleven notes are corrected. They had quoted the headline window as the full
    period, or a lead that lies outside the question.
  - See docs/ml/forward-archive/model-comparison.md (cells and offline verdicts).
