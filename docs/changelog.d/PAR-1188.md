### Fixed — Parky gives every time in the visitor's clock

The chat request carries the browser's time zone. `localizeTimes` rewrites every instant in a tool result into it before the model reads it, so Parky no longer does that arithmetic itself; it said a park closing at 18:00 closed at 17:00. The ride forecast keeps only the hours the park is open and, today, those not over yet. A new `show_card` kind `rides` lists a park's rides with their waits for the site's wait chips. A new tool, `look_up_glossary`, reads the glossary. The system prompt gives Parky a personality and nerd facts, and drops the narration of sources, checks and limits ([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).
