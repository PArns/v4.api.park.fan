### Added — Parky compares several parks on one day

"Where in Germany is it quietest on 28 June?" needed one forecast lookup per park, and Parky has at
most four rounds of tools per answer, so he picked three parks and answered from those. Asked
"where are the waits short right now?" without a park, he had nothing to compare at all.

- `compare_parks` takes a `country` (with `city` to narrow it, at most 30 parks) or 2 to 10
  `parks`, and a `date`, today by default and up to 60 days ahead.
- Each park's day comes from the calendar every park page shows, so all of them are rated by the
  same typical-day-peak level. Open parks come quietest first, a tie broken by the headliners'
  expected peak; open parks without a level, closed parks and parks whose calendar failed are
  listed apart.
- The system prompt (version 31) sends a question about several parks on one day there. The eval
  has a case for the quietest park in a country on Saturday.
