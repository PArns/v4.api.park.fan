### Added — Parky finds what is near a ride or the visitor inside a park, and shows the way

`find_restaurants` and the restaurants card take `near`, a ride, show or restaurant of the park,
and list the nearest first with metres and walking minutes; `find_nearby` lists the nearest rides
with their waits, shows, restaurants, toilets and drinking water; `get_walking_distance` measures
between places. With `fromMe` all of them measure from the position the visitor shared with the
message, which Parky asks for first, saying what for. The API now rounds that position to about ten
metres instead of a kilometre, for that one answer, and still stores, logs and forwards it nowhere;
the model sees distances only. `show_card` kind `navigate` draws the way to one place: an arrow the
visitor's browser points from its own position, and a walking route in Google Maps. Toilets and
drinking water come from OpenStreetMap, and an answer that names one carries its credit. Walking
minutes are the day planner's (a detour of 1.6 at 67 m a minute).

On 26 November a visitor asked for the restaurant under Talocan at Phantasialand, and Parky said it
did not exist: the name search found nothing, though Restaurante Cocorico stands 20 m from the
ride. `get_ride` now says `park_closed` with the next opening day while the park itself is shut,
instead of a reopening nobody knows. Prompt version 67 routes the tools, drops the reply chips that
only announce typing ("I know the date") and checks for any word on an earlier answer. Every tool
Parky has, with the day it came, is now listed in [Parky's tools](frontend/assistant-tools.md).
