### Added — Parky finds food near a ride and knows the walks inside a park

`find_restaurants` and the restaurants card take `near`, a ride, show or restaurant of the park, and
list the nearest first with metres and walking minutes; the new tool `get_walking_distance` measures
from one place to up to eight others. Walking minutes are the day planner's (a detour of 1.6 at
67 m a minute). On 26 November a visitor asked for the restaurant under Talocan at Phantasialand,
and Parky said it did not exist: the name search found nothing, though Restaurante Cocorico stands
20 m from the ride. `get_ride` now says `park_closed` with the next opening day while the park
itself is shut, instead of a reopening nobody knows. Prompt version 67 routes both tools, drops the
reply chips that only announce typing ("I know the date") and checks for any word on an earlier
answer ([assistant](frontend/assistant.md#places-in-a-park)).
