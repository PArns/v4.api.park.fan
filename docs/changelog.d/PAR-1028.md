### Fixed — A calendar day without a headliner forecast reads "unknown", not "moderate"

The calendar's crowd level for a day with no measurement (past) and no headliner
forecast (today and future) fell back to the first prediction of any ride for
that date, and then to a placeholder `moderate`. Within 60 days that first row
was usually a TFT row, whose `crowdLevel` was a hard-coded `moderate`; otherwise
it was one CatBoost ride's ratio-vs-P50 level, a live-regime value on a
typical-day-peak surface. Such a day now reads `unknown` ("no forecast") for
both `crowdLevel` and `peakLoad`. TFT rows carry `crowdLevel: "unknown"`
instead of the placeholder, and `/predictions/yearly` no longer rates a day
whose rows hold no predicted wait as `moderate`. Days with a headliner
forecast, measured past days and today's live reading are unchanged. The
forward archive's `crowdLevelFallback` flag keeps its meaning; its `crowdLevel`
on those days is now `unknown`.
