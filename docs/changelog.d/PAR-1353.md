### Added — automated visits are marked and left out of the reports, like the data centres

A visit whose first page view comes without a referrer, on a page that is not
English, from a browser whose `Accept-Language` names no language of that page,
is now `automated`: `network` reads `automated` on every row of the visit, its
readers count under the visitor scopes `aca` and `a<lang>`, and every report,
the live view and the MCP tools leave it out unless asked with
`automated=include` or `automated=only`. A data centre's visit stays
`datacenter`, and the most-read rankings never count an automated read.

The rule comes from the traffic of 2 to 15 November 2026. Direct visits from
Chrome with an English browser language came to 5,200 on the Spanish, German,
Dutch, French and Italian pages, 99 to 100 % bounces of 0 to 3 seconds, and
made up 63 % of the drop in visits from the first week of November to the
second. Direct visits from Safari almost never had a browser language the page
was not in (9 in those two weeks). The collector reads the open visit's start
beside the caps, in the same round trip, so the mark is known before the
visitor keys are written. Rows written before the deploy keep their network.
Programs on English pages are not caught: 1,205 such visits in the same two
weeks stay counted ([automated visits](frontend/site-analytics.md#automated-visits)).
