### Fixed — the global realtime board reads the gated park level and counts only the live catalogue

`/v1/analytics/realtime` rated its most and least crowded park with
`determineCrowdLevel(occupancy.current)`, the re-derivation the crowd-level rule forbids, so a park
without enough history read `very_low` where its own page says `unknown`; it now serves the level
`calculateParkOccupancy` already gated. `counts.parks`, `attractions`, `shows` and `restaurants`
left retired rows in, and `counts.openAttractions` counted rides of closed parks whose last
`OPERATING` reading was still inside the 24 h window; both are fixed. The ticker's Swagger enum for
`crowdLevel` is `CROWD_LEVEL_VALUES` now, so `unknown` is in the published contract
([analytics contract](frontend/analytics.md)).
