### Added — park duplicate detection pairs two rows that list the same rides

`ParkValidatorService.findDuplicates` has a sixth branch, `attractionsAgree`:
two rows with no source in common are reported when at least five attraction
names agree and they make up at least 0.8 of the shorter list. It asks neither
the name nor the location, because a Queue-Times-only row takes its coordinate
from the feed and its city from that coordinate, so a row filed on the wrong
point had nothing left that could contradict it. Three such rows were
undetected duplicates for about a year (PAR-245, PAR-309): against the feeds as
they stood before those merges, the branch finds all three at 10 of 10, 17 of
17 and 20 of 20 shared names, 85.7 km, 1,205 km and 2,195 km from the real park.

The source condition is load-bearing. Fantawild's water parks share up to 15 of
16 ride names, and every Fantawild row comes from ThemeParks.wiki alone; among
the catalogue pairs that are not both wiki rows, the highest share is 0.125
(211 parks, 22,155 pairs, measured 2026-10-02).

Such a pair is never `safe`, so no merge path acts on it. It is listed by
`GET /v1/admin/duplicate-parks` with a `reviewReason` that gives the distance
and, above 10 km, says to correct the location before merging. `findDuplicates`
logs the count after every park metadata sync.
