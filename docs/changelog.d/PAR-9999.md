### Added — park facilities from OpenStreetMap, as a draft behind `FACILITIES_ENABLED`

Toilets, drinking water, baby care, first aid, lockers, ATMs and defibrillators inside a
park's OpenStreetMap outline ([facilities](frontend/facilities.md)). An editor imports one
park at a time (`POST /v1/admin/facilities/{parkId}/import`), looks at what came in and
approves it; `GET /v1/parks/{…}/{park}/facilities` then serves the points with the
OpenStreetMap credit and the ODbL link in the payload. Nothing is imported on a schedule, an
unapproved park serves no row, and the public route answers 404 until `FACILITIES_ENABLED` is
`true` (default `false`). A re-import is idempotent, deletes what vanished from OSM, keeps a
hidden point hidden, and puts an approved park back to review when it brings a point nobody
has seen. Two new tables, `park_facilities_osm` (OSM-derived rows only, which keeps the ODbL
on that table and off the rest) and `park_facility_imports`.
