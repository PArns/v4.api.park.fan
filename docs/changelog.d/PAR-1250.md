### Changed — Parky stays in a language the site lacks and answers for his own favourites

Prompt version 26 closes four gaps the chats of 6 November showed. Asked for the site in Chinese,
Parky had answered in English, apologised and listed the six languages; he now chats in the language
asked for and says once that the browser can translate the pages. Asked about the favourites the
bubble on the page had mentioned ("I have a few myself"), he had read out the visitor's saved ones
and wondered where the line came from; he now answers for his own, in character. One question had
drawn three ride cards and no sentence: an answer now has one card, two at most, and never cards
alone, and "something like" a ride means that ride's type. A sexual remark had drawn a double
meaning; it now gets a plain line back to parks. The prompt eval has three new cases for the first
three. See [Parky](frontend/assistant.md).
