### Changed — hourly forecast `confidence` is `null` more than 24 h ahead

`hourlyForecast[].confidence` on the attraction response, and `confidenceAdjusted` where a deviation halves it, are now `null` for a slot more than 24 h after the moment of the response. Up to and including 24 h the value is unchanged, and `daily` is untouched. The distance term of `predict.py` reaches its floor of 50 at 22.5 h, so 60.2 % of 453,076 served values sat at 50 or below while the measured error rose only 4.9 % over the first 24 h (PAR-449). Beyond 24 h the error is not measured (PAR-658). The lead is counted from delivery, not from `prediction_time`, by `servedHourlyConfidence()` in `src/ml/utils/hourly-confidence.util.ts`; stored rows keep the model's value. `PredictionDto.confidence` and the Swagger description say `number | null`.
