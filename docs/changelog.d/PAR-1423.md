### Fixed — Park lists no longer open a park on a day its schedule marks CLOSED

When the window from yesterday to tomorrow held only CLOSED days, `isParkOpen` took the park for one without a schedule and judged it by its rides. A feed that kept rides OPERATING opened the park in the lists (Movie Park Germany with 19 rides at 5 minutes, Parque de Atracciones de Madrid with 16 rides at 60 minutes, 2026-11-24) while the park page and `/discovery/nearby` said CLOSED. A day published as CLOSED now counts as a schedule, through the same `isDecisiveScheduleRow` the SQL behind nearby and the open-park counts uses, so all surfaces agree. Parks that publish no schedule at all keep the ride fallback.
