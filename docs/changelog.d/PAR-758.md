### Fixed — a feed reading after our own row is always written

`isSignificantChange` decides each feed reading against the ride's newest row,
and that row could be our own: a carried heartbeat, or the `CLOSED` reverse
reconciliation writes for a ride no source has mentioned in 24 hours. A ride
back in the feed with the value that row already held was "no change", so it
stayed on our row until the 60-minute rule fired — and a ride on a
reconciliation row reads `UNKNOWN` for that hour. Measured against production
for the week to 2026-10-07: 74 returns to the feed waited 55–75 minutes on a
reconciliation row for their first reading.

A feed reading whose predecessor is our own row is now always written. Only in
that direction: the reconciliation still compares against its own `CLOSED` and
does not rewrite it on every tick. The PAR-162 note in
`source-absent-status.util.ts`, which argued a heartbeat cannot be told from a
steady ride, is corrected for PAR-720: a heartbeat now means no feed for at
least 10 minutes.
