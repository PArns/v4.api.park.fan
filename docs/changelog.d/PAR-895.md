### Added — morning summary push for the day's plan (`morning-plan`)

`PUSH_TOPICS` gains `morning-plan`. A subscriber with a stored plan gets one message per park day from 60 minutes before the park opens (not before 07:00 park-local), naming the first three plan entries with their times in the subscriber's language. The opening time is the earliest `OPERATING` schedule entry of the day; without one nothing is sent. `notification-planner.spec.ts` and `push-notification.processor.spec.ts` cover the window, the 07:00 floor, quiet hours and the second tick sending nothing.
