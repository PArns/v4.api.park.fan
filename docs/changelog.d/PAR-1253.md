### Added — Parky knows a ride's maintenance windows on any day, and plans around them

Parky knew a maintenance or works window only while it ran on the park's today: `get_ride` said
`reopening: works`, and the ride card in the chat wore the badge. Asked whether Droomvlucht runs on
8 January, or to plan it for that day, he had nothing to go on. `get_park_day` dropped the ride
from `rides` without a word, `get_ride_forecast` answered with empty hours, and `plan_add_rides`
reported the ride as added although the planner on park.fan refuses it.

- `worksPeriodOn` (`curated-out-of-service.util.ts`) names the window covering a park-local day.
- `PlanDayService.ridesInMaintenance` lists the rides a window shuts on a day the plan is asked
  about, by name. Nothing for a day already over, like the plan's own filter.
- `get_park_day` returns them as `ridesInMaintenance`, each with its window. A failed lookup costs
  the list, never the day.
- `get_ride_forecast` says `closedForMaintenance` with the window on such a day.
- `plan_add_rides` leaves such a ride out and returns it in `notAdded` with its window. With none
  left it sends no planner action.
- `get_ride`'s description names `worksPeriods`, which it already carried.
- The system prompt (version 27) has Parky call a window maintenance unless a post says rebuild,
  name until when, hedge an estimated end, and never leave a ride in maintenance out of an answer
  without saying why. A ride in maintenance stays findable; it only cannot be planned that day.
