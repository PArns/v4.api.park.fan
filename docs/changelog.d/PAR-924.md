### Fixed — mapping job matches a ride on its curated name as well as the wiki name

`EntityMappingsProcessor` matched ThemeParks.wiki rides to Queue-Times and
Wartezeiten rides on the wiki's feed name alone. The wiki names Movie Park's
Hell House "Helhuis" and The Slaughterhouse "Het slachthuis"; against the
operator's names in the other two feeds they scored 0.43 and 0.36 under the
0.75 name-only threshold, so neither got a mapping and `WaitTimesProcessor`
dropped every reading of `qt-ride-11118` and `qt-ride-11121`. Neither ride had
a wait time in 90 days, while the other six mazes with a name match had 11 or
12 days each.

`EntityMetadata` now takes `altNames`. The processor offers each wiki
attraction's `curated_name` as a second name and the matcher scores the best of
the names. A curated name is the name the operator uses, so it needs no entry
per ride.
