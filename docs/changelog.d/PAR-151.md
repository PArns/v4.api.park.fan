### Documented — a show merge keeps the winner's reminder when both rows follow the same show

`SHOW_DEPENDENCIES` dedupes `show_follows` on `subscriptionId`, so a subscriber who followed the same show from two park pages keeps the surviving show's row, whether it is the bell (`startTime` null) or a showtime badge. The docblock now records why that stays: keeping the row with a `startTime`, or the nearer one, needs a hand-written statement outside `MergeStrategy` and matches the intended reminder in about half the cases. No behaviour change.
