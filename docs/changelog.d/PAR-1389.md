### Added — the calendar warmup and a TFT training start from the admin

`POST /v1/admin/warmup-calendar` and `POST /v1/admin/train-nf` (editor), and the admin MCP's
`run_job` jobs `warmup-calendar` and `train-nf`, queue the jobs their crons queue. The
[deploy checklist](deployment/coolify.md#a-deploy-that-changes-a-served-forecast) runs both after a
deploy that changes served forecasts or a model input's meaning, which until now meant adding the
job by hand in Bull Board. The warmup route answers with the warmup already running or waiting
instead of starting a second, because two at once saturate the database's disk; a TFT training
skips itself while one is in flight, as before. [Endpoints](admin/endpoints.md#models-and-forecasts).
