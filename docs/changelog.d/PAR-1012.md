### Fixed — /stats and /stats/hourly answer a non-integer parameter with 400, not 500

`years`, `topN`, `minSampleDays` and `minAttractionDays` were read with
`Number(x) || default`, so `topN=1.5` or `minAttractionDays=Infinity` went to
Postgres as a `LIMIT` or `HAVING` parameter and came back as 500. Both routes now
read them through `QueryInt`: a fraction, `Infinity` or text is a 400 without
cache headers, an empty value reads the default. `years` and `minSampleDays` no
longer reach the cache key unrounded. The defaults and ranges are unchanged; `0`
is now clamped to the range instead of replaced by the default, so
`minAttractionDays=0` lists every ride.
