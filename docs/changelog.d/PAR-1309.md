### Changed — trip regions: a plan names the parks it leaves out instead of refusing them

A trip with more chosen parks than park days used to end in a refusal or in
a plan that dropped parks without a word: Orlando's seven default parks in a
7-day trip planned only five, and the client refused the trip outright.
`/plan` now answers `parksLeftOut`, the chosen parks with no visit in the
plan, each with its reason (`no_day_left` where the trip's park days went
to the other parks), and every `/when` window's `plan` carries the same
field, so a suggestion can say "EPCOT and Epic Universe don't fit into 5
park days" ([the plan](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-plan-get-v1trip-regionsorlandoplan)).
A park listed without a count, or with one, is never a 400 any more: the
plan fills the park days the trip has. Only pinned visits (counts of two or
three) that add up to more than the trip's days are refused, with the same
message as before. The plan and `/when` cache keys move to v2. `/when`'s
`after_holidays` now needs a break of at least five days: a trip from
23 January 2027 no longer reads "right after the holidays" because of
Martin Luther King Jr. Day's long weekend
([when to go](frontend/trip-regions-when.md)).
