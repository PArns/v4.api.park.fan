### Fixed — one over-long reply chip no longer drops all of an answer's chips

`suggest_replies` refused the whole call when one reply ran past 40 characters, so the answer
showed no chips at all: „Mantengamos el 24 de octubre al 2 de noviembre" (46) took the two short
ones with it in a Spanish Orlando chat on 15 November, the week's one `invalid_input`. The schema
now takes up to 200 characters and the tool drops a reply over `REPLY_MAX_CHARS` (40, the widget's
own limit) like a repeat or a URL. Contract: [reply chips](frontend/assistant.md#reply-chips).

### Changed — Parky keeps the chat's language, confirms without changing, and names only marked holidays

Four prompt rules from the chats of 8 to 15 November (`SYSTEM_PROMPT_VERSION` 60):

- A message that is only a name or a single word keeps the language the chat already speaks. A
  German chat on an English page turned English on „Taron" and German again on the next question.
- A yes to a step the plan already holds changes nothing. Asked „Alle großen Parks" with all three
  Japan parks planned, Parky set each to one visit, took it back with `trip_undo` and told the
  visitor so.
- A change Parky took back himself in the same answer goes unmentioned.
- A day is a holiday, a bridge day or in a school break only when a tool result marks it. Parky
  called Thursday 12 November 2026 at Disneyland Paris a „jour de pont" after the Wednesday
  holiday, which the calendar never set, and repeated it when the visitor doubted it.

Three eval cases cover them (`interview-parks-confirmed-de`, `language-single-word-keeps-chat`,
`no-inferred-bridge-day-fr`); the eval has 96. Background:
[Parky](frontend/assistant.md#language-and-feedback-from-the-chat).

### Fixed — a ride without one measured day in the year gets no forecast, best times or rope drop

Lanzadera at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid has no reading in the past 365 days and is closed, yet
its detail answer offered „15 min, optimal" twice a day, and Parky's `get_ride` passed that on. The
models fill every ride from the calendar and the weather, so such a ride's forecast is the prior.
The ride detail (`AttractionIntegrationService`, which the site, the admin MCP and Parky read) now
leaves `hourlyForecast` empty and sets neither `bestVisitTimes` nor `ropeDrop` when its typical
waits count no measured day (`hasMeasuredDays` in `typical-waits.util.ts`). A failed typical-waits
read keeps them, as before.

### Changed — Parky no longer reads a park's occupancy percentage

`get_park_live` and the park card drop `occupancyPercent` from the crowd reading they hand Parky
(`parkNow`). It is `comparedToTypicalPercent` plus 100, and Parky read it out as „el parque va al
192 % de su ocupación normal" for Six Flags México on 8 November, a park filled past capacity to
anyone reading it. `comparedToTypicalPercent` stays and reads as meant („rund 16 % über dem
typischen Wert" for Europa-Park on 15 November).
