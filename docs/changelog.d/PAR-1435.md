### Added — the app sends itself a sample of every notification kind

`POST /v1/push/devices/test { installId, kind, locale? }` sends the calling
install, and only it, a real APNs notification of one kind (`ride-wait`,
`ride-reopen`, `next-up`, `plan-change`, `rain-soon`, `morning-plan`, `news`,
`guides`), written by that kind's own writer with a sample visit to
Phantasialand, or the newest real post for news and guides. It skips the
device's choice of kinds, quiet hours and cool-downs but keeps Apple's
bookkeeping, marks itself with `test: "true"` and test ids for the action
buttons, and answers what Apple said. 20 an hour per install, 60 per address;
`PUSH_SELF_TEST=false` turns it off. For the app's hidden "Push-Tests"
([push](frontend/push.md#a-devices-self-test-post-v1pushdevicestest)).
