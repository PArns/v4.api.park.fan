### Fixed — the fit assistant keeps a pinned ride, and unticking a ride never makes the rest fit worse

`POST /plan/optimize` with `mode: fit` dropped a pinned ride whenever losing it let one more other
ride in: the visitor's order only ranked _which_ ride fell out after the count was settled. A pin is
now its own overflow tier above the headliners, and a pin the day has no minute for is named in
the new `fit.pinnedMissed[]` (optional for clients; empty without a pin). Unticking rides could
also come out worse than the whole list's plan with them taken out (on Phantasialand's 27 November,
29 of 630 one-or-two-ride unticks, one of them with a ride fewer fitted), because the beam search is
not monotone in its input; the engine now also plans the whole list and starts from that order.
Three recorded parity answers with an untick moved, each to the same rides with less idle or an
earlier end. See [the optimizer](frontend/plan-day/optimize.md#fit).
