### Added — Year rankings of rides by wait, per country and continent

`GET /v1/rankings/attractions/:continent[/:country]?year=` ranks every ride
with at least 60 measured days in the calendar year (more than the 20 of a
park's `topAttractions`, so Halloween mazes with 25 event nights stay out) by the average of its
hourly P90 waits, across all parks of the country or continent
([attraction rankings](frontend/attraction-rankings.md)). Each park contributes
its rows through the query behind its own `topAttractions`
(`ParkHistoricalStatsService.getYearAttractionStats`), so a ride's order within
its park matches the park's statistics page. A running year ends yesterday in
each park's time zone. Rows are cached per park and year under the park's
historical-stats key, the merged ranking per scope and year, both for 24 hours.
Behind `stats.history`.

Parky reads the ranking through a new tool, `get_wait_ranking`: a park's or a
named country's rides of one year (default the running one, the previous one in
January and February), with the place of one asked ride even far down the list
and a link to the ranking page ([assistant tools](frontend/assistant-tools.md)).

The tool links a country's ranking under its country page,
`/<locale>/parks/<continent>/<country>/longest-wait-times/<year>`, where park.fan
moved the rankings, as a park's sub-pages sit under the park.
