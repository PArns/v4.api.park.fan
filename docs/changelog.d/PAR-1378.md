### Fixed — `/plan/day` draws a season day at the season level the calendar shows

On a day of a running park season the served daily row carries the season level
([season level](ml/season-level.md), PAR-1343) and reads `tft+season` or `catboost+season`.
`/plan/day`'s H5 composer tested `modelVersion === "tft"`, so it never saw that level: the curve was
the plain profile of the ride's last 56 days, nearly all of them before the season, while `dayPeak`
and the calendar beside it showed the season. A visitor got two answers for one park-day.

The composer now routes by the row's source (`servedLevelSource`). A season level scales every
ride's profile, at every lead H5 is served, by the ratio to the ride's 56-day reference, the
rope-drop hour unscaled (`composer: "h5_season"`). The TFT gates, busy rides and leads 0–2, stay
TFT's: they were measured on TFT's forecast error, and the season level is what the ride measured
on this season's days of the same day type. Why, and what is still unmeasured, is in
[slots and H5](frontend/plan-day/slots-and-h5.md#on-a-day-of-a-running-park-season). The forward
archive codes those slots `e` (`composed_h5_season`) and counts them under `level_season`. The routed
shadow keeps TFT's routing only, so the `tfth5` column of `planner_h5_slots` never scores the
season level. No other reader made the same mistake: the lead snapshots and the forward archive
already read `servedLevelSource`, and the archive's `=== "tft"` checks pair the TFT and CatBoost
candidates on TFT's days on purpose.

H5 types its days by the country's weekend (`isCountryWeekendDate`, from
`getWeekendDaysForCountry`) instead of Saturday and Sunday everywhere, in the profile and on the
planned day; in Saudi Arabia a Friday is a weekend day and a Sunday a working day. The profile
cache moves to `plan-day:h5:v3:<parkId>:<date>`. Holiday typing and the fallback to all days for
a thin day type stay as the benchmark has them until `planner_h5_slots` is judged on 2026-12-22,
and change after that only through an ml-bench run
([known limits](frontend/plan-day/slots-and-h5.md#known-limits)).
