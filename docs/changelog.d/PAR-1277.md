### Changed — Parky gives every time in park time

Every time in a tool result used to be rewritten into the visitor's browser clock, and the cards
showed the reader's clock too. Somebody in London planning a day in Orlando heard the park's hours
in London time, while the trip planner, the showtimes and the park pages show park time.

- Each tool call records the parks it resolves (`resolvePark` reports the zone through the MCP
  context's new `onPark`; `get_ride` and `get_ride_forecast` note their ride's park), and
  `localizeTimes` writes the result's instants in that park's zone. The result names it as
  `timesIn`. A call about several parks keeps each instant's own offset.
- `get_park_live` and the park card's summary keep the park's zone and local time, and the park
  card's hours carry the park's offset (`parkInstant`).
- The system prompt (version 40) has Parky state times as given and add "park time" once when the
  visitor's zone differs.

The widget's cards read the park's clock off that offset in PArns/park.fan (PAR-1277).
