### Added — trip regions: Paris

`/v1/trip-regions/paris` answers with two resorts: Disneyland Paris
(Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World, Walt Disney Studios Park renamed
on 29 March 2026) and Parc Astérix with its own hotels, all three parks
headliners ([trip regions](frontend/trip-regions.md#paris-paris)). A Disney
hotel stay gets Extra Magic Time (`extra_magic_time`) at both parks on the days
the feed carries the slot; Parc Astérix's hotels get the Early Entry their
stays include (`early_entry`), which counts once the feed carries a slot for
the park. The trip stays in up to three bases, Disneyland Paris, Parc Astérix
and central Paris, in any order, with Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy, CDG and Orly as
gateways ([bases](frontend/trip-regions.md#bases-a-trip-across-several-cities)).
A gateway on the region route names its `bases`; it pins `startBase` or
`endBase` only where one base lists it, so CDG, which serves all three, pins
nothing. Two stay plans with the same number of moves whose cost differs by
less than 3 minutes per park day (the plan's "any order will do") tie, and the
tie goes to the bases closer to the trip's parks, never to the one listed
first: a 4-day trip to Disneyland Park and Parc Astérix stays at the Disney
hotels instead of in central Paris. Japan's trade-off between a move and a long
day trip is unchanged. A stay's hotel perks count only for the area the visitor
names.
Disneyland Park resolves by its path, since Anaheim's has the same slug. No
storm season; heat counts from a 30 °C forecast or a 25 °C normal. Travel is
OSRM's drive with the walk between the Disney gates, RER A from central Paris
and Val d'Europe, and RER B with the park's shuttle to Parc Astérix curated.
The ticket catalog seeds 9 products, Parc Astérix's in its own group. The 30
researched seasons for 2026/27 are in
`scripts/trip-regions/seasons/paris-2026-27.json`, not imported yet.
