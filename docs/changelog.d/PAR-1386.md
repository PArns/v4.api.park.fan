### Fixed — last year's matching day is one the park was open on, and a park's weekend is its country's

The day a year back that a trip day and Parky's `get_park_history` read (`lastYearReferenceDay`,
PAR-1377) was never checked for the park having been open. Phantasialand's Wednesday 23 December
2026, a Christmas-break day it is open on, read Wednesday 24 December 2025, a break day it was shut
on: its winter opened on the 26th. Each step of the lookup now passes over a day the park was closed
or nothing was measured and takes the next day it allows, in the same order and within the same
reach (three weeks, 36 days for a holiday), so that day reads Wednesday 31 December 2025, a measured
55 minutes. Only a day with no open, measured day in reach reads 52 weeks back, and with it nothing.
The trip calendar tests the candidates on its last-year snapshots by the rule a trip day already
read last year by (`lastYearMeasured`), so it reads every past month a reference day can sit in
(`lastYearReferenceMonths`): four or five snapshots for a 31-day window instead of one or two, all
kept by the warmup. Parky reads the day it picks and picks again when the park was shut
(`lastYearReferenceDayReading`), one calendar day per read
([the matching day a year back](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-matching-day-a-year-back)).

`/plan/day`'s `context.isWeekend` was Saturday and Sunday for every park, while the H5 composer in
the same service typed the day by the country's weekend: on a Friday in Riyadh the context said no
weekend and the curve came from the weekend profile. It now reads `isCountryWeekendDate` as well
([`/plan/day`](frontend/plan-day/README.md)). The school breaks of `/school-breaks` and `/when?within=`
were widened over Saturday and Sunday in every country; they take the country's weekend, so an
Israeli break from Sunday to Thursday takes in the Friday and Saturday on either side. Nager's bridge
days are stored less the country's weekend instead of less Saturday and Sunday (`bridgeDaysOf`).
`isWeekendDate`, the Saturday-and-Sunday answer for every country, is removed.

Left as they are: the weekend bonus and the bridge-day rule of `holiday.utils` and
`HolidaysService.isEffectiveSchoolHoliday`, which mirror the holiday features CatBoost is trained
on; ml-bench and the shadow `driver_level` port, which mirror the code they score; the trip
regions' `weekday` reason and the tickets' `weekend` kind, Saturday and Sunday in all four regions'
countries and named so in the ticket contract.

**After the deploy, train CatBoost, TFT and PCN by hand.** PAR-1379 changed what CatBoost's
weekday/weekend rolling features and TFT's and PCN's `is_weekend` mean for parks in Saudi Arabia,
Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman and Israel, and PAR-1375 what CatBoost's `is_weekend` means for the
UAE. The deployed models learned the old meaning and keep serving it until their nightly training.
[Coolify](deployment/coolify.md#a-deploy-that-changes-what-a-model-input-means) now lists the step
for every such deploy: a `train-nf` job in Bull Board for TFT (it has no admin route), then
`POST /v1/admin/train-ml-model`, then `POST /v1/admin/pcn/train`, one after the other.

PAR-1375's entry said `getWeekendDaysForCountry` and `add_weekend_feature` still read Friday and
Saturday for the UAE; that change took the UAE out of both, and the entry now says so.
