### Added — weather=avoid: the trip plan and /when keep extreme weather off the trip

`/v1/trip-regions/:region/plan` and `/when` take `weather=avoid` ("Extremes Wetter vermeiden" in
the planner's Parks step). Every region's heat model gets a stronger variant from one rule
(`AVOID_WEATHER` in `regions.ts`): heat counts from 3 °C lower on a forecast and 1 °C lower on a
climate normal, each degree costs twice as much (16 %) and the cap is × 2.5 instead of × 1.4; the
storm season (Orlando's hurricanes, Japan's typhoons) and `/when`'s peak term count twice. Theme
park days move to cooler days where their waits allow, and water parks, which take no heat term,
take the hot ones. A plan day that stays hot still says `extreme_heat`, read against the stronger
model. Without the option nothing changes, the cache key included.

Calibrated on production's data with 2026's measured highs as the forecast: Orlando 2–15 August
moves 4 park days and lowers the visits' mean high from 34.1 to 32.7 °C for 3.6 minutes more
per park day; 17–30 May moves 6 for 1.4 minutes (34.5 to 33.6 °C); Southern California 1–12
September takes Magic Mountain off its 39 °C day (32.6 to 28.9 °C, 4 minutes). Past the forecast
the normals barely differ within a trip, and the option ranks `/when`'s windows instead: Orlando's
June and July windows leave the top eight. Parky's `plan_vacation`, `change_trip` and
`find_trip_dates` take `weather`, and the hub link carries it. The forecast's thunderstorm code,
rain and wind stay unpriced: in Orlando's summer the thunderstorm code is an ordinary afternoon
([avoiding extreme weather](frontend/trip-regions.md#avoiding-extreme-weather-weatheravoid)).
