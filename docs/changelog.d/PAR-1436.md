### Added — `/weather/hourly`: a park's days hour by hour, for a day curve

`GET /v1/parks/{continent}/{country}/{city}/{park}/weather/hourly` serves the park's today and up to
14 following days, each midnight to midnight on the park's clock: temperature, feels-like,
precipitation and its chance, WMO code, wind speed, gusts and direction and the daylight flag per
hour, sunrise and sunset per day, times with the park's offset (23 hours on a spring-forward day, 25
on a fall-back one). Before, the API had only daily rows and the 6-hour nowcast, so the website drew
its day curve from its own Open-Meteo relay and the app could draw none. One Open-Meteo request per
~1 km coordinate per 2 h (`weather:hourly-detail:{lat}:{lon}`) serves every park and request on it;
the days are cut at read time, and the response is cached until `nextUpdateAt` (the refetch or the
park's next midnight). [Contract](frontend/weather.md#the-hourly-days).
