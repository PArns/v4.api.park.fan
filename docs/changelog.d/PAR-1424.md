### Added — push to the iOS app over APNs, with news push

The iOS app registers as a device (`POST /v1/push/devices`: install id, APNs
token, locale, zone, the kinds it wants, an optional park narrowing for news) and
then uses the existing ride-alert and show-follow routes with the returned
`apns:<installId>` endpoint, so every existing trigger reaches it.
`PushService.send` goes to APNs for those rows and never sends a kind the device
switched off; a token Apple calls dead (410, `BadDeviceToken`,
`DeviceTokenNotForTopic`) deletes the device. A new `news` half of the push job
announces a new post of the news export to devices with news on. Without the
`APNS_*` variables nothing changes. Web push is untouched. See
[push](frontend/push.md#ios-app-apns-v1pushdevices).

### Added — push to the iOS app when a watched plan changes

A device can link a stored plan (`tripId` on `POST /v1/push/devices`) and
choose the new device-only kind `plan-change`: a fifth half of the push job
compares each plan day from today on with what the device last heard and sends
one digest when the day's hours move or it closes, a planned show moves or is
cancelled, a planned ride goes down or comes back (today), or a planned ride's
forecast wait moves by 15 minutes and 30 % (up to a week out). Paced to one
digest per 30 minutes per day, three hours for waits alone; quiet hours hold it
back. The linked device also gets `next-up` and `morning-plan` when chosen.
See [push](frontend/push.md#plan-change--something-a-stored-plan-was-built-around-changed).
