### Fixed — Orlando: the Disney Springs Resort Area hotels get Early Entry

A stay on Hotel Plaza Boulevard planned as off-site, without the Early Entry
Disney gives those hotels' guests. Orlando now has a hotel area and hotel kind
`disney_springs` for the Hilton Orlando Lake Buena Vista, Hilton Orlando Buena
Vista Palace, Drury Plaza, DoubleTree Suites, Holiday Inn Orlando, Renaissance
Orlando Resort and Spa (the former B Resort), Wyndham Lake Buena Vista and
Wyndham Garden, which Disney's own list names among the "other
select hotels" with Early Entry. They get Early Entry at the four Disney theme
parks and no Extended Evening Hours, which that list keeps for the Deluxe
hotels, the Swan, Dolphin, Swan Reserve and Shades of Green.
`lake_buena_vista` stays off-site, for the hotels along SR 535, and both areas
drive from the same point
([hotel areas](frontend/trip-regions.md)).
