### Added — Parky ignores junk and slows down rapid fire

A visitor posting nothing but junk, or firing question after question, spent
Anthropic tokens up to the session limits of 20 questions per ten minutes and
150 a day. A third window now allows 3 questions per 30 seconds, enough for a
quick "yes" to an offer Parky made. Junk never reaches the model
(`assistant-junk.ts`): no letter or digit in any script, one key held down,
six or more lower-case Latin letters without a vowel, or the question just
answered asked again within two minutes. It gets a 422 `unclear` at no token
cost, and the third in a row a ten-minute pause for the session (429 with
`Retry-After`). A real question in between resets the count. Details in
[the assistant page](frontend/assistant.md#limits-and-cost).
