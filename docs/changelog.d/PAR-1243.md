### Added — Parky knows when a ride opens and reads a whole park day

Asked at 09:58 when Taron opens, Parky said it was closed with no date known,
although Taron starts at 10:00, an hour after Phantasialand's gates. The time
was in `PlanDayService` all along; Parky never asked it. `get_ride` now carries
`today.opensAt` and `runsOnWeekdays` for every ride, and the new `get_park_day`
reads one day as the planner builds it: hours, crowd level, weather, holidays,
early entry, each ride's opening, peak and shortest hour, and the shows.

`show_card` kind rides now returns its rows to the model, which before saw only
"6 rides of X" and could not name a ride from the list it had just shown. The
ride filter knows dark rides and water rides besides coasters. The prompt points
"when should I ride X" at the intraday forecast's `bestVisitTimesToday`, writes
dates in words instead of `2026-11-05`, keeps chatting in a language the site
does not offer instead of turning it away, takes part in small talk without
steering back to waits, and now and then drops a bit of nerd knowledge.
