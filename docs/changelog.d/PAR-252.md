### Added — the live closure-gap denominator is guarded by its result, not by its text

`active_floor` and the upper bound of the `active` CTE in `CURRENT_CLOSURE_GAP_SQL`
were asserted only as SQL text: a regular expression in `closure-gap.sql.spec.ts`,
and five E2E cases that leave `attraction_exposure_days` empty so `active_days`
stays 0 and the duty-cycle arm passes unconditionally. Turning `LEAST` into
`GREATEST`, dropping the `FILTER`, reading `opens_at` instead of `closes_at` in
it, or making the upper bound inclusive left all 39 unit and all 5 E2E cases
green — each of those can only move the denominator, and a moved denominator
shows up as extra live rows rather than as an exception.

Three E2E cases now seed a real denominator and assert `gap_days / active_days`
against `MAX_GAP_DAY_SHARE`: 3 gap days against 6 exposure days is at the
threshold and keeps the line, against 5 it is over and suppresses it, and which
of the two a fixture lands on turns on one single operating day at one of the
bounds. Measured: the four mutations above now fail 3, 1, 2 and 1 of the cases.
