### Documented — the nightly duty-cycle denominator lags one run, and the calibration includes it

`CLOSURE_GAP_INTERVALS_SQL` runs before the reconstruction job rewrites `attraction_exposure_days`, so
its `active_days` denominator and `blind_parks` read the previous run's rows and miss the newest
operating day (~1 in 30, n after n missed runs). That pushes the gap share towards `MAX_GAP_DAY_SHARE`
in the conservative direction: a real fault is withheld, a timetable is never published as faults.
Both fixes cost more than the bias: the statement inside the write transaction holds the delete locks
for 30.4 s a night, and passing this run's exposure rows in means ~30 days of rows per tracked ride as
parameters plus a merge with older history. The bias and the reasoning now stand at
`MAX_GAP_DAY_SHARE` and in [the closure-gap signal](analytics/ride-downtime/closure-gap-signal.md).
