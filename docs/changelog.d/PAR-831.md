### Added — a forward archive of the curves users are served, scored per lead

Only the freshest forecast per target ever survived (`wait_time_predictions`
deletes future rows each run, `prediction_accuracy` keeps the last), so the
intraday curve a visitor saw one to seven days ahead could not be measured. A
new hourly job captures, at each park's own 06:00, the served 15-min curve
(`getParkPredictions`, PCN override and blend included) for 48 h and the
`/plan/day` curve for d0–d7, plus a park-day row with tier, `crowdLevel`,
`predictedCrowdLevel`, `ridesUnavailable` and the model behind the day level
(TFT / CatBoost / climatology); at 07:00 the same for the sparse long leads
d10, d14, d21, d30, d45, d60 and d90; at 10–18 every two hours the next 4 h for
UC1, with the live wait at the origin. A daily job at 12:00 UTC scores
yesterday against BENCH-SPEC truth (15-min forward-filled STANDBY inside
published windows, wait ≥ 5) per use case × lead × source × ex-ante segment ×
region: MAE, bias, next-best-ride precision (D1), best-time hit rate and regret
(D3), dayPeak error and ordering, crowd bucket accuracy and busy-day
recall/precision (D6), band coverage and stated-vs-realised error (D8), and
coverage (D9), with UC3 and D9 also split by level source. Board:
`GET /v1/admin/forecast-archive`. ~87 k rows / ~29 MB a day with indexes, kept
14 days past the target date (~0.8 GB steady state), plain tables with app-side
batched retention. See `docs/ml/forward-archive.md`.
