### Added — a forward archive of the curves users are served, scored per lead

Only the freshest forecast per target ever survived (`wait_time_predictions`
deletes future rows each run, `prediction_accuracy` keeps the last), so the
intraday curve a visitor saw one to seven days ahead could not be measured. A
new hourly job captures, at each park's own 06:00, the served 15-min curve
(`getParkPredictions`, PCN override and blend included) for 48 h and the
`/plan/day` curve for d0–d7, plus a park-day row with tier, `crowdLevel`,
`predictedCrowdLevel` and `ridesUnavailable`; at 10–18 every two hours it keeps
the next 4 h for UC1. A daily job at 12:00 UTC scores yesterday against
BENCH-SPEC truth (15-min forward-filled STANDBY inside published windows, wait
≥ 5) per use case × lead × source × ex-ante segment × region: MAE, bias,
best-time hit rate and regret (D3), dayPeak error and ordering, crowd bucket
accuracy and busy-day recall/precision (D6), band coverage and stated-vs-realised
error (D8), and coverage (D9). Board: `GET /v1/admin/forecast-archive`.
~60 k rows / ~20 MB a day with indexes, kept 35 days (~0.7 GB), plain tables
with app-side batched retention. See `docs/ml/forward-archive.md`.
