### Added — Parky shows posts and glossary terms as cards and reads a post in the same round

A Haiku test with park.fan's real search hits and post texts (12 questions on news, guides and the
glossary) found Parky reading posts well, but every answer from a post took two rounds
(`search_posts`, then `get_post` with the slug), a post could only be shown by leaving the page
(`open_page`), and a question about events with no park named ("which Halloween events are still
on?") was met with "which park?" although the news carry them.

- `show_card` kind `posts` shows one to three posts as the guides menu draws them (cover,
  category, title, teaser, date, reading time), found by `query` and `postKind` or given by
  `slugs`. With a `question` the result also holds the first post's text (`read`), so showing a
  guide and answering from it is one call.
- `show_card` kind `glossary` shows a term with its short definition and canonical page (one by
  default, up to three).
- `search_posts` takes a `question` too and reads its best hit as `read`; a read that fails comes
  back as `read: { error }` beside the hits.
- Glossary links use the canonical page the frontend names (`/de/glossar/…`), and `open_page`
  accepts the glossary under its segment in all six languages.
- The system prompt (version 42) answers planning, news and event questions from a post shown as
  a card, with or without a park named, and shows a park word as a glossary card.

The widget draws both cards from PArns/park.fan, which has to ship first: until then it drops a
card kind it does not know ([assistant](frontend/assistant.md#card-shapes)).
