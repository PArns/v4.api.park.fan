### Fixed — the hourly history rollup reads observations, not carried rows

`computeParkHourlyHistoryForDate` had no condition on `is_heartbeat`, so the
nightly `attraction_hourly_history` rollup counted the rows
`writeHourlyHeartbeats` carries forward as measurements. Because a heartbeat
copies the previous row's `data_source` as well, nothing downstream could tell
them apart, and they went into the published `p90`, `avgWait` and `sampleCount`
of every 15-minute slot. On 2026-09-19 `Midnight` at Cedar Point published
twelve slots at p90 = 5 min for a day in which the feed delivered the ride not
once. The query now carries the shared `observedReadingsSql` predicate, the
same one the outage reconstruction uses.

The filter applies going forward only. Measured against production on
2026-10-15: over 2026-10-08..14, with the writer fixed in PAR-720, three of
176,797 slots fall away (0.002 %). Over the five weeks before that fix it
would have been 13–19 %, and 99.8 % of those slots carry a wait the same ride
was also measured at on the same day — so the existing rows are not recomputed.
