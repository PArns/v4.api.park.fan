### Fixed — `/predictions/yearly` reads the schedule, so a closed day is `closed`

The yearly predictions aggregated the raw forecast and never asked the schedule.
On 2026-09-22, in the 89 days both endpoints cover, `/calendar` called 49 days at
Legoland Billund CLOSED and `/predictions/yearly` rated 30 of them and
recommended 28. Phantasialand's whole February 2027 came back `very_low` /
`highly_recommended`.

`aggregateDailyPredictions` now applies the calendar's rule: a CLOSED schedule
entry, or a day without an OPERATING or CLOSED entry that falls inside a gap
between operating dates, or outside the season for a seasonal park. The gap and
season part moved into `isClosedByOperatingRange()`
(`src/parks/utils/schedule-closed-day.util.ts`), which `CalendarService` calls
too. Such a day reads `crowdLevel: "closed"` and `recommendation: "closed"`,
without `avgWaitTime`. If the schedule cannot be read the forecast is served as
before.

A day rated `unknown` no longer carries a recommendation. 8 of 40 sampled parks
(Aquatica Orlando with 181 days, among them) returned `unknown` next to
`recommended`. `unknown` is now in the DTO's `crowdLevel` enum, and the Swagger
text says the response ends about 182 days out instead of 365.
