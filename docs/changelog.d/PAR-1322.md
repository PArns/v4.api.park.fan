### Fixed — ticket names no longer carry the planner's `.h` token

Five products in the seed catalogs named their hopper option with the
planner's query token, "(1 Park, or 2 Parks with .h)": Disneyland Paris'
1-day ticket, Disneyland Resort's 1-day and 2- to 5-day tickets, Walt Disney
World's ticket with Water Park and Sports, and Universal Orlando's
date-based ticket. They now read "(1 Park, or 2 Parks)", "(1 Park Per Day or
Park Hopper)", "(Park Hopper Plus)" and "(1 park per day or park-to-park)".
A catalog already saved in the database keeps its names until it is edited
in the admin ([tickets](frontend/trip-regions.md)).
