### Changed — after 2.21.0: deleting revoked keys, ticket names without `.h`

- **A revoked data API key can be deleted for good.**
  `POST /v1/admin/api-keys/:id/delete` answers 204, 409 for a key that is not
  revoked yet, 404 for none. It deletes the row, then the key's Redis
  counters and caches its two secrets as refused, and writes `api-key.delete`
  to the audit log, where the key stays visible
  ([API keys](frontend/api-keys.md)).
- **Ticket names no longer carry the planner's `.h` token.** Five seed
  products named their hopper option "(… with .h)": Disneyland Paris' 1-day
  ticket, Disneyland Resort's 1-day and 2- to 5-day tickets, Walt Disney
  World's ticket with Water Park and Sports, and Universal Orlando's
  date-based ticket. They now read "(1 Park, or 2 Parks)", "(1 Park Per Day
  or Park Hopper)", "(Park Hopper Plus)" and "(1 park per day or
  park-to-park)". A catalog already saved in the database keeps its names
  until it is edited in the admin.
- **A ticket's market says where it is sold and no longer filters.** Plan
  first suggested only products sold in the region's home market (`us` in
  Orlando); it now weighs every product with a suggestion rank, whatever its
  `markets`, on the same rules (the most planned days covered, then the
  fewest extra days, then the rank). The seed ranks the UK tickets after the
  operators' own, so the 14-Day Magic Ticket is suggested from 11 Disney
  days, Universal's UK 14-day ticket from 7 Universal days, and the 7-Day
  Magic Ticket for Disney days spread wider than a date-based ticket reaches.
  `long_stay` alternatives are the resort's 14-day tickets other than the
  suggested one, and a promotion no longer needs the home market. `markets`
  stays on every product, link and alternative. Parky marks such a ticket
  "sold by UK shops" instead of "UK/Ireland only" (prompt version 55). No
  route took a `market=` parameter. A catalog already saved in the database
  keeps its ranks: there the UK tickets are suggested once the admin gives
  them one ([tickets](frontend/trip-regions.md#plan-first-metaticketsuggestion)).
