### Changed — after 2.21.0: spread park days, open ticket markets, deleting revoked keys, ticket names

- **A revoked data API key can be deleted for good.**
  `POST /v1/admin/api-keys/:id/delete` answers 204, 409 for a key that is not
  revoked yet, 404 for none. It deletes the row, then the key's Redis
  counters and caches its two secrets as refused, and writes `api-key.delete`
  to the audit log, where the key stays visible
  ([API keys](frontend/api-keys.md)).
- **Ticket names no longer carry the planner's `.h` token.** Five seed
  products named their hopper option "(… with .h)": Disneyland Paris' 1-day
  ticket, Disneyland Resort's 1-day and 2- to 5-day tickets, Walt Disney
  World's ticket with Water Park and Sports, and Universal Orlando's
  date-based ticket. They now read "(1 Park, or 2 Parks)", "(1 Park Per Day
  or Park Hopper)", "(Park Hopper Plus)" and "(1 park per day or
  park-to-park)". A catalog already saved in the database keeps its names
  until it is edited in the admin.
- **A plan spreads its park days among the days that tie, and marks spare
  days.** On last year's measured days, rounded to 5 minutes, many days of a
  long trip tie, and the plan kept its visits wherever the assignment happened
  to put them: Japan, 14–30 January 2027, three parks twice each, had rest from
  the 21st to the 25th. Among placements that tie the cheapest (under 3
  minutes per park day dearer, no visit more than 5 minutes dearer than the
  cheapest plan gave it), the plan now takes the one that spreads its park
  days best over the trip and each stay (`spreadDays` in
  `trip-optimizer.ts`), at the same waits: no run without a park past three
  days there. `meta.exact` still proves the cheapest. Five of the seven frozen
  Orlando plans and three windows of one `/when` search moved inside the tie.
  A rest day the visitor did not ask for carries `spare: true`, counted in
  `totals.spareDays`; `spareDayParks` names the unchosen parks open on spare
  days with their three cheapest (Fuji-Q from Tokyo, LEGOLAND Japan from
  Osaka). The plan and `/when` cache keys move to `v4`
  ([spread among ties](frontend/trip-regions.md)).
- **A ticket's market says where it is sold and no longer filters.** Plan
  first suggested only products sold in the region's home market (`us` in
  Orlando); it now weighs every product with a suggestion rank, whatever its
  `markets`, on the same rules (the most planned days covered, then the
  fewest extra days, then the rank). The seed ranks the UK tickets after the
  operators' own, so the 14-Day Magic Ticket is suggested from 11 Disney
  days, Universal's UK 14-day ticket from 7 Universal days, and the 7-Day
  Magic Ticket for Disney days spread wider than a date-based ticket reaches.
  `long_stay` alternatives are the resort's 14-day tickets other than the
  suggested one, and a promotion no longer needs the home market. `markets`
  stays on every product, link and alternative. Parky marks such a ticket
  "sold by UK shops" instead of "UK/Ireland only" (prompt version 55). No
  route took a `market=` parameter. A catalog already saved in the database
  keeps its ranks: there the UK tickets are suggested once the admin gives
  them one ([tickets](frontend/trip-regions.md#plan-first-metaticketsuggestion)).
- **A purchase link names its country store, and the seed lists every
  store that sells a product.** Attraction Tickets sells from a UK, an
  Irish, a Dutch, a German and a US store, each at its own prices, and every
  such link read only "Attraction Tickets". A link now carries `store`
  (`{ country, currency }`, ISO codes in capitals, `GB`/`GBP`), checked on
  every write, so the client names it "Attraction Tickets · UK (£)" without
  reading the url; `locales` only orders the links. The seed gives each
  Attraction Tickets page its store from the path (`ATTRACTION_TICKETS_STORES`
  in `tickets.ts`) and adds the 52 store pages it lacked: 28 Irish and 24 US
  ones, taken from each product page's hreflang alternates and each answering
  200 without a redirect (Orlando 24, Southern California 20, Paris 8). The
  UK and Dutch pages were complete; Walt Disney World's date-based tickets
  are not sold in the UK, Irish or US store. A catalog already saved in the
  database keeps its links until the admin edits them
  ([tickets](frontend/trip-regions.md#tickets)).
