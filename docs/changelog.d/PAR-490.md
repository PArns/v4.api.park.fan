### Changed — the Wikipedia curation hint names a language instead of leaving it open

`curatedWikipediaUrl` told the editor "Beliebige Sprache — der Artikel verlinkt
seine Übersetzungen selbst.", and that is why 22 of 24 curated links pointed at
`de.wikipedia.org`, including parks in the US, the UK, Spain, Italy, Poland,
Sweden, Denmark, Belgium, France and the Netherlands. A German article about a
Dutch park serves one of the six locales. The hint now asks for the English
article, keeps German for parks inside the German-speaking area, and says the
target title comes from Wikipedia's `langlinks` endpoint rather than from a
guess — two of the sixteen titles already converted differ from the German one
(`Heide_Park_Resort` and `Gardaland_Resort`), so guessing would have produced
dead links. The rows themselves are curated data and were changed in
production, not here: 22 de / 2 en before, 6 de / 18 en after.
