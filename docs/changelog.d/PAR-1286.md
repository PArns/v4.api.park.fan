### Changed — park.fan's Instagram and Facebook links count as social

A page view's `source` may now also be `instagram.com` or `facebook.com`
(`SOCIAL_SOURCE_HOSTS` in `src/site-analytics/referrer.util.ts`), which
`REFERRER_RULES` already file under `social`. park.fan tags the links in its
own posts with `utm_source`, and Instagram's in-app browser and the apps a bio
or story link opens in send no referrer, so those visits counted as `direct`:
over the last 90 days 20 of 14,665 visits came from social networks, 58 % were
direct. `validSource` dropped every value outside `AI_SOURCE_HOSTS`; it takes
both sets now, and still only a host, never a raw spelling such as `instagram`.
The contract is in [site analytics](frontend/site-analytics.md).
