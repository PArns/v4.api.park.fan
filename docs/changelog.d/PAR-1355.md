### Added — Performance schedules for rides that start at fixed times

A ride can now carry curated start times per run of days
(`curatedPerformanceSchedules`, group „Vorstellungen" in the admin, also through
`curate_attraction`), served as `performanceSchedules` on the park payload and
the attraction detail. Phantasialand's „Die 3 Mausketiere" plays at 13:00 to
17:00 on the hour during Wintertraum, and the feed reports the cinema as an
attraction without showtimes that reads CLOSED. A client shows the next start
instead of a wait time. `get_attraction` adds the schedules and today's start
times. `get_park` lists the rides on a schedule today (`rides.performancesToday`,
with the next start) and leaves them out of `notRunToday`. Parky's `get_ride`
answers with `today.performances` instead of a reopening, and the system prompt
(version 58) has Parky name the next start, never a wait. Contract:
[performance schedules](frontend/performance-schedules.md).
