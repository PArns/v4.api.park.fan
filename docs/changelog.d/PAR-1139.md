### Fixed — Model-comparison cells no longer wait on a lead that never gets a row

Two matrix cells asked for a lead no archived row can reach, and a cell with
a lead without rows reports 0 days, so five switch decisions on the ML overview
would have read "0 von 14" for good. `cal_far` asked TFT for d60, but TFT's
horizon ends on forecast day + 59, so the archive's d60 day is always
CatBoost's: it now covers d30 and d45. The CatBoost shadow columns of
`cal_rank` / `cal_busier` asked for d0, which CatBoost's daily forecast never
covers (it starts tomorrow): `MODEL_MISSING_LEADS` takes d0 out of their leads.
The TFT row of the overview now says "1 bis 59 Tage".

The fixed evaluation dates follow the earlier floors: "TFT nur noch für volle
Fahrgeschäfte" is judged on 2026-12-07 (was 2026-12-22), both grid-TFT
decisions on 2026-12-24 (was 2027-01-08). The others keep theirs: H5 and
CatBoost-off still wait on d60 / d90, Chronos on its post-deploy fortnight,
the driver level on midsummer.
