The attractions list route documented three of its surplus rows as "two
different rides carrying the same curated name", hidden by the park payload's
grouping. Resolved against the upstream entities on 2026-09-20, none of the
three is a hidden ride: in each group the ride the odd slug names is already
served as its own row. `wahoo-racer` at Hurricane Harbor Arlington sits on
themeparks.wiki's Typhoon Twister while Wahoo Racer is
`wahoo-racer-twisted-whizzard`; Sea World's `castaway-bay-sky-climb` sits on
Wally the Walrus while the Castaway Bay climb is `castaway-bay-sky-fortress`;
Hurricane Harbor New Jersey's two rows answer to two upstream entities the feed
itself names alike. They are the rows §4a of the attraction-status guide
describes, where one row was handed the name another row's slug claims.

The route's Swagger description now says so, and carries the current
population: 48 surplus rows across 15 parks in 45 name groups. Measured over
all 210 parks and 7300 rows, grouping by name serves 7252 attractions and
grouping by name plus slug base serves 7255 — three names published twice, not
three rides recovered. `park-integration.dedupe.spec.ts` pins the three pairs,
so the upgrade to a slug key fails the suite rather than production.
