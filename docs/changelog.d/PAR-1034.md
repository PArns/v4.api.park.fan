### Fixed — PCN targets: in-force truth behind a flag, no heartbeats, no backtest leak

PCN trained on, and scored its own board against, the median of the `queue_data`
rows written inside each 15-minute slot. Rows are written on change and at least
hourly, so an unchanged ride landed in about one slot of four and change slots
weighed ~4×. `PCN_TARGET_MODE=in_force` (default `median`, so nothing served
changes) builds targets and board actuals from the BENCH-SPEC truth instead: the
reading in force at the slot midpoint, ≤ 3 h old, OPERATING, wait ≥ 5, inside the
published operating window. Unconditionally: every PCN `queue_data` read drops
heartbeat rows (PAR-1039), the offline backtest keeps training targets out of the
eval window and fits its scale on training slots only. `PCN_RESIDUAL` (default
off) predicts the last observation plus a learned change. See
`docs/ml/pcn-intraday-review.md` §12.
