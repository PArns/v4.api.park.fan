### Added — "Welcher Tag ist voller?" is measured as a ranking of a park's days

The question carried a per-ride day-peak error. It is now `cal_rank`: within a
park, every two dates of the window whose headliners' realised level differs by
at least 5 minutes, and whether the forecast ordered them the same way (D7,
until now unbuilt). The day-peak questions compare with TFT on the days TFT
levelled and with CatBoost on CatBoost's: `cal_far` stops at 60 days, where
TFT's horizon ends, and the 90-day lead moved to `cal_far_90` with CatBoost as
served. CatBoost, which never levels the same day as TFT, gets its live figure
against the weekday average under `comparisons`.
