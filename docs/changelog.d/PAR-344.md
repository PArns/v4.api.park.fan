### Added — what an attraction is for, as a curated field (`attractionKind`)

A nullable `attraction_kind` on attractions, hand-decided through the existing
curated-field editor: `RIDE`, `TRANSPORT`, `SHOW`, `WALKTHROUGH`. It reaches
both the attraction endpoint and every ride on a park payload.

`TRANSPORT` is the value it exists for. A railway station or a cable car builds
its queue from the timetable rather than from demand, so its wait time means
something different from a coaster's — and nothing in the data says which is
which. The upstream's `attraction_type` cannot: it holds a value on 1 of 7,374
rows (measured against production on 2026-09-22). The name cannot either. Of
151 transport-sounding attractions, 17 are headliners and only 3 of those are
transport systems — `Big Thunder Mountain Railroad`, `Seven Dwarfs Mine Train`
and `Ghost Train` are a coaster, a coaster and a dark ride. A backfill from
either source would seed exactly the wrong rides, so the column starts empty
and fills by hand.

The headliner tiering is deliberately untouched. A transport system still
counts toward a park's baseline as it did before: of the three real cases,
Disneyland Paris is capped at ten headliners and loses nothing by keeping its
railway, while Ocean Park's two cable cars are two of only four rides there
with enough history — excluding them drops the park below the three-headliner
mark and switches its crowd baseline from absolute thresholds to relative ones.
This field describes; it does not re-rank.

Null means nobody has judged the attraction, which is true of nearly all of
them. It is never "it is a ride".
