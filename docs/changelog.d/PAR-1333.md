### Fixed — two accuracy reads use the park's day and hour instead of UTC

The nightly `forecast_accuracy_profile` rebuild cut its truth at UTC midnight while it counted
target days up to yesterday, so the newest day of every American park was scored without its
evening: a day that read 20 minutes at 10:00 and 60 at 20:00 park time went in with a peak of 20.
The profile feeds `/plan/day`'s `expectedError` and `accuracy.typicalError` and TFT's
`uncertaintyMinutes`; one of 45 target days per ride in the Americas was affected. A day now
counts once it is over in the park's timezone, with all of its hours
([the served error grid](ml/long-range-forecasting.md#7-the-served-error-grid)).

The ML dashboard's `patterns.hourly` and `patterns.weekday` grouped by the UTC hour and weekday
across all parks, so "hour 14" pooled Florida's 09:00 with Germany's 15:00 and Japan's 23:00, and
a Saturday evening in Orlando counted as Sunday. Both group by the park's own time now. Admin
only. `test/e2e/ml-accuracy-park-local.e2e-spec.ts` runs both against the Timescale container.
