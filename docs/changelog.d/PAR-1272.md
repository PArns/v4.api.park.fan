### Changed — Parky's tools say why something is missing instead of leaving it out

Several tools dropped what they could not answer without a word, so Parky could only say "there
is none". Each gap now comes with its reason.

- The live state of a closed park (`parkNow`) keeps its crowd figures for the day so far and sets
  the level to null with `levelWhy`, rather than removing it. The park cards of `show_card` kind
  `parks` read the same summary, so a closed park on them shows no level either.
- `compare_parks` reads up to a year ahead and lists every park it cannot rank with the reason:
  closed (with the day it opens next), open without a rating, hours not published yet (with the
  calendar's estimate) or not known (past the forecast, calendar unreadable).
- `get_best_days` counts the closed days in its window and, when none is open, names the next day
  the park opens.
- `show_card` kind `parks` lists a park it could not show and why; `get_shows` names the shows
  without a time that day; `get_ride` lists the facts nobody recorded as `unknownFacts`;
  `get_ride_forecast` says why a ride has no hours that day.
- The ride lists name what they could not judge, the first 20 each: `withoutValueRides`,
  `typeUnknownRides`, `shelterUnknownRides` (`LEFT_OUT_NAMES_LIMIT`, shared with the height lists).
- `open_page` opens a park's average-wait and with-kids pages where the park has them, by the
  frontend's own gates, and otherwise refuses with the reason ("knows the minimum height of only 8
  of its 30 rides").
- The system prompt (version 36) has Parky tell such a reason in a few words and never turn "not
  known" into "there is none".
