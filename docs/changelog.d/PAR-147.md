### Fixed — a push subscribe can no longer leave a pointer to a deleted trip

`POST /v1/push/subscriptions` read the trip without a lock and wrote the subscription
outside a transaction. A subscribe landing between the `UPDATE push_subscriptions` of
`DELETE /v1/trips/:id` and its commit kept `tripId` and `topics` of a plan that no
longer existed, until the nightly `sweepExpired` cleared it.

`PushService.subscribe` now reads the trip with `pessimistic_write` inside a
transaction when a `tripId` is sent, the lock `TripsService.remove` and `update`
already take, and answers 404 when the trip is gone or expired. Calls without a
`tripId` (ride alerts, show follows) take no lock. No schema change.
