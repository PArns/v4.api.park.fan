### Fixed — CatBoost trains on OPERATING readings and no frozen after-hours rows

`ml-service` training data, and the serving reads that mirror it:

- **OPERATING only.** The hourly label is the median of OPERATING rows. The old
  filter `status = 'OPERATING' OR waitTime > 0` let in CLOSED, REFURBISHMENT
  and DOWN rows that still carried a wait. DOWN rows still count towards
  `downtime_minutes_today`: they are summed over the earlier hours of the day
  in SQL (`downtime_count_before`).
- **Frozen readings out.** A row more than 60 min outside the published window
  is dropped when its `lastUpdated` is more than 120 min older than its
  timestamp, or NULL. Frozen re-writes were hours to ~30 days old. Real
  Halloween-event readings were 13–64 min old, so they stay.
- **`is_park_open` no longer comes from the label.** Training set it from
  `waitTime >= 10`, which serving cannot see. It is now 1 inside a window, 1
  on a day without one (as serving reads it), and 1 for a fresh after-hours
  reading.
- **Measured days admitted (PAR-1054).** Days with
  `park_day_operations.measured_operation = true` are now included.

**Needs a retrain.** Compare `holdoutMetrics` with the previous model: the
champion gate may reject it.
