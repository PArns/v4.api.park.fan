### Added — a ride bound to a park season is seasonal, with the season's days as its window

Phantasialand's ice rink and skate hire exist only during Wintertraum, and
outside it the park page counted them as closed. A park season of kind
`halloween`, `christmas`, `winter`, `summer_nights` or `special_event` that names
a ride in `attractionIds` now binds it: the ride is seasonal, in season on the
season's days (its `dates`, or every day between its bounds, park-local) and out
of season on every other. `expected` seasons bind, `cancelled` ones do not;
`maintenance`, `opening` and `closure` keep their meaning. The ride's own
curated months or a curated `false` override the binding, and the binding
overrides everything `detect-seasonal` wrote.

There is no new column. `Attraction.seasonBindings` is a virtual column (a
subquery per row on `idx_park_season_park_start`), and both resolvers read it:
`resolveCuratedFacts`/`isCurrentlyInSeason` and their SQL twin
`attractionIsOutOfSeason`, which agree on all 756 combinations checked on a
scratch Postgres. Every surface that reads a season follows without code of its
own: the park payload and its counters, the ride page, favourites, nearby, the
free-flow gate, `/plan/day` (judged on the planned day), ride alerts, the SQL
counters, the outage lines, `get_attraction`, `get_park` (a new
`OUT_OF_SEASON` bucket and an `outOfSeason` list) and Parky's reopening, which
names the season and its next day. The silent-cluster card, the "season or
gone?" list, the retirement candidates and the children sync's absence step
leave a bound ride alone. Attractions gain `boundSeason` (the season whose
bounds hold today, else the next, else the last). A season write now evicts
the rides it binds, and `plan-day:resp` moves to `v2`. Contract:
[season-bound rides](frontend/seasonality.md#a-ride-bound-to-a-park-season);
precedence: [seasonality](architecture/attraction-status/seasonality.md#a-ride-bound-to-a-park-season).
