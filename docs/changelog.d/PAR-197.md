### Added — a park can carry a curated early entry, peak and off-peak minutes

Hotel guests enter a chosen set of rides 15 to 60 minutes before opening at
Disney World, Universal, Europa-Park and Efteling, and no feed says so. The park
now has three curated columns, `curated_has_early_entry`,
`curated_early_entry_minutes_peak` and `curated_early_entry_minutes_off_peak`,
all nullable, so `synchronize` adds them to the populated `parks` table without a
backfill. They appear in the generic editor at `/admin/parks/<id>` with no
frontend change, and a park merge carries them over. `context` of
`/v1/parks/:slug/plan/day` returns `hasEarlyEntry` and the minutes only when the
flag is `true`. No values are written yet.
