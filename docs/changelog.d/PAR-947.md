### Performance — the nowcast stops polling Open-Meteo every 30 min for closed parks

The nowcast is the only Open-Meteo surface on a 30-min cadence, and it is
fetched on demand, so crawlers and evening visitors kept closed parks on it: on
2026-10-20 at 19:34 UTC, 20 of the 29 warm nowcast keys belonged to parks that
were closed (Universal Studios Japan at 04:34 local). The per-park cache TTL now
follows the park's hours (`nowcastCacheTtlSeconds`): 30 min while open or within
an hour of opening, up to 3 h while closed but never past one hour before the
next opening, and 30 min when no upcoming window is known.
