### Changed — every container's log rotates at 10 MB, five files per service

`docker-compose.production.yml` sets `logging: json-file` with `max-size: 10m` and `max-file: 5` on
all seven services, so a container keeps at most 50 MB of stdout instead of whatever the host's Docker
default does, which may be no rotation until the container is replaced. What is still to check on the
host for the privacy policy, the Coolify proxy's access log and Cloudflare's own logs, is listed in
[Coolify](deployment/coolify.md#container-logs).
