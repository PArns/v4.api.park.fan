### Changed — every container's log rotates at 10 MB, five files per service

`docker-compose.production.yml` sets `logging: json-file` with `max-size: 10m` and `max-file: 5` on
all seven services, so a container keeps at most 50 MB of stdout and stderr instead of whatever the
host's Docker default does, which may be no rotation until the container is replaced. The first deploy
with the block recreates every container, Postgres and Redis included. See
[Coolify](deployment/coolify.md#container-logs).
