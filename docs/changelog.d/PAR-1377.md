### Fixed — trip regions: a day past 60 days reads last year's matching day, not whatever fell 52 weeks back

From day 61 every trip surface rates a day by the measured day a year back (PAR-1319), and that
day was always 52 weeks back. Holidays slipped: Easter Monday 2027 (29 March) read Monday 30 March
2026, an ordinary Monday, while Monday 5 April 2027 read Easter Monday 2026. Memorial Day,
Ascension, Whitsun and every school break that moved did the same.

The day is now picked by what it is
([the matching day a year back](frontend/trip-regions.md#the-matching-day-a-year-back)): a public
holiday reads the same holiday last year by name in the park's own region; a bridge day or a
school-break day reads the nearest day of the same weekday, at most three weeks from 52 weeks back,
that was one too; any other day moves off a holiday, bridge day or break day by a week or two. A
day past the stored school calendar does not move off last year's breaks, since its own break is not
known yet. Inside a confirmed or announced park season, a day of last year's edition goes first. A
park without a country, or whose holidays cannot be read, keeps 52 weeks back.

`lastYearWait`, the `last_year` days of `/calendar`, `/best-days`, `/plan` and `/when`, and Parky's
`get_park_history` read the same day; the latter now names it `comparableDayLastYear`. The warmup
keeps 13 past months instead of 12. The dev shim has no holiday table and still reads 52 weeks
back.
