### Fixed — season flags read the park's month, not the server's

`isCurrentlyInSeason` (`curated-attraction-facts.util.ts`) and the shows on the
park payload read `new Date().getMonth()`, the container's month, so at every
month boundary a seasonal ride or show was in or out of season for a few hours
against the park's own calendar: on the 31st at 23:30 UTC it is already the next
month in Tokyo and still this one in New York. The resolver now takes the park's
timezone and every caller passes it (park and attraction payloads, `nearby`,
ride alerts, `rain-soon`); `/plan/day` asks about the planned date's month
explicitly. Its SQL twin `attractionIsOutOfSeason`, which feeds the park-card
counters, reads `NOW()` in the park's timezone as well, so the two still agree.
A test pins a month-end instant for America/New_York and Asia/Tokyo
([seasonality](frontend/seasonality.md), [shows](frontend/shows.md)).
