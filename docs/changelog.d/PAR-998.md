### Added — a whole park can be retired, and keeps its page while it leaves every list

`parks.retired_at` and `parks.retired_reason` mirror the attraction columns: the
operator's last operating day and why, with the source. A retired park keeps its
page — `/v1/parks/...` serves `retiredAt` and `retiredReason` so the frontend can
render it as closed permanently, the way a ride retired as closed is rendered —
but it leaves `findAll` (and with it every job that loops parks), the geo lists,
search, discovery and nearby. `POST /v1/admin/retire-parks` writes it and retires
the park's still-active rides with the same date and reason; rides already
retired keep their own date. First use: Six Flags America, closed for good on
2025-11-02, which still listed 47 active attractions. Contract:
[Retired parks](../frontend/retired-parks.md).
