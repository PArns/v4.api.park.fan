### Added — Parky opens a park's crowd calendar, the park comparison and the site's own pages

`open_page` knew a park, a ride, a post and a glossary term. Parky offered "the park's page, where
the crowd calendar is" and could open only the park page, and he could not take anybody to the
park comparison, their push alerts or the best-time overview.

- `open_page` takes `section: "calendar"` with a park, which opens its wait-time calendar by the
  English segment; the widget's `localizedPath` respells it in the reader's language.
- `compare` (2 to 4 parks) opens `/compare?parks=` with each park's full geo path, which no other
  park shares, so Disneyland Park Paris and Anaheim cannot swap.
- `page` opens `alerts`, `best_time`, `favorites` or `trip_planner`.
- Exactly one target per call. The average-wait and with-kids pages stay out: they exist only for
  parks with enough data, and the frontend decides that.
- The system prompt (version 32) names the new targets.
