### Added — a daily check that every surface shows a park-day alike

A park-day is shown on the crowd calendar, `/best-days`, `/plan/day`, the trip-region calendar, the
park payload and the park lists, and by Parky in his park card, his chat tools and his day tip, each
through its own read path. Nothing compared them, and in three days visitors saw two contradictions:
Europa-Park's today panel against its day dialog, and on 2026-11-17 Parky's tip at Phantasialand
("hoher Andrang, lieber nicht") above a day the calendar rated very low.

`DisplayConsistencyService` reads one park's days, today and the next eight, through the methods those
endpoints and tools call, and compares the pairs the code makes one statistic: the calendar's level
against best-days, `/plan/day`, the region calendar, `get_days`, `get_park_day`, `todayExpected` and the
tip's stamp, the headliner and per-ride waits, the live level of the payload against the park lists and
Parky's card, and the stamp of each tip served from its 12-hour cache against the one it would get
now. Live against the day's forecast, today so far and last year are different statistics and are
listed apart, never as mismatches; an unknown or absent value is never one either. Each pair and its
rule: [display consistency](analytics/display-consistency.md).

`GET /v1/admin/display-consistency/check?park=&days=` and the MCP tool `display_consistency` run it on
demand. The job `analytics` / `check-display-consistency` runs it at 09:40 UTC, after the morning
calendar warmup, over the parks in a running season and the 25 most read park pages of the week, keeps
each park's result for two days, and `GET /v1/admin/display-consistency` and the data-quality report
(`displayConsistency`) read the last run.
