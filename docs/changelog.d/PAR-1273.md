### Added — Parky compares particular days of one park and knows which of them it is closed

"Saturday or Sunday?", "10 or 17 May 2027?" and "does Baron 1898 run at the end of December?" had
no tool: `get_park_forecast` reaches 31 days from today, `get_best_days` names only the ten
quietest days, and `get_park_day` reads one day.

- `compare_dates` takes a park and 1 to 10 dates, or a stretch of up to 31 days, up to a year
  ahead, and reads them from the calendar every park page shows, one month at a time.
- Each day says whether the park is open, closed or has not published its hours yet. A closed day
  names the next day the park opens; a day past the published schedule is `UNKNOWN` with the
  reason and the calendar's estimate, never a confirmed closure.
- The search for the next open day is one helper now (`firstOpenDay`), which `get_ride`'s
  `reopensOn` reads too.
- The system prompt (version 35) sends questions about particular days there, and has Parky call
  a closed day closed, with the next open day.
