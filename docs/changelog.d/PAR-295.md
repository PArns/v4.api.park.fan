### Fixed — a retired ride no longer gets hourly heartbeat rows

`writeHourlyHeartbeats` loaded every attraction without a `retiredAt`
predicate. A ride retired less than 24 hours after its last upstream sighting
passed all three checks that follow (park open, last reading older than an hour,
`attraction:last-seen` younger than `STALE_THRESHOLD_MS`), so the job could
write an `is_heartbeat` row for it every hour until the last-seen key aged out.
The load now filters `retiredAt IS NULL`, the same predicate the live-data
preload has carried since PAR-159.

Production showed 0 such rows on 2026-09-16. The gap was open in the code but
not yet hit; PAR-621's absence retirement makes it reachable more often.

No other job calls `attractionsService.getRepository().find()` without a
predicate. Four scoped reads still return retired rows and stay as they are:
the two per-park loads in the children sync (they have to see a retired row to
match it), `processLandData` (writes land metadata, not `queue_data`) and the
`six-flags-heights` backfill (writes `minimumHeight`).
