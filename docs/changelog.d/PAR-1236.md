### Fixed — a park its schedule calls closed no longer reads open on the word of a frozen feed

On 2026-11-05 park.fan showed Energylandia open at 22:40 local time with 20-minute waits, on a day its
schedule calls closed. Queue-times kept repeating last weekend's waits for 33 rides all day (one value
each for 12 hours, fresh timestamps), and `ConflictResolverService` turned the wiki's correct CLOSED
into OPERATING whenever a second source reported 5+ minutes (Kennywood the same). LEGOLAND Billund's
wiki entries kept saying OPERATING with a source timestamp from April. The wait-times poll now asks
the park's own schedule first (`scheduleSaysClosedNow`, today's decisive rows, an hour of grace around
each OPERATING window): while it says closed, the resolver's override is taken back, an OPERATING
from queue-times or wartezeiten-app alone reads CLOSED, and so does one whose source timestamp is over
a day old (`retractClosedParkOverrides`). A fresh wiki reading stays, and a park without a decisive
schedule row is left as before.

The same rows fooled the data-quality monitor's health gate, so the admin listed parks between
seasons as "rides went silent at once" (8 of 21 clusters that day). `findSilencedClusters` now drops
a cluster when the park's schedule decided every day since the silence and opened on none; a park
without schedule rows for those days stays on the list.
