### Added — `/plan/day` looks back a year where the forecast ends (`climatology` tier)

Past a park's schedule coverage `/plan/day` answered `tier: "long_range"` with no
curves. A new tier `climatology` fills that gap with the hourly median of what was
measured on comparable days a year earlier: the same weekday 52 (and 104) weeks
back, up to three weeks either side, in the same holiday situation
(`holiday` / `school_vacation` / `regular`). It is marked as a retrospective, never
a forecast: `climatology.label: "how_it_was_last_year"`, `accuracy.basis:
"unmeasured"`, no `expectedError`, no `leadTimeMae`.

A ride and each of its hours need at least 4 reference days, otherwise the day
stays `long_range`. The floor is measured: over 3,479 held-out ride-days, the
median of 3 days missed by 5.86 min, of 4 by 5.60, and each further day took
less than 3 % off. Rides out of season get no curve, as with `composed`.
New read `AnalyticsService.getParkHourlyHistoryOnDates`; no schema change.
