### Fixed — holiday features land on their own rows, and training and inference compute them the same way

The `holidays` table holds several rows for one (country, region, date) — CH-ZH
2025-04-21 five times; 4,569 such regional keys and 1,577 national ones in
production. Training merged that table onto the feature frame and assigned the
result back by index label, so the merge came back longer than the frame and
every row after the first duplicate read another row's holiday. Replayed on the
production holidays and parks (one row per park-day, 2024-10-09 → 2026-10-09),
50.9 % of 154,241 park-days change at least one holiday feature, and
`is_school_holiday_primary` alone 46.8 % — the totals barely move (59,401 →
57,871 school days), the flags were on the wrong days. Inference was a separate
re-implementation (last-write-wins on duplicates, only `public` counted as a
holiday, no national fallback for neighbour slots, no Easter fallback, and
today's holidays dropped out of the fetch window): 3.3 % of the next year's
park-days change there.

Both paths now call `ml-service/holiday_features.py`: duplicates collapse to
OR-ed `public` (public/bank/bridge) and `school` flags, evaluated once per
(park, local date) and written back by position with a row-count assertion.
Region aliases (`NRW→NW`, `NDS→NI`, `England→ENG`, …) now match
`region.util.ts`. Also fixed: nf-service never matched a regional influencing
region (`DE-NW` vs `NW`), and shape-service never matched a regional school
break (`DE-NW` vs the park's `NW`).
