### Fixed — ride-profile writes carry their reason and source in the audit row

`PUT` and `DELETE /v1/admin/content/attractions/:id/ride-profile` wrote their
`admin_audit_log` row with `reason` and `source_url` empty, because
`RideProfileWriteDto` had no such fields. All 8 `ride-profile.write` rows on
2026-10-16 had no source, against 399 of 662 `attraction.curate` rows with one.

Both endpoints now accept an optional `reason` and an optional `sourceUrl`
(validated as an http or https URL) and pass them to `AdminAuditService.record`.
The two fields are stripped before the profile upsert. Rows written earlier are
not backfilled.
