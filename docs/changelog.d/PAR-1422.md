### Added — Parky's chat for park.fan's native app, without Turnstile

`POST /v1/assistant/session` without `turnstileToken` now issues a session to a request whose data key carries the new permission `assistant.chat` (group `assistant`), so park.fan's iOS app, which cannot solve a Turnstile challenge, can chat with Parky ([assistant](frontend/assistant.md#the-challenge)). The key passes the data routes' own check (401 unknown, revoked or expired; 403 `api-key-permission-missing`; its hourly allowance), the address limit of 10 sessions an hour still applies, and a new ceiling of 2,000 sessions an hour per key bounds the whole app. Everything after the session, the per-session limits and the abuse handling included, is unchanged, and so is the browser's Turnstile path. park.fan's `internal` keys hold the permission by their usage; keys from before permissions do not ([API keys](frontend/api-keys.md)).
