### Fixed — a container in shutdown takes no new Bull job, and the deadline line says what held it

On SIGTERM/SIGINT every Bull queue in the process is now paused locally
(`queue.pause(true, true)`) before Nest runs a single lifecycle hook. Measured on
2026-10-22: three of six deploy shutdowns ran into the 20 s deadline, and in one
of them the old container took a `live-cache-warmup` job 2 s after the signal,
which the deadline then killed and the next container marked stalled.

The `deadline-exceeded` line in `logs/shutdown.<date>.log` now also carries
`openHooks` (`<Class>.<hook>` still running) and `activeJobs` (per queue, the job
id and name this process still runs). The 20 s deadline is unchanged. See
`src/common/utils/shutdown-work.ts`.
