### Removed — `POST /v1/page-views` and the `page_view_days` entity; `source` in the collect format

The deprecated alias `POST /v1/page-views` is gone (404): park.fan's beacon has sent `page_read` to
`POST /v1/analytics/collect` since PAR-1151. Its DTO, `PageViewsService.record` and their specs went
with it; `GET /v1/page-views/top` and `GET /v1/admin/page-views` are unchanged. Bots are still counted
once as dropped, now only through the collector.

`page_view_days` has no entity any more, and park and ride merges no longer move its rows: nothing
read it since its rows were copied into `site_events`. `synchronize` does not drop a table, so it stays
in production until the owner drops it by hand after checking the import marker
([runbook](troubleshooting/db-health-runbook.md#2b-dropping-a-table-whose-entity-is-gone)).

A page view may carry `source`: an AI assistant's host from `utm_source`, checked against the same
`AI_SOURCE_HOSTS` list the frontend uses, and used when no `referrer` came. Until park.fan sends it,
the old shape (that host in `referrer`) counts the same, and a dotless `referrer` is still dropped as
a stray `utm_source`; that rule goes one release after the frontend switched. See the
[wire format](frontend/site-analytics.md#wire-format).
