### Fixed — park stats no longer count a day the schedule calls CLOSED

`/stats` built `byMonth` and `byDayOfWeek` from every day `queue_data_aggregates`
had rows for. Some feeds report rides as OPERATING with a wait of 0 while the
park is shut, around the clock: Europa-Park's headliners wrote 25,418 such rows
in February 2026, a month the schedule lists as CLOSED on every day, and the
month came out as 28 sample days with a median of 0.

`queryHeadlinerDayValues` now drops a day that has a park-level CLOSED schedule
entry and no park-level OPERATING one, the same answer `/calendar` gives. The
filter sits on the day values, so `byMonth`, `byDayOfWeek`, `totalSampleDays`
and the typical-day-peak all change together. Europa-Park's February goes from
28 sample days to 0 and its total from 264 to 213; Toverland goes from 262 to
225 and Walibi Holland from 173 to 148. Phantasialand, whose feed reports
nothing on closed days, stays at 211.
