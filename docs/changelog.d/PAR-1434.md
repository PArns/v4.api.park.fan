### Changed — Push notifications say the number first, and the iOS app gets levels, stacks, photos and action categories

Every notification now follows one pattern: the title is the thing and the number or time that
decides ("Taron: 15 Min." instead of "Taron: nur noch 15 Min.", "Chiapas in 10 Min." instead of
"In 10 Min.: Chiapas"), the park is a line of its own (`aps.alert.subtitle` on a device, the first
line of the body in a browser), and the body is the reason ("Unter deiner Grenze von 20 Min.").
A plan change is no longer titled "Dein Plan: …": its first change is the title. The morning plan
adds the day's crowd level, high and chance of rain. A long ride name is cut so the number stays
(32 characters). All six languages; a French colon gets its space, an English date its month name.

For devices, `buildApnsRequest` now writes per kind: `interruption-level` (time-sensitive for
wait and reopen alerts, next-up, shows and rain; active for plan changes and the morning plan;
passive, silent and priority 5 for news and guides), `relevance-score`, `category`, a `thread-id`
per park and park day, an `apns-expiration` at the moment the message stops being true (before:
15 minutes for everything), and, where the media index has a photo that needs no credit line, an
`image` URL with `mutable-content`. A device gets at most 8 time-sensitive notifications in 12
hours, the next ones as ordinary ones; the same wait alert notifies at most every 30 minutes.
`next-up` opens the plan day instead of `/`; a rain warning's tag is one per park day.
`PUSH_IMAGES=false` sends no photos. Installed app builds ignore the new keys. The contract is in
[push](frontend/push.md#the-device-payload).

Each kind now has one fixed pictogram leading its title, the same in all six languages and for
browsers as for devices (⏳ wait alert, ✅ reopened, 🎢 next up, 🎭 show, ☔ rain, 🔄 plan change,
🌅 morning plan, 📰 news, 📖 guide; 🚶 is the app's own reminder): the owner's decision for
notifications, against the brand's no-emoji rule everywhere else. The title budget of 32
characters includes it. News and guides get a subtitle in Parky's voice ("Parky hat Neues für
dich", "Parky hat einen neuen Guide"), in six languages; the post's title stays the title.
