### Added — School holidays for Great Britain and Japan

Great Britain had bank holidays but no school-holiday rows: neither Nager.Date
nor OpenHolidays carries GB school data, because English term dates are set by
each local authority. The holiday sync now writes a curated file,
`src/holidays/data/curated-holidays-gb.ts`, with the term dates of
**Staffordshire County Council** (Alton Towers) under `GB-ENG`: May half term
2025 through summer 2029, the last day the council has published
([docs](architecture/holiday-sources-outside-europe.md#great-britain-par-1072)).
All seven GB parks are in England, so they all get this one calendar, which is
a documented simplification (Surrey, Thorpe Park's council, differs in a few
weeks).

Japan gets `src/holidays/data/curated-holidays-jp.ts`: the school breaks of
**Nagoya** (LEGOLAND Japan, `JP-23`) from winter 2025-26 through March 2027,
from the city's own school-year page
([docs](architecture/holiday-sources-outside-europe.md#japan-par-1072)).
Urayasu (Tokyo Disney Resort), Osaka (USJ) and Yamanashi (Fuji-Q) are not
curated yet and stay unknown.

**Changed:** GB and JP join `REGION_ONLY_SCHOOL_COVERAGE`, so Scotland and
Wales (influencing regions of every GB park), Chiba, Osaka, Yamanashi and the
neighbour prefectures read as unknown instead of borrowing another region's
coverage.
