### Added — page views per kind, page and language for the "most read" lists

New module `src/page-views/`. `POST /v1/page-views` counts one view of a post (`article`, `news`,
keyed by `translationKey`), a park or an attraction (keyed by UUID) in one of the six locales, as
one upsert into `page_view_days (kind, key, locale, day)`. The frontend's beacon posts it straight
from the browser. Not counted: bots (by User-Agent), `DNT: 1`, `Sec-GPC: 1`, a fourth view of a
page by one reader (address bucket, an IPv6 /64, plus User-Agent) per UTC day, and anything past
500 views per address bucket and day. No address is stored or logged: the caps are Redis counters
keyed on an HMAC under `PAGE_VIEWS_HASH_SECRET` (new, optional) that expire with their UTC day.
`GET /v1/page-views/top` ranks one kind in one language over 7, 30 or 90 days (at most 50) and is
cached for an hour; `GET /v1/admin/page-views` gives the admin the totals, the split by language,
the most viewed pages (over all languages or ranked by one) and every day of the window. A merged
park or ride hands its views to the survivor. The table gets `fillfactor=80` and a 1 % autovacuum
threshold at start-up. See `docs/frontend/page-views.md`.
