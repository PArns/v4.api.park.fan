### Added — page views per kind, page and language for the "most read" lists

New module `src/page-views/`. `POST /v1/page-views` counts one view of a post (`article`, `news`,
keyed by `translationKey`), a park or an attraction (keyed by UUID) in one of the six locales, as
one upsert into `page_view_days (kind, key, locale, day)`. The frontend's beacon posts it straight
from the browser. Bots (by User-Agent) are not counted, and one address counts the same page at most
three times per UTC day. No address is stored: the cap is a Redis counter keyed on an HMAC under
`PAGE_VIEWS_HASH_SECRET` (new, optional) that expires with its UTC day. `GET /v1/page-views/top`
ranks one kind in one language over 1–90 days and is cached for an hour; `GET /v1/admin/page-views`
gives the admin the totals, the split by language, the most viewed pages and the views per day. See
`docs/frontend/page-views.md`.
