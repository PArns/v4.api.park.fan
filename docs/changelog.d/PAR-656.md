### Fixed — a curation write no longer buys a vanished ride another 60 days

The children sync retires a row whose id has left its park's `/children` for
`ABSENT_UPSTREAM_RETIRE_DAYS`, and it used to read that window off `updatedAt`.
`updatedAt` is an `@UpdateDateColumn`, so every writer moved it — and the
writers that moved it most were the curation endpoints. Measured on 2026-10-04:
six active rows in three parks (Bellewaerde, Dollywood, Plopsaland Deutschland)
were absent upstream for months, silent since 2026-04-23, and carried the
`updatedAt` of the `indoorOutdoor` charge. All six had their retirement pushed
out to late November, and in PAR-587 that was the whole difference between two
duplicates resolving themselves and a dead row keeping the counter-free slug.

The absence now has a column of its own, `attractions.absent_since`, which the
children sync sets when a park syncs successfully without listing the id and
clears the moment the id is listed again.

A row absent for the first time is seeded from the evidence that it existed. If
the sync wrote it inside the last three daily runs, the feed was listing it
that recently and the clock starts today whatever its readings say. Without
that window the 60-day grace against a feed dropping an id would be gone
for the 421 active rows measured on 2026-10-04 that carry no real reading in 60
days while still being listed. Only a row whose own write is older is
back-dated, to the earlier of its `updatedAt` and its last real reading, so a
row that went quiet long before anyone looked does not get a fresh clock; where
no real reading is left, `updatedAt` is all there is and the seed can then only
delay a retirement, never cause one.

Nothing else writes the column except `unretire`, which sets it to the moment
of the un-retirement: a person stating that the ride exists is a sighting, and
a better one than the feed's silence.

The clock is bookkeeping about the feed rather than about the retirement, so it
also runs for the seasonal rows the absence step leaves alone (PAR-682). The
season gate sits after it: an editor who answers the "season or gone?" list
with `curated_is_seasonal = false` retires the row on the absence it has
already accrued, instead of starting a fresh 60 days at the answer.

The 72-hour window reads `updatedAt` as evidence that the feed listed a row,
and that is a heuristic rather than a proof: the column only moves on a real
`UPDATE`, so a row the sync lists without changing keeps an old one. Measured
on 2026-10-05, 23 of the 106 rows carrying a write older than the window are
still listed upstream, 7 of them with every other gate open. The docstring
carries the numbers and PAR-714 carries the options.
