### Fixed — A ride's history and today's chart stay filled when the park closes at or after midnight

A park open 16:00–00:00 (Six Flags Qiddiya City) got the closing slot "00:00" as the upper bound
of its day, so every slot after opening was dropped: the wait-time history read "no data" and
today's chart showed a single 00h bar. A closing slot at or before the opening slot now means the
day runs past midnight and has no upper bound; closings at 00:00, 01:00 and 02:00 are tested.
Slots after midnight still belong to the next park-local day, so the chart ends at 23:xx.
