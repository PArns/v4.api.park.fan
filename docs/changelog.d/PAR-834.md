### Added — `/plan/day` in quarter-hours, composed from the H5 profile (behind a flag), and a composer A/B in the forward archive

The planner's composed days stretched a year's hourly P50 profile until its
maximum equalled the day level. Scored on 15-minute truth in the ML review's
offline benchmark, that is worse than a plain weekday-median profile from d3 on
(+0.5 to +1.1 min MAE; first-hour MAE 6.50 against 3.87, bias +2.3, because a
median profile with its top pinned to a P90-like level lifts every hour). With
`PLAN_DAY_H5_COMPOSER=true` composed hours now come from the H5 profile — the
ride's last 56 days, its first hour aligned to the published opening (rope-drop
ramp), its last hour to the closing, hourly medians interpolated onto
quarter-hours in between, weekday/weekend first and all days second — a port
pinned against the benchmark's own SQL by a golden test. A TFT level scales it
(by the ratio to the ride's 56-day median daily P90, opening hour unscaled) only
for ex-ante busy rides (window q90 ≥ 45) at leads ≤ 6 days, the one cell where
the benchmark's horizon table shows it pays; everywhere else, and never with
CatBoost's level, the plain profile is served. Days past 90, rides with no
profile, and a failed profile read (replayed for 45 s) keep the old composer.
Every ride with a 15-minute answer gains `slots[]` (`minute`, `start`,
whole-minute `wait`, `source`); a composed H5 hour is the mean of its slots;
`sampleDays` keeps its meaning and the H5 window is `profileDays`. With the flag
off the response is byte-identical to before except for `composer` on composed
rides. Independently of the flag, every 06:00/07:00 forward-archive capture now
writes the old composer, plain H5 and routed H5 on the same quarter-hours under
three shadow surfaces, scored as `UC3S` (≤ ~120 k rows / ~43 MB a day, ≤ ~1.5
GB steady state). Profiles are read from `attraction_hourly_history` and
`schedule_entries` (never `queue_data`) under statement limits, with a new
`("parkId", date)` index built concurrently in the background. Contract,
remaining differences from the benchmark and frontend changes:
`docs/frontend/plan-day-endpoint.md` §2a.
