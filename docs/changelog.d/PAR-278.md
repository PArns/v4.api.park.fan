### Changed — the nightly closure-gap statement reads the works period through its helper

`CLOSURE_GAP_INTERVALS_SQL` spelled out the curated works-period predicate by
hand instead of calling `attractionIsCuratedOutOfService("ca", "g.op_day")`,
the helper the DOWN reconstruction already uses. It now calls the helper. The
emitted SQL is the same three conjuncts plus one outer pair of parentheses.
Against production on 2026-10-02 the two forms agree on all 8,284,664
(attraction, day) pairs over 7,559 attractions and 1,096 days, including the one
attraction that has a window declared. `closure-gap.sql.spec.ts` now fails if
either statement drops the helper or adds a second copy of the predicate.
