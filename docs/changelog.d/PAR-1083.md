### Fixed — The hourly wait-time band no longer jumps between neighbouring hours

The factor that recalibrates CatBoost's hourly `uncertaintyMinutes` (PAR-1060)
is now clamped to [1, 2] when served: it never narrows the model's own band and
never more than doubles it. The fitted factor ranged 0.25 to 4.2 within one lead
bucket because it was compensating the median's bias, so a ride's band could
leap from 6 to 101 minutes an hour later. Measured over the calibration window,
coverage rises from 0.949 to 0.957, the mean live band falls from 62 to 34
minutes, and jumps of 40 minutes or more fall from 1437 to 58.
