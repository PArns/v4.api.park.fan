### Fixed — No round-the-clock hourly forecasts for parks without an opening window

A park with no OPERATING schedule entry for a day got hourly forecasts for all
24 hours of it: 20 parks / 491 rides held stored slots at 02:00–04:00 local,
and Knoebels (schedule UNKNOWN) served 192 `hourlyForecast` slots with 05:45 as
its first "optimal" `bestVisitTimes` entry. On such a day only the hours the
park's queues have actually been measured in are now kept — the `/stats/hourly`
rule (an hour needs 10 measured days), the same profile `/plan/day` uses for
`hoursSource: "observed"`. Without a profile that day gets no hourly slot. Days
with an OPERATING window are unchanged. The gate sits on the ml-service read, so
the park payload, attraction detail, favorites, calendar today, `/plan/day`,
the forward archive and the stored rows all inherit it.
