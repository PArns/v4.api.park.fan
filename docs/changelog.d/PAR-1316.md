### Added — trip regions: how accurate the forecast was, per region

Each trip region now says openly how far off its forecast was:
`GET /v1/trip-regions/:region/accuracy` answers the mean absolute error of
the day's peak wait at the headliners as forecast a week ahead, over the
region's last 30 measured days and averaged over its parks, with the window,
the lead, the parks counted and the measured days
([accuracy](frontend/trip-regions.md#how-accurate-the-forecast-was-get-v1trip-regionsregionaccuracy)).
It reads the archive the planner's expected error reads, the served daily
level against each headliner's day P90, so the hub and the plan never
measure two different things. Where fewer than 100 rows or 14 days are
measured, or no park holds 20 rows, the figure is `null`. The route is open,
an hour in the browser and a day at the edge, and the archive is read once a
day per region.
