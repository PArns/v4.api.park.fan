### Changed — without a key only the wait times are open, and a data key carries permissions

Until now a data key opened the forecasts and everything else was open. Now a caller without a
key reads `GET /v1/parks/{…}/{park}/wait-times` and nothing else, and every other public route
needs a key that carries the route's permission: 19 permissions in seven groups (live, parks,
rides, statistics, forecasts, planner, travel), listed at `GET /v1/api-keys/permissions` and in
[data API keys](frontend/api-keys.md). Weather, opening hours, the park directory, ride heights
and figures, seasons and statistics are behind a key too. A key without the permission gets 403
`api-key-permission-missing` with `requiredPermission`; the park and ride detail drop each field
whose permission the caller lacks (`PERMISSION_FIELDS`). Every route names its permission with
`@ApiPermission` or says why it is open with `@OpenRoute`, and `api-permission.coverage.spec.ts`
fails on one that does neither. A key issued before permissions reads every permission, and
park.fan's own key every permission plus the internal ML and debug routes. Every keyed request now
counts against the hourly allowance and in the statistics.

Developers register themselves (`POST /v1/developer/register`) as accounts with the new role
`developer`, below `viewer`: they reach their own session endpoints and `/v1/developer/*` (their
keys with statistics, pickup, rotation, end of grace, revocation; their requests) and nothing of
the admin, no MCP tool and no token (`developer-role.spec.ts`). Key requests are stored in
`api_key_requests` and decided by the owner at `/v1/admin/api-key-requests`; an approval issues a
key bound to the developer's account whose first secret nobody sees until its holder picks it up.
A request is `free` only for open source that earns nothing but donations, `paid` otherwise.
