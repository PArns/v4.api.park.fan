### Fixed — CatBoost holiday dropout feeds what serving sees before the break

The holiday dropout in `ml-service/train.py` is meant to make the model learn
the holiday flags. It replaced the lags and the occupancy with proxies taken
from 7-day windows, and inside a multi-day break those windows already hold the
break. The occupancy proxy came out at about 1× the real value, and the 28d/90d
means were not replaced at all. On `catboost_v20261024_0600` the flags were
still at about 0 % importance (`is_holiday_primary` 0.00,
`is_school_holiday_primary` 0.05, `holiday_count_total` 0.00).

A dropped-out holiday row now carries the values serving gives it when the
forecast is made before the break (`holiday_pre_break_values`). The origin is
local midnight after the park's last non-holiday day:

- rolling means (7d, weekday/weekend, 28d, 90d), trend, volatility, lags and
  downtime: from the ride's last row before the origin;
- weekly same-hour slots: only those before the origin;
- velocity: 0;
- occupancy: the park's 8-week weekday-by-hour profile before the origin.

**Needs a retrain.** Afterwards, check the holiday flags in
`metrics.feature_importances` (`GET /model/info`). Compare `holdoutMetrics`
with the previous model: the champion gate may reject it.
