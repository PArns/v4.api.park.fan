### Added — `rain-soon` push: a rain warning that names the covered rides that are open

Subscribers can ask for the new topic `rain-soon` (`PUSH_TOPICS`). When the
nowcast puts the start of rain at most 30 minutes ahead at a park they have a
plan for today or a ride alert in, the existing five-minute push job sends one
notification per park and rain front, for example "Regen ab etwa 14:20 Uhr" with
up to three open covered rides and their standby wait. Rain that is already
falling sends nothing, and a second front the same day warns again once the first
has stopped. The text exists in all six languages. The covered-ride gate (half of
a park's in-season rides need an `indoorOutdoor` value) and the ranking follow the
frontend's `covered-rides.ts`; below the gate the push is the warning alone.
Quiet hours apply. Documented in `docs/frontend/push.md` (rain-soon).
