### Fixed — Park statistics windows now cut at park-local midnight

The three aggregate queries behind a park's historical statistics and its
hourly profile bounded their window at midnight UTC while both bounds are
park-local days. East of UTC the first day lost its early local hours and the
last day gained the next day's; west of UTC the other way round. The bounds are
now park-local timestamps, written as a range on the column so the hypertable
index and chunk exclusion still apply — the hourly-profile query dropped from
3.06 s to 0.93 s against production as a side effect, because the old bound's
`date` cast is stable and was re-evaluated per row.
