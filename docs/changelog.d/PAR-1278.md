### Changed — Parky reaches a ride by its name and calls fewer tools twice

A test run of 41 questions through Haiku with Parky's own prompt and tools showed where he spends
rounds and calls tools twice.

- `get_ride`, `get_ride_forecast` and `set_ride_alert` take a ride by its name with its park
  (`rideIdOf`), so "tell me when Blue Fire is under 20 minutes" needs no `find_rides` round
  first. Several rides of the same name come back to pick from.
- `compare_rides` without `by` lists every ride of its filters by name, each marked `ridden`
  where the visitor ticked it off, for "which coasters haven't we done".
- `get_days` ranks a stretch's five quietest open days (`quietestOpenDays`), so "the quietest
  days in May 2027" needs no reading of 31 rows.
- `get_shows` and `find_restaurants` say that their cards return the same rows; the prompt never
  pairs a card with its lookup. A refusal for a day not in the plan says what works instead (a
  party's height goes to `get_park_day` and `plan_add_rides`). `get_park_live` names
  `todayExpected`; `show_card` names its park parameter for shows and restaurants.
- The system prompt (version 41): small talk needs no tool, and "where is it quiet right now" with
  no park goes to `find_parks_nearby`. The eval has a case for an alert by ride name.
