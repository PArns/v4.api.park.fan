### Added — a "season or gone?" list for rides retired as absent with no season decided

The absence retirement skips seasonal rows, but the detector cannot see a
season before 330 days of watching, so a maze in its first year is retired like
a demolished ride (17 such rows on 2026-10-03). Those rows are now listed:
`absenceRetiredUnreviewed` on `GET /v1/admin/data-quality`
(`findAbsenceRetiredUnreviewed`), and one WARN per park from the nightly
data-quality job. Setting `curated_is_seasonal` — true with the months, or
false — takes a row off the list.
