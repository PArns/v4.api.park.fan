### Fixed — a ride with no reading and no status is rated unknown, not very_low

The park page and the ride page rated a ride `very_low` (or the model's `moderate` placeholder) when it had no wait time and its status was UNKNOWN. Hell House at Movie Park Germany showed "unknown" next to "very low". Both endpoints now return `crowdLevel: "unknown"` for that case, so the two payloads agree. Rides with a status other than UNKNOWN keep the model fallback.
