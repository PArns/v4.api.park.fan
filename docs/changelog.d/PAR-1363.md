### Fixed — Parky's interview no longer runs into the 30-second window chip after chip

Parky's interview answers one reply chip after another, and Parky answers in 3 to 5 seconds: a
day interview sent its first question and three chip taps within about 25 seconds, and the fourth
got a 429 from the window of 3 questions per 30 seconds. A question whose text is one of the
replies Parky offered under his last answer (`answersOffer`, his `suggest_replies` compared
without case and extra spaces) no longer counts in that window. It still counts in the 20 per 10
minutes and 150 per day and in the visitor's token budget, and typed questions keep the short
window. Details in [the assistant page](frontend/assistant.md#limits-and-cost).
