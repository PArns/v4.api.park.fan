### Changed — CatBoost's hourly band is calibrated to the coverage it is named for

CatBoost's `uncertaintyMinutes` is `q0.95 − q0.5`, a one-sided band that should
hold 95 % of outcomes and measured 53–57 % (PAR-167). A daily job
(`prediction-accuracy` / `calibrate-bands`, 04:20 UTC) now fits one multiplier
per lead bucket × predicted-wait segment from the last 28 days of scored slots
that were served CatBoost, so that `actual ≤ served wait + k·band` holds 95 % of
the time (split conformal; a cell under 500 slots borrows the pool of its own
lead bucket, never another bucket's — a lead bucket without 500 slots serves the
raw band).
The table lives in Redis for three days and multiplies the band on every
CatBoost hourly slot served; PCN slots (no band) and Chronos slots (their own,
near-calibrated band) are never touched, and without a table the band is served
raw as before. The raw band is now stored on `prediction_accuracy`
(`uncertainty_minutes`) so the fit never reads its own output. The current table
and its in-sample coverage before/after are on `GET /v1/admin/ml-overview`
(`bandCalibration`). Measurement: D8 band coverage in the forward archive before
and after.
