### Added — `nearby` lists the shows of the park the visitor is in

`GET /v1/discovery/nearby` with `type: "in_park"` now carries `data.shows[]`:
every non-retired show of the detected park, not cut to the rides' radius,
nearest first, each with `distance` (metres), `status`, today's park-local
`showtimes[].startTime` (ISO 8601), the season flags and `url`. The "you are in
the park" card can name the next show without a second request
([discovery](frontend/discovery.md)).
