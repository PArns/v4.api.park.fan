### Changed — weekly dependency refresh, minor and patch only

`@nestjs/throttler` moves from 6.5.0 to 6.7.0, and the dev toolchain follows its
own patch and minor releases: `eslint` 10.10.0 to 10.11.0, `jest` 30.5.1 to
30.5.2, `prettier` 3.9.6 to 3.9.8, and the `@types/node` definitions 26.4.1 to
26.6.2. All stay inside their current major, so no call site changed. The build,
the unit suite and the linter pass unchanged.

The majors on offer were left where they are: the `@nestjs/*` packages, `typeorm`
and `ioredis` each cross a major, and `typescript` 7 and `dotenv` 18 carry their
own breaking notes. Each of those wants its own change with its own migration,
not a line in a routine refresh. `pnpm audit` reports no advisories.
