### Changed — the churn tables are vacuumed at 04:50 UTC, before the CatBoost training

On 2026-10-12 at 06:15 UTC the kernel OOM-killed the 06:00 CatBoost training on the
shared host, with ten autovacuum workers each allowed 2 GB beside it. A new
`db-maintenance` queue runs `vacuum-churn-tables` at 04:50 UTC: `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` on
`pcn_forecasts` (9.46 M dead of 122 M), `prediction_accuracy` (1.11 M dead, 5 320
autovacuums), `shape_forecasts` (2.40 M), `tft_forecasts` (0.49 M) and `holidays`
(14.8 % dead), one at a time on its own connection with `lock_timeout` 5 s,
`maintenance_work_mem` 256 MB and a statement timeout that ends every VACUUM by 05:58.
It skips a table already being vacuumed, starts nothing at or after 05:50 and never
fails. Hypertables stay with autovacuum, which yields to a compression policy where a
manual VACUUM would not.

Two lock and churn sources in the training window went with it. `compare-accuracy`
ran the `prediction_accuracy` retention DELETEs on every 15-minute tick — 96 times a
day, one of them at 06:15:17 on the morning of the OOM — although the daily
`cleanup-old` at 04:00 applies the same windows; the per-tick copy is gone. And
`pcn-service` ran `CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS` on `pcn_forecasts` in every write and
prune, which takes a SHARE lock that cancels any autovacuum of the table; the DDL now
runs once per process. See [DB Health Runbook §5a](../troubleshooting/db-health-runbook.md).
