### Added — a trip across cities: the visitor picks the buffer for a hotel change

`moveBuffer=` (30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes) sets what a hotel change costs on top of the train in a
region with bases: check-out, luggage and check-in. Without it the region's own
`stayRules.moveOverheadMinutes` holds, 30 in Japan, where most travellers send their bags ahead by
courier (takkyubin), and 60 everywhere else. The answer names the buffer it used in
`meta.moveBuffer`, the region route lists the choices in `stayRules.moveBufferOptions`, and the
value is part of the plan's cache key. The client asks the visitor instead of deciding for them
([trip regions](frontend/trip-regions.md#bases-a-trip-across-several-cities)).
