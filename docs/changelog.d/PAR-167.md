### Added — the lead-time archive records CatBoost's daily error 90 days out

`prediction_lead_snapshots` samples a seventh lead bucket, `d90`. The TFT
serves up to forecast day + 59, so the row is CatBoost's served daily level and
its `uncertaintyMinutes`, the measurement PAR-167 needs to calibrate CatBoost's
daily band past the seam at day 60. It costs one row per headliner per night
(about +17 %) and reports after about three months. Until a bucket has 100
scored rows, `/plan/day`'s `leadTimeMae` now answers with the nearest published
bucket below it instead of going blank, so the new bucket does not blank the
`d60` figure for days 90 and beyond
([the seam](ml/quantile-serving-and-calibration.md)).
