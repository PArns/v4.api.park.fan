### Changed — Parky offers the next step when a visitor only names a park

A visitor who typed only "Magic kingdom" got the park card and "closed right
now, check the card for today's hours", and nothing after that. The system
prompt (`system-prompt.ts`, version 11) now treats a message that only names a
park or a ride, or a question about when to go or how busy it gets, as someone
sizing up a visit. Parky shows the card, adds one sentence on what it means
right now, and ends with one offer: open the park's page with its crowd
calendar, forecasts and planner, plan a day there, or find the quietest days.
It never offers the page the visitor is already on, and it stops offering once
one was taken or turned down. A yes counts as asking, in the prompt and in the
descriptions of `open_page` and `plan_day`, so Parky then acts on it. The
contract is in [the assistant page](frontend/assistant.md#the-answer-loop).
