### Fixed — A free-flow ride's history no longer reads closed on the days it ran

The detail's `history` counts only readings of 5 minutes or more. A free-flow
ride (`open_with_park`) reads 0, so its days were missing and the ride page drew
every open day as „ganztägig geschlossen": Phantasialand's Die 3 Mausketiere
showed one measured day in 31. On a park day with an OPERATING schedule, inside
the ride's season and, for today, once the park has opened, such a ride now gets
a day with `withoutWait: true`, an empty `hourlyP90` and `utilization:
"unknown"` (`freeFlowRanOn` in `free-flow-status.util.ts`). See
[effective status](frontend/effective-status.md).
