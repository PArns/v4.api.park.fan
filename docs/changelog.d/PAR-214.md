### Added — a ride alert can wait for the ride to reopen

`POST /v1/push/ride-alerts` accepts `kind: "reopen"`: one push when a ride seen closed, down or in refurbishment reports `OPERATING` again, with no wait-time threshold. `ride_alerts` gets one nullable column, `kind` (`null` stays the wait-time alert), and `thresholdMinutes` becomes nullable. The trigger reads the status edge, so a ride that stays open sends nothing on later five-minute cycles, and a ride out of season never fires. Parks with no readable wait-time source answer 400, as for wait-time alerts. Notification texts exist in all six languages.
