### Fixed — a redeploy now shuts the API down in order instead of killing it

Nothing in this process reacted to SIGTERM: Nest runs `onModuleDestroy` and
`onApplicationShutdown` only from `app.close()`, and `enableShutdownHooks()`
was called nowhere. A Coolify redeploy stops the old container with
`docker stop --timeout=30`, so it had 30 s to wind down and used 133 ms of it —
31 Bull queues went down with their job locks still held, ~19 TypeORM pool
connections were dropped mid-flight, and the abortable boot chains from PAR-79
were never reached outside the test suites. In the retained Bull history, all
10 jobs that failed with `job stalled more than allowable limit` were picked up
again 1:41 to 9:09 after a deploy started, three of them the daily `downtime`
run.

SIGTERM and SIGINT now run the hooks, bounded by a 20 s deadline: if a hook is
still running then — Bull's `close()` waits for the job in flight — the process
exits with 143 rather than waiting for Docker's SIGKILL at 30 s. Each shutdown
writes its duration to `logs/shutdown.<date>.log`, which survives the deploy
that caused it. SIGUSR2 is deliberately not in the set: it is how a diagnostic
report is requested from a hanging process.
