### Fixed — a park whose feed has gone silent no longer announces future operating days

The schedule and the live data come from the same upstream, and when that
upstream stops measuring it does not stop publishing. La Ronde had been silent
for 89 days while its ThemeParks.wiki schedule still named all 344 remaining
calendar days as OPERATING — 59 of them in the Montréal January, with no CLOSED
day anywhere in the range — and the calendar offered 11 January with real
opening hours, `isEstimated: false` and `recommendation: "recommended"`.

`saveScheduleData` now stores a park's future OPERATING days as UNKNOWN, without
hours, while its whole feed has been silent for `PARK_FEED_SILENT_DAYS` (30) —
the same threshold that already stops a silent park's rides from reading
OPERATING. Past days and CLOSED days are untouched in both directions, and the
first real reading brings the operating days back on the next sync.

Measured on production 2026-09-21: 10 parks, 1254 future park-level OPERATING
rows. The 9347 future operating days at the 148 parks whose feed still answers
are unaffected.
