### Changed — a ride that gets its riders wet is marked `tooColdToGetWet` while the park is cold, and is no short wait

Below 15 °C felt at the park (`WET_RIDE_MIN_FEELS_LIKE_C` in `src/parks/utils/wet-ride-weather.util.ts`,
`weather.now.apparentTemperature`, or `temperature` where that is missing), a ride with
`mayGetWet: true` is no tip, because its queue is short only because nobody wants to sit wet in the
cold. On 2026-11-24, at 2.2 °C (felt -2.1 °C), Phantasialand's Chiapas led park.fan's homepage with
5 minutes against a usual 40.

- **`shortWaits`** of `/v1/discovery/nearby` (`pickShortWaits`) leaves such a ride out, and the next
  ride takes its place.
- **`tooColdToGetWet: true`** is new on the rides of the park payload, on the attraction detail and
  on the rides of the `in_park` answer. `isHeadliner` stays as it is: the ride is still a headliner,
  and the key says why it is no tip right now. The attraction detail and `in_park` read the reading
  from `park:integrated:<id>`, one `GET`; a miss marks nothing.
- **Parky** gets the key on `get_ride`, on `get_park_live`'s longest waits and on the rows of
  `show_card` kind rides, and the prompt (`SYSTEM_PROMPT_VERSION` 66) has him name such a ride as a
  headliner but never as a walk-on, a short wait or a tip without saying it is too cold to get
  soaked.

A park without a reading, and a ride whose `mayGetWet` is `null`, are never marked. See
[too cold to get wet](frontend/too-cold-to-get-wet.md).
