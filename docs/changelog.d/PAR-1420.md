### Changed — a ride that gets its riders wet is no short wait and no headliner while the park is cold

Below 15 °C felt at the park (`WET_RIDE_MIN_FEELS_LIKE_C` in `src/location/wet-ride-weather.util.ts`,
read from `weather.now.apparentTemperature` in the cached park payload, or `temperature` where that
is missing), a ride with `mayGetWet: true` leaves two places in `/v1/discovery/nearby`:

- **`shortWaits`** (`pickShortWaits`) no longer lists it, and the next ride takes its place. On
  2026-11-24, at 2.2 °C (felt -2.1 °C), Phantasialand's Chiapas led park.fan's homepage with 5
  minutes against a usual 40: short because nobody wanted to get wet, not a tip.
- **`in_park`** answers it with `isHeadliner: false`, so the compass and the headliner list stop
  sending the visitor across the park to it. It stays in `rides`. The reading comes from one `GET`
  of `park:integrated:<id>`, which the five-minute prewarm keeps warm.

A park without a reading, and a ride whose `mayGetWet` is `null`, keep the ride and the flag. See
[discovery](frontend/discovery.md).
