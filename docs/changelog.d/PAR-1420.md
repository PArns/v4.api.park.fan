### Changed — short waits leave out a ride that gets its riders wet while the park is cold

`pickShortWaits` (`src/location/short-waits.util.ts`) no longer lists a ride with `mayGetWet: true`
while the park's felt temperature now is below 15 °C (`SHORT_WAIT_WET_MIN_FEELS_LIKE_C`), read from
`weather.now.apparentTemperature` in the cached park payload, or `temperature` where that is
missing. On 2026-11-24, at 2.2 °C (felt -2.1 °C), Phantasialand's Chiapas was the first card on
park.fan's homepage with 5 minutes against a usual 40: short because nobody wanted to get wet, not
a tip. The next ride takes its place. A park without a reading now, and a ride whose `mayGetWet` is
`null`, keep the ride. The payload already held both fields, so the filter costs no query. See
[discovery](frontend/discovery.md).
