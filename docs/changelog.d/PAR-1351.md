### Fixed — every trip-region route takes the frontend's release as `v`

After a deploy that changed a region's answer, Cloudflare kept serving the old
copy of `/v1/trip-regions/:region` for up to 26 hours (a day at the edge, two
hours stale), and the API cannot purge it: on 15 November the edge copy of
Orlando was about 18 hours old and had no `weather`, `hotels` or
`ticketMarkets`. The frontend now sends its release as `v`, so each of its
releases, deployed after the API's, reads a fresh edge copy. The routes with a
query DTO (calendar, best days, plan, ideas, `/when`, school breaks) take `v`
through one shared DTO, `TripReleaseQueryDto`, instead of a 400 for an unknown
parameter; no route reads it, and no Redis key holds it. The edge windows stay
as they were ([trip regions](frontend/trip-regions.md)).
