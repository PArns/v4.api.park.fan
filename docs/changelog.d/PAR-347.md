### Added — a show's `endTime` on the park payload

Each showtime on a park's `shows[]` now carries `endTime` when the feed's end
is after the start and at most three hours later. The feed already sent one,
the park payload dropped it. On 2026-11-06 31 % of 1,913 showtimes passed: 53 %
had an end equal to the start (a placeholder, every Universal park and most of
Disney US), 16 % had none, and a few Disney entries spanned 3–9 hours. The
favorites list, which passed the feed's end through unchecked, now applies the
same rule. See [shows](frontend/shows.md).
