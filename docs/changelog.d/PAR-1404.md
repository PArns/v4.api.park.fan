### Added — Visitor accounts carry the parks ticked off as visited

The trip-region hubs on park.fan let a visitor tick off the parks they have already been to, so the
day's tip moves on to one not seen yet. A signed-in visitor's account now carries those ticks to
their other devices like the ridden rides: `PUT /v1/account/data` takes `visitedParks`, a list of
`{ region, park, date }` (two slugs and the region's date of the tick, at most `MAX_ACCOUNT_IDS`),
and `GET /v1/account` answers it. A region's park is kept once, with the later date
(`uniqueVisits`). The column `account_data.visited_parks` is jsonb with `[]` as its default, so the
deploy adds it to every row; the account's deletion and the data export include it with the rest
of the row. See [accounts](frontend/accounts.md).
