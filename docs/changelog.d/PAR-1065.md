### Added — real school and public holidays for KR, SG, AU-QLD and CN; USH region fixed

The fixed-window `peak:` rows that stood in for Asian and Australian school
holidays were deleted from production. In their place, the holiday sync now
writes data taken from the publishers themselves
([docs](architecture/holiday-sources-outside-europe.md)):

- **KR**: school vacations from the NEIS open API, per region, where a day
  counts when the majority of 15 sampled elementary schools are off. Public
  holidays, including substitute holidays, from KASI. Both need a key
  (`NEIS_API_KEY`, `KASI_SERVICE_KEY`) and are skipped without one.
- **SG**: MOE school vacations and school holidays for 2026–2027.
- **AU-QLD**: state school holidays and student-free days, 2026–2029.
- **CN**: the State Council's 2026 holiday blocks, plus the six weekend
  make-up working days (调休) as the new `holidayType = 'working_day'`. That
  type is filtered out of every holiday reader and is not yet used as a model
  feature.
- Curated files live in `src/holidays/data/`, with a source URL on every
  entry. Nothing is extrapolated: past the last published day the data reads
  as unknown (PAR-1049).

**Fixed:** Universal Studios Hollywood had `regionCode = FL`, left over from
its old Bull Creek geocode, because correcting a park's coordinates never
re-derived its region. `correct-location` now does.
