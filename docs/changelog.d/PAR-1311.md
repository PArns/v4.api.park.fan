### Added — the trip planner's usage and server metrics in the admin MCP

Two MCP tools look at the multi-day trip planner
([admin MCP](admin/mcp.md#the-trip-planners-tools)). `trip_planner_report`
reads the frontend's 22 `trip_*` events and `ticket_link_clicked` per region:
the funnel from a start to days saved, an `.ics` or a copied link with its
drop-off, the dates and parks read off the requests, find dates, ticket
answers and modes, hopper, views, the errors shown, and the purchase links by
seller kind and position, each attributed to its region by the product's
catalog. Small values fold and visitor strings come under `visitorData`, as in
`planner_report`.

`trip_planner_ops` reads a new record the server keeps of every answer of
`/plan`, `/when` and `/best-days`: latency percentiles (all answers and the
built ones), the answer's and the calendar's cache, whether the search was
proven, the loosened rules, the visits left out and why, the error codes, the
owned tickets by kind, and the slowest shapes by trip length, parks and bases
([what the server records](frontend/trip-regions.md#what-the-server-records)).
A row holds the UTC hour, the API's own codes and the trip in counts: no date,
name, message, address or visitor id.

**Schema:** a new table, `trip_request_metrics`, created by `synchronize` at
the deploy; nothing else changes. Rows are kept 90 days and deleted nightly at
01:25 UTC by the new `trip-metrics` / `prune` job.

### Changed — the trip planner's funnel follows the dates-first planner, and Parky's tools have a report

`trip_planner_report`'s funnel is now started, requested, shown, changed,
tickets and kept
([the trip planner's tools](admin/mcp.md#the-trip-planners-tools)). Changed and
tickets are optional, so they read as shares of the plans shown, not as a
drop-off; kept is rated against the plans shown and counts the day planner
opened from a trip day. Since the dates-first planner, a ticket answer fires
only after a plan, only with yes and once per browser, and it is no longer sent
at all, so the old second step read as a near-complete drop-off. The
first UTC day the new planner's events were counted (`newFunnelFrom`) splits a
window: the days before it are counted by the old planner's events, the days
from it by the new ones, and every rate is taken over the days both steps
count. The report reads the tracking audit's new events (edits, options,
ticket openings, parks left out and their fixes, the finder, the date grid's
mode, the accuracy note, the stay suggestion) as soon as the frontend sends them, and
names the page types the planner was used from. The planner tile on a region
park's pages is read as a way in beside the sources: its views
(`section_seen` of `trip_tile`, attributed to a region by the park in the page)
and its clicks (`trip_tile_clicked` by link, plan or calendar) per region. The
site statistic tools list
the trip regions' page types `trip_hub`, `trip_month` and `trip_best_time` and
filter by them.

Two MCP tools look at Parky ([Parky's tools](admin/mcp.md#parkys-tools)).
`assistant_tools_report` sums his tool calls across chats per tool, outcome,
trip region and prompt version. A failed call now records why as a closed code
(`unknown_tool`, `invalid_input`, `refused`, `failed`), with the paths a schema
refused and the keys it dropped, never a value. `assistant_report` reads his
window, questions, reply chips and interview from the site statistic, with the
chips' press rate and the interview's completion.

`trip_planner_ops` now also records the plans of Parky's trip tools
(`caller: parky`), and on every row the parks left out and, in a region with
bases, the move buffer, the ends the visitor fixed and whether other stays were
suggested ([what the server records](frontend/trip-regions.md#what-the-server-records)).

**Schema:** a new table, `assistant_tool_day_stats` (the daily sums the chat
prune keeps of the tool calls it deletes, kept like `assistant_day_stats`), and
five columns on `trip_request_metrics`: `caller` with the default `site`, and
`left_out`, `move_buffer`, `gateways` and `stay_suggested`, nullable. The tool
call's reason code lives in the `toolCalls` jsonb, with no column change.
