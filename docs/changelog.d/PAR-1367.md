### Added — Parky says one sentence about a day in the crowd calendar

`GET /v1/parks/:continent/:country/:city/:parkSlug/parky-tip?date=&locale=` answers the calendar's
day dialog on park.fan with `{ date, verdict, text, promptVersion }`. The text is one sentence of at
most 160 characters in Parky's voice, written from the calendar's entries for the day and three days
on each side. The verdict (`good`, `meh`, `bad`) comes from the day's crowd level, so the model
cannot call a packed Saturday a good idea.

A closed day, a day without a forecast, one outside today to 90 days ahead, Parky switched off or a
spent token budget answer 204. Redis keeps each park, day and language for twelve hours, a refusal
included, so a day costs one model call per language; the edge keeps it six. Details in
[the assistant page](frontend/assistant.md#parkys-tip-for-a-calendar-day).
