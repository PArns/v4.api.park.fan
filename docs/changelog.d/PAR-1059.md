### Added — Frozen decision reports and a drift watch on the admin ML overview

A decision of `/admin/ml` is now judged once and stays judged: after the 12:00
UTC forward-archive scoring on its `evaluateOn`, a report of the window ending
the day before is written to the new table `ml_decision_reports` (plain, no
FK, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) and never recomputed. It carries every
cell's diff, both CIs (park × date and park-cluster), parks, days and joint
rows, the weekday mix of the paired park × date cells, the holiday / school
break / bridge-day share, the season, and the verdict. Three mandatory checks
— a weekday above 25 %, holidays above 40 %, an autumn-only window — plus a
window that ends early turn into `warning` with plain-German lines, never a
silent green. The overview reads a stored report's verdict instead of
re-reading its window, which ages out of `forecast_archive_paired` after 120
days. `MlDecisionDto.report` is additive.

The same daily step computes a drift watch per served model (raw CatBoost
with q0.8 / q0.95 coverage, served PCN and Chronos-2) from
`prediction_accuracy`: last 7 days against the 28 before, coverage and MAE ÷
the weekday median (same ride, weekday and hour, the four weeks before each
slot). A coverage move above 5 / 3 points or a ratio rise above 10 % sets
`MlOverviewDto.drift.status = "drift"`; nothing is sent anywhere.
