### Changed — the storm season is August to October in Orlando and Japan, and every trip day says its typical weather

The trip planner named a storm season wherever any storm had ever come: Orlando's hurricane season
ran June to November, Japan's typhoon season June to November, half the year, and in November every
Japan trip read "typhoon season" although neither Tokyo nor Osaka has had a typhoon approach in
November in thirty years (JMA normals 1991–2020). One rule now holds for every region: the season is
the months in which storms reach the region at least half as often as in its peak month, named and
priced alike. Orlando: tropical storms and hurricanes within 150 km of Magic Kingdom, 1950–2025 (NHC
best track), 3 in June, 3 in July, 8 in August, 13 in September, 9 in October, 3 in November; Japan:
mean approaches near Tokyo and Osaka 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.15, 0.7 and none a month. Both seasons are
now 1 August to 31 October; the peaks are unchanged. The region route's `weather.storm.from`/`to`
follow the season by half month, so the planner's calendars mark exactly the days a plan warns of,
and `/when`'s months say `hurricaneSeason`/`typhoonSeason` when any day of the month is in it
([how a day is priced](frontend/trip-regions.md#how-a-day-is-priced)). On the synthetic Orlando year
the ten- and seven-day searches now rank their November window first, at April's wait.

Every `/plan` day carries `climate`, the date's typical weather at the day's park (a day without one
at its base's nearest park, else the region's climate park), and every `/when` window the same over
its days: the mean high and low, the range half of such days fell in (the 25th to 75th percentile,
"typically 16–18 °C"), the precipitation and the chance of a rain day (at least 1 mm). The climate
normals now keep that spread beside their means, computed from the same 70 days per date (ten years
of ERA5, ±3 days); normals cached before it are served as they are and fetched again in the
background ([the spread](architecture/weather.md#the-spread-typically-1618-c),
[typical weather](frontend/trip-regions.md#typical-weather)). The day planner's context names the
date's normal in `climateNormal` where no forecast reaches, every park day of the region calendar
carries its `weather` (the forecast, past it the normal with its range), so the hub and the planner
can draw the weather beside the days, and Parky reads it as the historical
average, never as the day's forecast: `get_days`, `get_park_day` and `compare_parks` mark it with a
note and the typical range, and `plan_vacation`'s days and `find_trip_dates`' windows say their
`typicalWeather` ([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).
