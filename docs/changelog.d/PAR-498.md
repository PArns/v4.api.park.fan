### Fixed — the attraction sitemap no longer advertises the slug the park payload dropped

The park payload keeps one row per attraction name; the sitemap listed every row,
so the loser of a name group was published as a URL that resolves to nothing.
Measured on 2026-09-26 over all 201 parks in the sitemap: **48 of 7,304 entries**
had no row in the payload, in 15 parks, and they were exactly the 48 losers of the
45 duplicate name groups — no gap existed in the other direction.

Which row won was worse than the gap: the rule preferred the OPERATING row, then
the row with coordinates, so the surviving public URL moved with the live feed.
Two of the groups the ticket lists as 404 on 2026-09-24 were served two days later
with their `-2` siblings missing instead, without a row having changed.

The choice now reads `slug` and `name` and nothing else — no counter suffix beats
a counter suffix, then the slug `generateSlug(name)` would produce, then the slug
itself — and `deduplicateEntities` and the sitemap both call it, so there is one
rule and not two. Nine of the 45 groups changed winner, seven of them from a `-N`
slug onto the base slug that had been 404ing. `CacheKeys.sitemapAttractions()`
goes to `v3` so the new list is served on deploy rather than up to 24 h later.
