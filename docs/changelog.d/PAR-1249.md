### Fixed — Show patterns skip a day the feed published with the wrong time offset

From 7 to 27 September ThemeParks.wiki served Universal Studios Japan's show
programme nine hours early, so the nightly pattern rebuild learned shows at 02:00
and 03:40. The rebuild now leaves out a park day on which at least five showtimes,
and more than half of them, fall before 06:00 park time. Against 56 days of
production that rule matches exactly the 15 shifted USJ days and no real late-night
programme. See [shows in the day plan](frontend/plan-day/shows.md).
