### Fixed — the schedule sync rebuilds a best-days snapshot only for a day the snapshot holds

The schedule sync rebuilt a park's best-days snapshot whenever it wrote a schedule day version.
The gap fill writes one every day for the placeholder it adds half a year out, so nearly every park
went through a cold 90-day calendar build, one after the other, at 03:00 and 15:00 UTC. The sync
now rebuilds only a park with a changed day from its today to 90 days on, in the park's timezone,
which are the days the snapshot holds, and waits the calendar warmup's 2.5 seconds between two
rebuilds. `recordScheduleVersions` answers the changed days instead of their count
([the forecast ring](architecture/caching-strategy.md#the-forecast-ring)).
