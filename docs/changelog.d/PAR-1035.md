### Fixed — every cell of a model-comparison question is judged on the same rows

The admin matrix paired each model with what is served on that model's own
coverage: persistence at every origin, the H5 and composer shadows only from the
06:00 origin, Chronos-2 only from the 10–18 h origins. `live_h1_2` × H5 was
therefore the rope-drop hour of the parks that open before 08:00 ("−9.1 minutes
on 7 parks"), and two cells of one question carried different reference MAEs
(6.31 vs 6.50). A slot or ride-day is now paired only where every candidate of
the question has a value, so the cells of a question share one reference MAE
lead by lead (a spec builds the matrix from the scorer and asserts it). The
live questions are measured from the 10–18 h origins, `today_h2_6` therefore on
2–4 hours and `today_h6_12` without persistence. Chronos-2 vs H5 and H5 + TFT
vs H5 are reported directly under `comparisons`. Paired definition 3: the
scoring run replays the last 14 dates under it.
