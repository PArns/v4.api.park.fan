### Fixed — the season level types a US park's working days, and a park-day reads the same on more surfaces

An influencing region whose school data ended before the park's today no longer bounds the
neighbours' edge of the [season level](ml/season-level.md#neighbour-regions). Mexico's rows end on
2026-07-15, and Mexico influences the parks in California and Texas and every US park in a state
without an entry of its own, so every working day and eve of their seasons was unknown and served
the model's number. Ireland did the same to English parks, Italy to Swiss ones, Spain to Portuguese
and Hungary to Romanian ones. Such a region is now left out like one without school data; its
stored breaks still make neighbour days. A park's own region is not left out: in Spain, Italy,
Hungary, Mexico and Ireland, whose own data ends before their seasons, and in Sweden and Singapore,
which have none, weekdays stay on the model.

A failed load of the season level's inputs, usually the 20-second statement timeout, was run again
by every serving read of the park and logged on each. It is now remembered for five minutes
(`ml:season-level:v4:{parkId}:failed`) and logged once with the park's name. A calendar month the
warmup built during such a failure keeps no level until the next warmup
([reads and cache](ml/season-level.md#reads-and-cache)).

The calendar warmup now evicts the yearly outlook's served rows (`ml:park:…:yearly:{today}`, 13
hours) and its route answer with the daily ones, so the yearly view takes a new season level with
the calendar instead of up to 13 hours later.

The schedule sync rebuilds the best-days snapshot of each park whose schedule it changed and rings
`best-days:<slug>` with the calendar tag. Before, it evicted the months and rang the calendar only,
and „Prognose heute" kept forecasting a day the grid showed closed until the evening warmup
([the forecast ring](architecture/caching-strategy.md#the-forecast-ring)).

The display-consistency check also reads the today panel's single day,
`/calendar?from=<today>&to=<today>`, an edge copy of its own, and compares it with the API's
calendar: up to four relay requests a park. Its page now names the two settings outside this repo
without which every run reports failures: a Cloudflare skip rule for
`User-Agent: api.park.fan-display-consistency` on the relay paths, and `FRONTEND_URL` equal to the
relays' origin ([display consistency](analytics/display-consistency.md#the-relays)).

**After a deploy that changes a served forecast, run the calendar warmup before the frontend
deploy**, and again after a hand-started TFT or CatBoost training: until the warmup the calendar
and best-days keep the old level while `/plan/day` and Parky read the new one. It has no admin
route; add a `warmup-calendar-daily` job to `park-metadata` in Bull Board
([Coolify](deployment/coolify.md#a-deploy-that-changes-a-served-forecast)).
