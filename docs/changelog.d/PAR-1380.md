### Fixed — the forecast warmup rings the calendar with best-days, and Parky's tip follows the day's level

After each calendar warmup the API told park.fan only `best-days:<slug>`. The calendar month the
frontend relays had no tag at all and stood a day at the edge plus a day stale, so after the
morning forecast „Prognose heute" moved while the day dialog under it kept the night before's
level. A visitor reported exactly that through Parky on 2026-11-15.

The warmup now posts both of a rebuilt park's forecast tags in one call (`forecastCacheTags`):
`best-days:<slug>` and the new `park-calendar:<continent>/<country>/<city>/<slug>`
(`parkCalendarCacheTag`, a twin of the frontend's), with `"expire": 0`, and the same tags again
after `CDN_SETTLE_MS` through the `revalidate-tags` job. For about 200 parks that is some 400 tags
in eight POSTs, twice a day. `sync-schedules-only` sends `park-calendar:` beside each park's own
tag (`scheduleCacheTags`). `RevalidationService.revalidateBestDays` is gone; the log of a delayed
ring counts its tags instead of listing 400 of them.
[The forecast ring](architecture/caching-strategy.md#the-forecast-ring).

Parky's day tip reads the day's level before its cache, and the level is part of the Redis key,
`parky-tip:v2:{parkId}:{today}:{date}:{crowdLevel}:{locale}`: once the forecast moves a day, the
sentence is written again for the new level instead of being kept for twelve hours under it. The
read is the window the prompt sees (the date ± 3 days) from the `calendar:month:v2` month caches,
one `MGET` of one or two months for a tip already written. The answer carries the level as
`crowdLevel` ([Parky's tip](frontend/assistant.md#parkys-tip-for-a-calendar-day)). A closed or
unrated day answers 204 from that read without a cache entry.
