### Fixed — The wait band no longer doubles for rides whose band already covered

The hourly forecast's upper band (`uncertaintyMinutes`) is widened per lead
and wait segment so it covers 95 % of actual waits. A segment with too few
scored slots borrowed its lead bucket's pooled factor even when its own slots
showed the raw band already covered: at more than a day ahead, the mid segment
(30–59 min) had 424 slots at 100 % raw coverage yet took the pool's 2.35,
served as 2. The pool is three-quarters quiet rides, which do need the wider
band. Taron's late-November forecast of 30–35 min therefore read "up to +50".
A borrowing segment with at least 50 slots of its own now takes the smaller of
the pool's factor and its own fit, so those days read about +25. See
[quantile serving](ml/quantile-serving-and-calibration.md).
