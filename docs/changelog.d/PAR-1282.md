### Changed — every ride list Parky reads carries the ride's id

Two rides can share a name (a "Wilde Maus" in the Prater and one in Tripsdrill), but the lists of
rides a result leaves out named them only: `notAllowedRides`, `heightUnknownRides`,
`withoutValueRides`, `typeUnknownRides` and `shelterUnknownRides` (`compare_rides` and the rides
card), `get_park_day`'s `plannedTooShort` and `plan_add_rides`' `notAdded`. Each row now carries
`id` beside `name`, and the system prompt (version 45) has Parky take a ride's id once a result in
the chat gave one, a name only before that. A ride card asked for by a name two rides share draws
nothing and hands back both with their ids to pick from.

The post search needs every word to match, and with the whole question as its query ("Lohnt sich
der Quick Pass im Phantasialand") it found one of the three fitting posts. `search_posts` and the
posts card now ask for two or three key words, never the whole question
([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).
