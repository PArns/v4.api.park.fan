### Fixed — the day planner has rides again past tomorrow at Europa-Park and four other parks

`ParkHistoricalStatsService.queryHourlyProfile` failed at plan time with
`XX000 no tlist entry for key 6` for Europa-Park, Energylandia, Hersheypark,
Kennywood and Plopsaland Belgium — 5 of 211 parks, measured on production on
2026-10-09 with the planner's 60-ride fetch (the 24-ride fetch of
`/stats/hourly` failed for none). `PlanDayService` caught it, so
`/plan/day` answered 200 with `rides: []` and `data_unavailable` for every day
it composes from the hourly shape — everything past the model's own hourly
window — and, because a failure is never cached, re-ran the 3–6 s year-long read
on every request. The trigger is the PAR-698 closed-day anti join in the same
grouped SELECT as `COUNT(DISTINCT local day)` over the hypertable:
TimescaleDB's ChunkAppend then cannot place the sort the aggregate asks of its
chunks. With `timescaledb.enable_chunk_append = off` the same statement runs.
The rows are now read behind an `OFFSET 0` fence and grouped outside it; the
output is byte-identical to the old statement on all 211 parks at both fetch
sizes. The planner now logs a failed profile at ERROR and into
`infrastructure-errors`, where it was a WARN only `docker logs` kept.
