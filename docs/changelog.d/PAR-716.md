### Security — clear the 20 advisories a fresh audit reported

`pnpm audit` reported 20 advisories on 2026-10-05: 6 high, 10 moderate, 4 low. Sixteen sat under `@testcontainers/postgresql` and `@testcontainers/redis` — undici and `@grpc/grpc-js`, both dev-only and reached only by the e2e suite — and cleared when those two bumped from 12.1.0 to 12.2.0. The last four were each a transitive dependency one patch release short of its fix, so three overrides in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` moved to the patched floor: `multer` to `>=2.4.0` (the denial of service through an orphaned request on `@nestjs/bull`'s copy), the production 5.x `js-yaml` under `@nestjs/swagger` to `>=5.4.1` (GHSA-2883-xcg3-v3hh, the same unbounded-merge CPU fix already pinned for the 3.x and 4.x instances), and `fast-uri` to `>=4.1.5` (the inconsistent-host-case and mailto-injection reports, dev-only via `@nestjs/cli`). A re-run reports no known vulnerabilities.

The same pass carried eight direct dependencies to their latest minor or patch within the current major: `pg` 8.23.0 to 8.23.1, `supertest` 7.3.0 to 7.3.1, `@types/node` 26.6.3 to 26.6.4, `@typescript-eslint/eslint-plugin` and `@typescript-eslint/parser` 8.70.1 to 8.71.0, and `eslint` 10.11.0 to 10.12.0. No major was moved. Lint, the type check, the 2,931-test unit suite and `nest build` with the Swagger spec are unchanged and green.
