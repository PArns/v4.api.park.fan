### Fixed — a reopen alert reads a closed park as closed and refuses free-flow areas

`RideAlertsService` read the raw STANDBY row for `kind: "reopen"`, so a source that kept reporting `OPERATING` after closing time fired "wieder offen" for a ride that `effectiveStatus` shows as closed. The sweep now asks the park status once per cycle, only when a reopen alert exists, and a `CLOSED` park reads every watched ride as not operating. That also re-arms a spent alert overnight. A failed status lookup counts as unknown and leaves the old behaviour.

Free-flow areas (`openWithPark`) are served as `OPERATING` whenever their park is, while their feed row stays `CLOSED` all day, so there is no closed-to-open flank to read. `POST /v1/push/ride-alerts` now answers 400 for `kind: "reopen"` on one; wait-time alerts are unchanged.
