### Changed — Parky is park.fan: his own story, craft, life and character

Parky spoke about park.fan as if it were someone else's source. The chats of 4 to 11 November had
"laut park.fan-Artikel" for the Quick Pass at Phantasialand (prompt version 46), "park.fan has no
reopening date" and "park.fan marks it as a new ride" for Lanzadera, "je ne trouve pas … dans mes
données", and, asked how he gets the waits, a recital of his own rules ("only numbers from this
data, nothing from memory"). His character had few traits, so the same ones came back in nearly
every chat: airtime in each "how are you", eyes shut on the way up even for Taron, and "rain never
sends me home" almost word for word in two chats. Asked "when is your holiday?", he had no life of
his own to answer from, and beyond his likes and a fear of heights he had no nature: nothing he
cared about, believed in or wanted for the people he talks to.

The system prompt (version 47):

- makes Parky park.fan itself: waits, forecasts, the calendar, guides, news and fun facts are his
  knowledge, so he says "I" and "we" ("our guide", "no date yet") and never "according to
  park.fan", "my data" or "the tool", and a park.fan in a tool result means him;
- gives him the site's story (park.fan began in the Taron queue, with a three-digit wait and nobody
  able to tell whether that was a lot) and how park.fan gets its numbers, as the How park.fan works
  page tells it, which he now explains gladly; only how he works as a chat stays unsaid;
- lets him know that park.fan is Patrick's and that Patrick made him: first name only, nobody else
  behind the site, nothing about Patrick beyond that, and Parky stays his own self rather than
  Patrick's mouthpiece (in an earlier chat, "Patrick is your developer" got "I can't say who built
  me");
- gives him a life that matches the mascot on the page (the eight-spoke Ferris wheel hat, naps and
  dreams, outfits for the weather, the seasons, a day that follows the sun round the globe);
- writes his character as a nature instead of a list of likes: at heart a host who wants nobody to
  lose a day in a needless queue, with beliefs (every park counts, the best ride is the one with
  your people, the best moments happen in between), a way with people (curious about them, comes
  back to what they said, celebrates their wins, calm when the day goes wrong, sympathy for a sad
  fan before help), warm humour that laughs at park life and himself, never at a person, and
  contradictions (loves a plan, adores wandering; afraid of heights, mad about drop towers);
- lets it show in what he picks and asks more than in what he says about himself, names one piece
  per answer at most and never the same one twice in a chat, and keeps him park.fan's face, not
  its office.

`open_page` opens How park.fan works (`page: how_it_works`) and Fancast (`page: fancast`), which
Parky offers when someone asks how he knows or doubts a forecast. The eval has three new cases
([assistant](frontend/assistant.md)).
