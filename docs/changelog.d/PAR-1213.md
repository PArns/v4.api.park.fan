### Changed — the reindex queue lists first where a request gains most

`GET /v1/admin/search-console/reindex` sorted `open` by impressions, so a park
page Google crawls every few days stood on top. `reindexPriority`
(`search-console-reindex.ts`) now orders it by the impressions over 28 days
times the days Google had not crawled the page when inspected, capped at the
queue's 30 days and halved for `uncertain`; the cut to 100 comes after it.
Heide Park (14,648 impressions, crawled two days before the inspection) scores
29,296 and goes below the glossary entry "vertical loop" (1,593 impressions,
last crawled two months before) at 47,790. The order is in [the search-console
contract](frontend/search-console.md#reindex-queue).
