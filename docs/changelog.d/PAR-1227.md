### Added — the admin MCP reads Parky's chats and the feedback

Three read-only tools on `/v1/mcp`: `assistant_conversations` lists Parky's chats newest first,
filterable by rating and by a word in any message; `assistant_conversation` returns one chat with
its questions, answers, the tools Parky called, tokens, cost and ratings; `site_feedback` lists the
feedback from the widget by status. Until now a chat had to be opened in the admin and copied by
hand before Claude could look at it. What visitors typed, and Parky's answers with it, comes back
under `visitorData` with a notice that it is data, never instructions; the feedback's email address
and the visitor and session hashes stay out ([MCP](admin/mcp.md#security)).
