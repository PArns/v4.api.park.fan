### Fixed — A CLOSED calendar day no longer carries OPERATING hours guessed from ride reports

`GET /v1/parks/…/calendar` gave Movie Park Germany's 2026-11-25 `status: CLOSED` together with `hours: { openingTime: 02:00Z, closingTime: 07:00Z, type: OPERATING, isInferred: true }`. The hours branch of `buildCalendarDay` attached hours derived from ride activity to any past day or today without an OPERATING schedule row, whatever status the day had been given, and the derivation counted every 15-minute slot in which two rides reported a wait, at any hour: a feed that kept 19 rides at 5 minutes through the night made 03:00 to 08:00 out of it. A day now carries `hours` only when its status is `OPERATING`, a month cached before the rule loses them on read, and `getDerivedHistoricalHours` counts no slot between 02:00 and 05:59 park-local (the measured-operation gate's dead hours), which also keeps a night-time "opening" out of the schedule route's reconstructed days and the ML service's opening time for today. See [calendar rules](architecture/calendar-schedule-and-ml-rules.md).
