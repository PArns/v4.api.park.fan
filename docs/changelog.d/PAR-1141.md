### Fixed — ml-bench: the image has a C compiler for Triton, and Seldon's licence is read

The first GPU probe of `kumo_level` (PAR-1405) failed with "Failed to find C compiler": Triton
builds its CUDA launcher at the first kernel call, and the slim image had no compiler. The CPU smoke
never got that far. The bench `Dockerfile` now installs `gcc` and `libc6-dev`.

The [tabular foundation models](ml/foundation-models/tabular.md) page records Seldon's licence,
read on 2026-11-22. It is a research and evaluation licence that forbids production use and serving
through a website or API, free of charge or not, so Seldon and Seldon-Mini stay out of the
benchmark. TabArena had not changed since its version of 2026-11-20 apart from TabPack
(Yandex, Elo 1542, below tuned CatBoost).
