### Fixed — The calendar no longer counts a ride in maintenance in a day's forecast

The model forecasts every ride for every day and knows nothing of curated maintenance or works
windows. So a ride shut for maintenance kept its ordinary predicted wait. It counted towards the
day's average and crowd level and could head the day's `headlinerForecast` list, while the same day
listed it as closed. On park.fan, a January day's dialog showed Droomvlucht with „~30 Min“ at the
top and as „wegen Wartung geschlossen“ below.

- `withoutRidesInMaintenance` (`curated-out-of-service.util.ts`) drops a ride's predictions on the
  days its window covers. `AttractionsService.getMaintenanceWindows` reads the park's windows in one
  query.
- The calendar applies it before it aggregates anything: the crowd level, `headlinerForecast` (the
  average and the list) and the hourly curve. `/plan/day` and the MCP's `park_forecast` read the
  calendar and carry the same day.
- The yearly predictions apply it as well, so both views rate a day alike.
- If the windows cannot be read, the day is served as before; only the filter is missing.
- `rideWorksPeriods` turns a park's rows into the windows list, for the calendar and for
  `PlanDayService.maintenanceWindows`. The latter now also reads `curatedOutOfServiceToUncertain`,
  so a single legacy window keeps its estimated end.
