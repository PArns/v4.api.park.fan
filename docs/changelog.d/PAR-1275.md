### Fixed — Parky's days, cards and plans after walking 32 visitor scenarios

Visitor questions walked through the prompt and the tools showed where Parky would answer wrong.

- **Days without published hours.** `get_best_days` refuses a range past its 90-day snapshot and
  points at `compare_dates` (May 2027 came back empty and read as "closed"), and counts days whose
  hours are not published yet apart instead of ranking them. `get_park_day` says `hoursPublished`
  and `statusWhy`; the tiles' result carries the day's `status`.
- **The rides card.** It reads every waiting ride, so "shortest first" at a big park is no longer
  the shortest of the 30 longest; takes `riderHeightCm` and gives each row its limits and id; and
  a closed park's empty card says why, with the next opening.
- **Cards hand their rows to the model:** the calendar's days, the shows with their times, the
  restaurants with the ids `set_favorite` takes. `find_restaurants` keeps the id too.
- **Planning for a child on a new day.** `get_park_day` and `plan_add_rides` take the party's
  `riderHeightCm` when the plan does not hold it yet.
- **Size.** `compare_dates` gives the reason for unpublished days once, and `get_park_day` puts the
  long ride list last, so a cut at the 12,000-character limit takes rides, not the day.
- **Today.** `get_park_live` and the park card carry `todayExpected`, the calendar's rating of the
  whole day, beside the live level, so "how full will it get today?" has an answer before opening.
- **References.** `find_rides` takes the park by name, slug or id; "Disneyland Paris" is the
  Disneyland Park in Paris (a park is also known by its name and city); a closed favourite park
  has no live level.
- **The prompt (version 38):** "Saturday or Sunday?" and a date's events go to `compare_dates`,
  the parks card only for today, a paid queue's price is the one price Parky may state, and a
  child's height for planning is fine to ask.
