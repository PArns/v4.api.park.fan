### Added — The day optimizer plans the rest of today from the ride the visitor stands at

`POST …/plan/optimize` takes `from: { attractionSlug }` for the app's "Umplanen ab hier". Until now the optimizer knew no place on the day itself: the first stop after now cost no walk and could start at the next quarter hour wherever the visitor stood. With `from`, on the park's today, the visitor leaves that ride at `nowMinute`, the first stop starts no earlier than now plus the walk to it, that walk is priced like every other, and a day whose first entry cannot be reached in time is re-ordered instead of answered `unchanged`. Ticked-off entries, free blocks, shows and entries the clock has passed stay where they are.

The answer then carries a `receipt`: the start, the walk to the first ride, each moved entry with its old and new minute, and the entries whose queue is no longer joined before closing (kept and flagged, never dropped). The slug is checked against the park's catalogue; one that is not a ride of this park is a 400. It takes a slug and never a position, and nothing of it reaches a log line, a cache key or a stored plan. Without `from` the answer is the same string as before, which the untouched browser-parity suite and `start.spec.ts` hold. No schema change. See [the optimizer](frontend/plan-day/optimize.md#from-where-the-visitor-stands).
