### Added — TiRex-2 and Toto 2.0 as ml-bench plug-ins, measured against Chronos-2

Two more pretrained time-series foundation models in the offline benchmark
(`tirex2`, `tirex2_grid`, `toto2`, `toto2_nocov`, `toto2_grid`), on the same
inputs as `chronos2` wherever the model takes them and scored **inside the same
run** as `chronos2` / `chronos2_nocov`, because the harness pairs models within
one run and a cross-run comparison would carry no paired confidence interval.
Both are **Apache-2.0 in code and weights**, which makes them the first
foundation candidates whose licence does not stand in the way of a production
decision — TimesFM 3.0's weights are non-commercial, and the original TiRex is
under the NXAI Community License. TiRex-2 has native past and known-future
covariates; Toto 2.0 has a `known_dynamic` hook but exogenous input is not a
trained feature of 2.0, so `toto2_nocov` is the honest variant and the one
paired against `chronos2_nocov`. Neither model has a q95 head beyond its 0.1–0.9
quantiles, so D8 reports that field as missing rather than inventing it.

Two construction traps are recorded in `docs/ml/foundation-models.md`: importing
`tirex2` pulls in `xlstm`, which resolves a CUDA kernel's paths at **import
time** and raises `OSError: CUDA_HOME environment variable is not set` on a GPU
box without a CUDA toolkit — aborting the whole run at the first origin with
nothing written, rather than degrading — and `tirex2` wraps blocks in
`@torch.compile`, which needs a runtime C compiler the slim image does not ship.
Both are handled in the backend's constructor. On the RTX 5080 all seven models
score one origin over 30 parks in 431 s at a 3.3 GB VRAM peak.

The measured result: neither model beats Chronos-2 except at a 15-minute horizon, where both
are slightly better. Both clearly beat the served hourly profile in the live bands and lose to
it in the planner from one day out. `tirex2_grid` ties Chronos-2 at every planner lead on all
rides (and loses on busy ones), and the level-on-H5-shape composite `tirex2_x_h5` reaches 21
days on busy rides and wins the 10-day hand-over cell — the one lead TiRex-2 takes off
Chronos-2. `toto2_grid` is unusable (+4.3 to +11.6 minutes, q80 coverage 0.54), traced to Toto
2.0's handling of a zero-padded regular grid rather than to the harness. Both models are
therefore DROP for serving; TiRex-2 is kept as the Apache-2.0 fallback plug-in. See
`docs/ml/foundation-models.md` (PAR-853 section) for the numbers.
