### Added — `/stats` names the park's five busiest and five quietest measured days

`GET /v1/parks/…/stats` now returns `busiestDays` and `quietestDays` beside
`byDayOfWeek`. Each entry is a park-local `date` with the same `avgCrowdScore`,
`avgCrowdLevel`, `avgWaitP50` and `avgWaitP90` fields a weekday carries, read from
the headliner day values `byDayOfWeek` already averages, against the same
typical-day-peak. A day the schedule calls CLOSED is not a measured day and cannot
appear, nor can a day measured for fewer than 6 hours. A park with fewer than 10 such days gets two empty lists, so the two
lists never share a day. The lists cover the measurement window (about 8.6 months),
not all time. `schemaVersion` goes from 3 to 4 and the stats cache key from v4 to
v5; no schema change.
