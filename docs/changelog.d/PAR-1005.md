### Documented — a schedule day carries one day-status row, not one row

Two architecture pages still said the cleanup leaves a single row per date, and
both sentences predate PAR-276. `docs/architecture/schedule-sync-and-calendar.md`
read "Only the API's OPERATING/CLOSED then remains for that date" and
`docs/architecture/calendar-schedule-and-ml-rules.md` "This ensures one source of
truth per date". Since PAR-276 the cross-type phase arbitrates only OPERATING,
CLOSED and UNKNOWN; `TICKETED_EVENT`, `PRIVATE_EVENT`, `EXTRA_HOURS`,
`MAINTENANCE` and `INFO` stay beside the day-status row, and PAR-640 made the
readers pick the status row by type.

Measured on production 2026-10-26: **3107 park-level days at 73 parks hold more
than one row** — 1766 `EXTRA_HOURS` + `OPERATING`, 1008 `OPERATING` +
`TICKETED_EVENT`, 291 `INFO` + `OPERATING`, 42 with all four. Rulantica carries
`EXTRA_HOURS` "Open To Hotel Guests" from 09:00 next to `OPERATING` from 09:30 on
375 days, and the ThemeParks.wiki month endpoint publishes both rows itself. No
day holds two day-status rows: grouping all 125 663 rows of `schedule_entries` by
`(parkId, date, attractionId)` over the three status types returns nothing, at
park level and at ride level.
