### Changed — trip regions: one planner for several regions, Japan by its cities

The trip planner no longer assumes Orlando. What differs between regions is
the region's own config in `regions.ts`, and the routes read it
([trip regions](frontend/trip-regions.md#what-a-region-defines)): its hotel
kinds and perks (`hotel=` is checked against the region's, the region route
lists them as `hotels`), the name of its early slot (Tokyo's Happy Entry and
Paris' Extra Magic Time are Orlando's Early Entry under their own names), its
heat thresholds and storm season (Orlando's hurricanes keep `hurricane_season`
and `hurricane_peak`; Japan's typhoons get `typhoon_season` and
`typhoon_peak`, and `penalties` names the storm by its kind), a season named
but not priced (Japan's rainy season, `rainy_season`), its climate park, where
its travel times come from (OSRM's drive, or legs curated from timetables for a
region travelled by train; a curated leg in an OSRM region says
`source: "curated"`, and the transport modes gain `shinkansen`, `rail`,
`metro`, `highway_bus` and `shuttle`), the markets its tickets are sold in,
and a short curated code per park. `GET /v1/trip-regions` lists every region
with its park count and groups for the site's navigation.

A region whose parks lie in several cities has **bases**
([bases](frontend/trip-regions.md#bases-a-trip-across-several-cities)): the
plan searches the stays with the parks (which cities, in route order one way
or the other, how many nights each), prices a long day trip by the hours it
takes and a move day as a park after a morning move, a park before an evening
move or a travel day (`kind: "travel"`), and charges each move 20 minutes. The
visitor fixes what they know (`bases=tokyo:4@maihama,fujikawaguchiko:0`,
`startBase`, `endBase`, from the airports and stations each base lists as
`gateways`); the answer carries `meta.stays`, each day's `base` and `move`,
`startAt` after a morning move, and, where nights were fixed,
`meta.staySuggestion`. The `japan` region is the first: Tokyo Disneyland,
Tokyo DisneySea, Fuji-Q Highland, LEGOLAND Japan and Universal Studios Japan
from four bases, with its 20 tickets seeded, the evening passports among them
(`entryFrom`, `validDays`: a ticket that admits from a time on certain kinds
of day is planned as the evening of such a day and never suggested).

`/when`'s months carry `localSchoolBreakDays` (the region's own school breaks;
`usSchoolBreakDays` stays in the US regions), `hurricaneSeason`,
`typhoonSeason` or `rainySeason` only where the region has one, and the school
breaks offer Spain and Italy by name
([when to go](frontend/trip-regions-when.md#school-breaks-get-v1trip-regionsregionschool-breaks)).
Parky's trip tools take a `region` and the stays (prompt version 52).
Orlando's plan, `/when` and `/best-days` answers are pinned unchanged by a spec
over a rich synthetic year, and matched the code before the change on
production's own data.
