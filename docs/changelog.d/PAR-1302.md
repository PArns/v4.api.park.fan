### Changed — trip regions: one planner for several regions

The trip planner no longer assumes Orlando. What differs between regions is
the region's own config in `regions.ts`, and the routes read it
([trip regions](frontend/trip-regions.md#what-a-region-defines)): its hotel
kinds and perks (`hotel=` is checked against the region's, the region route
lists them as `hotels`), the name of its early slot (Tokyo's Happy Entry and
Paris' Extra Magic Time are Orlando's Early Entry under their own names), its
heat thresholds and storm season (Orlando's hurricanes keep `hurricane_season`
and `hurricane_peak`; Japan's typhoons get `typhoon_season` and
`typhoon_peak`, and `penalties` names the storm by its kind), a season named
but not priced (Japan's rainy season, `rainy_season`), its climate park, where
its travel times come from (OSRM's drive, or legs curated from timetables for a
region travelled by train; a curated leg in an OSRM region says
`source: "curated"`, and the transport modes gain `shinkansen`, `rail`,
`metro`, `highway_bus` and `shuttle`), and the markets its tickets are sold in.
`GET /v1/trip-regions` lists every region with its park count and groups for
the site's navigation. A ticket may admit from a time on certain kinds of day
(`entryFrom`, `validDays`: Tokyo's Early Evening and Weeknight Passports);
an owned one is planned as the evening of a day of its kinds and never
suggested. `/when`'s months carry `localSchoolBreakDays` (the region's own
school breaks; `usSchoolBreakDays` stays in the US regions),
`hurricaneSeason`, `typhoonSeason` or `rainySeason` only where the region has
one, and the school breaks offer Spain and Italy by name
([when to go](frontend/trip-regions-when.md#school-breaks-get-v1trip-regionsregionschool-breaks)).
Parky's trip tools take a `region` (prompt version 52). Orlando's plan,
`/when` and `/best-days` answers are pinned unchanged by a spec over a rich
synthetic year.
