### Added — a ride shut by the cold or the snow says so, and when the day's temperature lets it open

A ride shut during opening hours (CLOSED or DOWN) now carries `weatherClosure` when the weather
explains it with at least 90 % confidence: the cause (`cold` or `snow`), the temperature now, for
cold the temperature from which the ride usually runs and the forecast hour that reaches it, or
that today stays below. A weekly job on its own queue learns each ride's profile from a year of its
own open hours and the Open-Meteo archive: cold on dry hours, snow on snowy ones, against the
ride's mild-weather rate, with one park-local day counted as one observation, and without the days
after a season's last run, rehab streaks, park-wide closures or our own bookkeeping rows. On
Europa-Park and Phantasialand that keeps Taron, which closes for snow and never for dry cold, from
being called cold-sensitive, and leaves no ride over the bar after one winter (Poseidon's cold
profile stands at 0.85); the line appears as winter days accumulate. Parky's `get_ride` answers
"when does it open" from it, and the MCP `get_park` and `get_attraction` carry it. Contract:
[weather closure](frontend/weather-closure.md).
