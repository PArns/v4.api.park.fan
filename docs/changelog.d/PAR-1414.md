### Added — a ride shut by the cold or the snow says so, and when the day's temperature lets it open

A ride shut during opening hours now carries `weatherClosure` when the weather explains it with at
least 90 % confidence: the cause (`cold` or `snow`), the temperature now, for cold the
temperature from which the ride usually runs and the forecast hour that reaches it, or that today
stays below. A weekly job learns each ride's profile from a year of its own open hours and the
Open-Meteo archive, cold on dry hours and snow on snowy ones, with one day counted as one
observation. On Europa-Park and Phantasialand that keeps Poseidon (shut in 66 % of dry hours under
4 °C, 0.2 % in mild weather) and keeps Taron, which closes for snow and never for dry cold, from
being called cold-sensitive. Parky's `get_ride` answers "when does it open" from it, and the MCP
`get_park` and `get_attraction` carry it. Contract: [weather closure](frontend/weather-closure.md).
