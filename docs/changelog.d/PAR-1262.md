### Fixed — Parky no longer recommends or plans a ride the party's child is too short for

Asked to plan a day at Rulantica for a party with a 90 cm child, Parky added one slide and told
the family to ride Vinter Rytt (125 cm) and Svalgur Rytt (110 cm) early, saying the other slides
had no known minimum. `compare_rides` had only counted the rides it left out (`notAllowed`,
`heightUnknown`), so "too short" and "not known" read the same, and nothing held the rides of a
day or the plan against the height the visitor gave the planner.

- `compareRides` (`ride-facts.util.ts`) names what a height filter leaves out, the first 20 of
  each: `notAllowedRides` with their limits, `heightUnknownRides` by name. The counts stay.
- `get_park_day` carries each ride's `minimumHeightCm` where known. With the day's
  `riderHeightCm` in the planner snapshot it marks `tooShortForParty` and lists the planned rides
  among them as `plannedTooShort`.
- `plan_add_rides` leaves out a ride the party's shortest rider is under the minimum for, and
  returns it in `notAdded` with reason `too_short` and the minimum. `despiteHeight` adds it, for
  an adult riding alone once the user has heard the limit. Only the minimum counts, as in the
  planner's own flag (`partyTooShort`).
- The system prompt (version 28) has Parky name such rides with their minimum and say a minimum
  is not known rather than that a ride is fine.
