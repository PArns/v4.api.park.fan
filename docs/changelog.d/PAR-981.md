### Fixed — park statistics count only the hours a park is open

`/stats`, `/stats/hourly` and the park pages averaged every hourly aggregate of an open day, including the night. Some feeds report rides as `OPERATING` around the clock: Energylandia posts 0 minutes from 22:00 to 09:00, Toverland 5 to 10 minutes all night. Zadra's `avgWaitP50` was 10 against a daytime median of about 20, and Energylandia's and Toverland's `byDayOfWeek` read 10.8 and 10.0 (PAR-981). `isNotAClosedParkDay` only drops days the schedule calls CLOSED, so it never saw these hours.

`openingHoursCtes` and `isWithinOpeningHours` in `closed-park-days.sql.ts` keep an hour when it overlaps the window of a park-level `OPERATING`, `EXTRA_HOURS` or `TICKETED_EVENT` entry. A day without a timed `OPERATING` entry keeps every hour, so a park without a timetable reads as before. `queryTopAttractions`, `queryHeadlinerDayValues` (which feeds `byMonth`, `byDayOfWeek`, the busiest and quietest days) and both halves of `queryHourlyProfile` apply it, so the sample days and the hour counts move with the averages. The cache keys `park:historical-stats` (v5 to v6) and `park:hourly-profile` (v6 to v7) are bumped, so the new figures show on deploy.

Checked on a throwaway PostgreSQL 16 against a park with a 10:00 to 20:00 timetable and zeros overnight (median 20, hours 10 to 19) and a park without one (24 hours, unchanged), and in `park-historical-stats.service.spec.ts`. Not measured: the statements' run time on production data.
