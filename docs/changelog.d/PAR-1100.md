### Fixed — a ride no source has ever reported reads UNKNOWN in the park list, not open

The park payload's optimistic fallback serves an open park's ride without a
current row as `OPERATING`, so one ride going quiet at a working feed does not
read "park open, every ride closed". A ride with not a single observed row in
`queue_data` landed in the same branch: Disneyland's *Main Street Pumpkin
Festival* read as running, and 64 such rides sat in parks with a working feed on
2026-09-28. `QueueDataService.findNeverObservedAttractionIds` now asks, for the
rides that reach the fallback only, whether any source ever reported them
(heartbeats and reconciliation do not count); those read `UNKNOWN`. A quiet ride
with history and free-flow rides are unchanged, and a failed probe keeps the
fallback ([status regimes](architecture/attraction-status/status-regimes.md)).
