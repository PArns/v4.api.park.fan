### Added — the data-quality monitor names a blind park's OPERATING day without usable hours

Since PAR-129 the live closure statement takes its days from the shared window builder, which drops a
schedule row without an opening or closing time, or whose close stays at or before the opening after
repair. Such a day leaves the early-end filter's denominator without a word, and a ride can slip
under `MIN_DAYS_FOR_CYCLE_TEST`. None existed when measured (2026-09-15, 2391 blind-park days). The
nightly data-quality sweep now warns per blind park when one appears in the last `CYCLE_WINDOW_DAYS`
(`findBlindParkUnusableScheduleDays`). The `park_day_close` comment points at PAR-1094 for this and
for the open question it still holds: whether `early_end` should join the readings to the window
with an INNER JOIN. That waits for a before/after measurement. See
[the closure-gap signal](analytics/ride-downtime/closure-gap-signal.md).
