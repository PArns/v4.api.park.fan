### Added — Reindex queue: changed pages Google has not crawled since

`GET /v1/admin/search-console/reindex` lists the pages whose sitemap `<lastmod>` is within 30 days
and whose last crawl predates the change, with clicks, impressions, CTR and position over 28 days and
the reason (`unknown`, `stale`, `uncertain`). A daily `reindex-scan` job (10:00 UTC, also
`POST …/reindex/scan`) reads the sitemaps and spends up to 300 URL inspections on them.
`POST …/reindex/requests` records a "request indexing" sent in Search Console, once per change, so a
page waiting for Google's crawl is not requested twice; the answer counts the requests of the last
24 hours against Google's ten a day. Two new tables, `search_console_reindex_pages` and
`search_console_reindex_requests`. See [Search Console](frontend/search-console.md#reindex-queue).
