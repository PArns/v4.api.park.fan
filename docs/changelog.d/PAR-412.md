### Added — `/v1/favorites` hands out the baseline a ride's crowd level was rated against

A favourited ride now carries `baseline`, the P50 its live wait was divided by
to get `crowdLevel`, and `null` wherever the level did not come from that
division (park closed, source unreadable, no wait, park too thin to rate, no
P50 row). The frontend needs it to show a ride's crowd badge in that ride's own
minutes (PAR-378). „Hoch" is 20 minutes on Mission: SPACE and 55 on Remy's, and
without the number a favourite card could only show the word.

Only the batch path for cache misses was missing it. A favourite served from
the `attraction:integrated:{id}` cache already passed the detail payload's
`baseline` through. Checked on 2026-09-22 against twelve rated EPCOT rides:
`/v1/parks/…` carried a baseline for every one, `/v1/favorites` for none.

The Swagger text on `AttractionResponseDto.baseline` said „90th percentile
baseline wait time for this hour/day". It is the P50 over the ride's samples,
with P90 as the park payload's fallback for a ride that has no P50 row yet, and
the description now says so.
