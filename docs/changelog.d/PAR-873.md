### Changed — `pcn_forecasts`: no rewrite of unchanged forecasts, 4-day retention

- **The forecast upsert skips a value-identical re-send.** After every deploy the
  first PCN tick re-predicted each closed park's unchanged origin and rewrote it
  (measured 2026-10-12: 0.43 M rows, 4.8 % of the day's writes, 26 k–195 k rows per
  deploy), each a dead tuple plus three index entries. The rewrite also moved
  `created_at` to now, so the serving read's 3-hour staleness guard briefly took
  those parks' stale forecasts for fresh ones again. `ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE …
  WHERE predicted_wait IS DISTINCT FROM EXCLUDED.predicted_wait` leaves such rows
  alone; a retrained checkpoint that changes the values still rewrites them.
- **Retention 14 → 4 days** (`PCN_FORECAST_RETENTION_DAYS`). The scorer is the
  oldest reader at ~60 h; serving reads 3 h, the ML overview 2 days, `ml-bench`
  not at all. Expected ~8 GB instead of 28 GB at steady state, and the lead-curve
  scorer, which walks every row of a ride over the retention, reads ~3.5× less.
- **The prune is batched**: 200 k rows per transaction, at most 10 M per hourly
  run, so the ~87 M-row backlog after the deploy drains over ~9 runs instead of in
  one transaction.
- Disk only comes back after a rewrite of the table (`VACUUM FULL` with
  `SERVE_PCN_INTRADAY` off; `pg_repack` is not installed). Not done by this change —
  see docs/ml/pcn-intraday-review.md §10.
