### Fixed — the schedule sync no longer spends the whole ThemeParks.wiki budget in seven minutes

The daily bulk sync fired its 2730 requests (195 Wiki parks × 14) back to back at
366 per minute against a published budget of 300 per 60 seconds, and a 429 made
things worse rather than better: the client threw instantly while the cooldown
stood, so the loop asked for the next park at once and every request in the
window failed for free. On 2026-09-23 that gave 87 of 200 parks `Fetched total 0
schedule entries`, and La Ronde's opening hours stood untouched for 17 days.

Every request is now spaced 250 ms apart through an atomic Redis reservation —
at most 241 in any 60-second stretch, wherever the upstream's window boundary
falls — and a standing cooldown is waited out instead of thrown, so throttling
costs latency and not data. The client also reads the `RateLimit-*` headers the
upstream publishes and pauses on its own once 25 slots are left, which keeps it
off the penalty path entirely; the headers are ignored on a Cloudflare cache hit,
whose counters belong to whichever request filled the cache. `Retry-After` is
capped at 15 minutes.

And a throttled fetch is no longer reported as an empty one: `getScheduleExtended`
raises `ThemeParksRateLimitError` rather than returning a list the caller writes
as "no change", and the bulk run counts the two apart —
`Synced N entries across X park(s) · empty upstream: Y · throttled: Z`, with each
park's held future entries named in the warning.
