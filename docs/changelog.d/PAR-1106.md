### Added — the park payload says when its live data was read (`dataTimestamp`)

`GET /v1/parks/…/{park}` carries `dataTimestamp`, an ISO 8601 UTC instant set
when the payload is built, just before its live reads. The frontend derived its
"as of" line from `max(queues[].lastUpdated)` over every ride until its first
poll, which for a park whose rides are all closed was an old reading rather
than an old answer, and cost a pass over every ride
([data timestamp](frontend/data-timestamp.md)).
