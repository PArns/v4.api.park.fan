### Added — Parky knows which rides the visitor marked as ridden

park.fan lets a visitor tick rides off as ridden ("x of y ridden"), and Parky never saw it: "which
headliners haven't I done yet?" had no answer.

- The chat request takes `ridden`, the ids of the marked rides as the browser keeps them: UUIDs
  only, at most 300, a list that does not parse costing the marks and never the question.
- "This chat" says how many, and `show_card` kind `rides`, `compare_rides` and `get_park_day` mark
  each of them `ridden: true`.
- The system prompt (version 34) answers "what haven't I ridden yet" from the rows without the mark
  and never calls a ride ridden that is not marked.

The frontend sends the field from its own change (PAR-1270).
