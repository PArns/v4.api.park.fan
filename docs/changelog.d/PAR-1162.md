### Fixed — `pnpm test:e2e` no longer runs out of heap halfway through

`test:e2e` ran all 26 suites with `--runInBand` in one Jest process; each suite
boots `AppModule` and leaves heap behind, so the run died with `JavaScript heap
out of memory` at about 2 GB after 9 to 12 minutes and the remaining suites
reported nothing. The suites still run one at a time (`maxWorkers: 1`, one
shared database), but in a child worker that `workerIdleMemoryLimit: "1GB"`
replaces once it grows past the limit. `test:e2e` and `test:e2e:cov` drop
`--runInBand`, which would switch the limit off.
[Setup](development/setup.md) explains the run and how to remove containers
left behind with `TESTCONTAINERS_RYUK_DISABLED=true`.
