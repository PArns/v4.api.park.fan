## 2. Rides bound to a season

A ride that runs only during a season gets bound to it through the season's `attractionIds`.
1. Find the rides with `find_attractions` and the seasons with `list_trip_seasons`.
2. Set `attractionIds` with `update_trip_season`.

Bind at least these:
- **Phantasialand:** the ice rink and "Ice Skate Hire", to Wintertraum 2026/27.
- **Movie Park Germany:** its Halloween mazes, to every Halloween Horror Festival season in
  production (2027 included).
- **Other parks:** the Halloween mazes of every other park that has a Halloween season. Rides
  flagged as mazes are listed by `get_park` (maze badge, `docs/architecture/attraction-status/maze-rule.md`).
- **Winter rides:** the ice rinks and other winter-only rides of each park with a `winter` or
  `christmas` season.

Bind only rides that clearly belong to the event. List what you bound.
